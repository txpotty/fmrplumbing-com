    Frequently Asked Questions About Drain Services in Pahrump

    The cost of drain services in Pahrump, NV can vary widely, typically ranging from $150 for a simple clog removal to over $5,000 for a major sewer line replacement. Factors like the type of blockage, its location, and the method required (for example, snaking versus hydro jetting) all affect the final price. We provide transparent, upfront pricing after an on-site assessment.

    A typical drain cleaning for a single clogged fixture can take anywhere from 30 minutes to 2 hours, depending on the severity and location of the clog. More complex issues, like main sewer line clearing or hydro jetting, may require several hours. We provide an expected timeline after our initial assessment.

    Hair, grease, soap scum and hard water scale cause most fixture clogs. Main line clogs are often caused by wipes, tree roots or a belly in the pipe.

    For most homes, a professional drain cleaning every one to two years is enough for preventative maintenance. However, homes with older plumbing, frequent hair or grease buildup, or known tree root issues may benefit from annual cleaning. Businesses, especially restaurants, often require more frequent service.

    Avoid pouring grease down drains, use drain screens in sinks and showers to catch hair and food particles, and be mindful of what gets flushed down toilets. Regular preventative maintenance, like an annual professional drain cleaning, can also be very effective.

    While readily available, we generally advise against using chemical drain cleaners. They can be corrosive, potentially damaging your pipes, especially older ones, and are often ineffective against tough clogs like tree roots or heavy grease. They also pose a risk to your health and the environment. Professional methods are safer and more effective.

    A camera inspection is worth it if clogs keep coming back, you are buying an older home or roots are suspected. It shows the exact location and cause of the problem.
