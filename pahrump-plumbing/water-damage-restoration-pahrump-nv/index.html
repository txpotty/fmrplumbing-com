    Musty odors, bubbling paint, warped baseboards and soft spots in floors are common signs. Stains on ceilings and walls also point to hidden leaks. A rising water bill can indicate a leak you have not found yet.

    Water damage can stem from any plumbing brand.     Drywall soaked by clean water can sometimes be dried in place if addressed quickly. Drywall that has been wet for a long time or exposed to contaminated water usually must be replaced. We test moisture levels to decide.
