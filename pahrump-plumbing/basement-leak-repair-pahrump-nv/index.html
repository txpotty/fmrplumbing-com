    How We Fix Basement Leaks in Pahrump, NV

    Exterior Waterproofing Solutions in Pahrump, NV

    Exterior waterproofing means digging down to the footing, cleaning the wall, and applying a membrane with a drainage board over it. It is the most thorough fix and the most disruptive one. Expect a few days of work. Our crew handles the footing drain and its outlet, and a waterproofing partner applies the membrane.

    Hydrostatic Pressure Management in Pahrump, NV

    Water pressing against the outside of a basement wall finds the weakest point, usually the cove joint where wall meets floor. Snowmelt in the northern mountains can raise groundwater in spring, which can affect basements and low-lying properties. An interior drain channel along that joint relieves the pressure by giving the water a path to the sump. No pressure, no seepage.

    Wall and Slab Seepage Repair in Pahrump

    Seepage at the wall or the slab joint is usually surface water pooling against the foundation. Caliche, expansive clay and rocky desert soil are common in Nevada, and caliche can make digging for sewer lines slow and costly. Before anyone cuts the floor, we check the gutters, downspout extensions and the grade along that wall, because fixing those outside often stops seepage for a fraction of the cost. We always try the cheap fixes first.

    Crack Injection for Basements in Pahrump

    Injection works on poured concrete walls, not block. Ports go along the crack, the face gets sealed, and resin goes in from the bottom up until it fills the full depth. Epoxy bonds the two sides. Polyurethane is better for active leaks because it foams on contact with water.

    Interior Drainage Systems in Pahrump

    Where digging outside is not practical, an interior drain system does the same job from inside. A strip of slab comes out along the walls, perforated pipe goes in on stone, and it all drains to a sump. The wall gets a dimpled membrane. Seepage runs down behind it and into the pipe.

    New Sump Pump Installation in Pahrump

    Installing a new sump means breaking out a section of slab, setting a lined pit, and running the discharge out through the rim joist. We use a cast iron pump in most homes and a 1-1/2 inch discharge with a check valve. Discharge cannot go into the sewer in most places. It has to discharge outside, far enough from the foundation that it does not cycle back into the pit, often ten feet or more.

    Clogged Drain Tile Repair in Pahrump

    Footing drains clog with silt and roots over the years, and once they stop flowing the water table at the wall rises. We camera the tile from the sump inlet to see where it is blocked. Jetting clears it. Crushed clay tile has to be dug up and replaced with perforated PVC or corrugated pipe in a sock.

    Outside Wall Waterproofing in Pahrump, NV

    Excavating the outside of a foundation puts the repair where the water actually gets in. Intense summer heat and a dry climate are the main stresses on plumbing in Nevada, especially in the southern part of the state. We pick a membrane rated for that exposure, lay new perforated drain pipe at the footing in clean stone, and backfill so the grade slopes away from the house at least six inches over the first ten feet. We extend the downspouts while the yard is open.
