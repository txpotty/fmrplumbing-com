    Cast Iron Sewer Line Repair in Pahrump, NV

    Cast iron drains and sewer lines corrode from the inside, leaving rough scale, cracks and channel rot along the bottom of the pipe. We descale the pipe with chain knockers or cutters, then line it to restore a smooth, sealed interior. This is particularly useful for cast iron running under slabs where excavation is costly. Lining can add decades of service.

    Root Intrusion Repair in Pahrump, NV

    Tree and shrub roots go after moisture in sewer joints. Once inside, they grow into dense masses that cause recurring backups. We cut roots with a mechanical cutter or hydro jet, then line the pipe to seal the joints. That breaks the cycle of cleaning the line every few months.

    Causes of Sewer Line Damage in Pahrump, NV

    Sewer lines in Pahrump, NV can suffer damage from several factors, including shifting soils that cause pipes to crack or separate. Clay soils that swell when watered and shrink when dry can stress slabs and buried drain lines over time. Tree root intrusion is another major culprit, as roots seek out moisture in pipes and grow, causing blockages and structural damage. Age and corrosion, especially in older cast iron or clay pipes, also add to deterioration. Our trenchless solutions address these common issues effectively.

    Pipe Bursting Sewer Repair in Pahrump

    Pipe bursting is a trenchless method where a new pipe is pulled through the old one while the old pipe is fractured outward. This creates a new, seamless and often larger diameter sewer line without the need for long trenching. It is best for severely damaged or collapsed pipes in Pahrump that require full replacement rather than just relining.

    Emergency Trenchless Sewer Repair in Pahrump

    Sewer line emergencies, such as major backups or collapses, require urgent attention. FMR Plumbing offers emergency trenchless sewer repair services in Pahrump to quickly address critical issues.     Cured-In-Place Pipe Lining in Pahrump

    Cured-in-place lining creates a new pipe inside the old one. We clean the line, then pull or invert a resin-saturated liner into place and cure it with hot water, steam or UV light. The result is a seamless, jointless pipe that roots cannot penetrate. Lining works well for cracked or root-intruded lines that still hold their shape.
