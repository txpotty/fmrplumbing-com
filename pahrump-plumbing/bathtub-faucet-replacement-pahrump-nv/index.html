    Same-Day Tub Faucet Replacement in Pahrump, NV

    Some faucet failures cannot wait. Snapped handles and broken spouts get same-day visits when a plumber is free. Close the stops or main while you wait. Leave the tub drain open.

    Diverter Valve Repair and Replacement in Pahrump, NV

    Water running from the spout during a shower means a failing diverter. On a single-handle setup the diverter is usually the lift gate in the spout itself, and on a three-handle faucet it is the middle stem, and both wear out until water splits between the spout and the head. A new diverter spout takes minutes. Middle stems need a seat too.

    Hard Water Tub Faucet Replacement in Pahrump

    Water in much of Nevada is very hard, and scale buildup is one of the most common reasons water heaters and fixtures fail early. Scale packs into cartridges and spout diverters until handles stiffen and the diverter will not lift. Cleaning helps lightly scaled valves, but pitted brass or a fused cartridge means replacing the faucet. We pull a cartridge to check.

    Tub Faucet Repair vs Replacement Costs in Pahrump, NV

    A cartridge costs much less than a valve. Replacement inside the wall makes sense when parts are discontinued, the valve body is cracked or pitted, or you want anti-scald protection on an old three-handle faucet that has none. You see both prices. Most people choose repair first.

    Brand Tub Faucet Replacement in Pahrump

    Brand matters more on tub valves than anywhere else. Each maker uses its own cartridges and trim threads, so a same-brand trim kit usually bolts on without plumbing changes. Mixing brands rarely works. We find the logo or the casting mark first.

    Thermostatic Tub Valve Installation in Pahrump, NV

    Thermostatic valves hold the temperature you set. They sense real water temperature, not just pressure, which helps kids and older adults avoid hot spikes. Many have separate heat and flow controls. They cost more than pressure-balance.

    Most dripping tub faucets need internals, not a new valve. We replace Moen and Delta cartridges or old compression stems and seats, and we grease the O-rings with silicone plumber's grease so the handle turns smoothly for years. Seat wrenches come out often. About an hour, usually.
