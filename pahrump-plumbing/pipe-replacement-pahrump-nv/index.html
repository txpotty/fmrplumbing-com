    PEX Repiping in Pahrump

    PEX-A is flexible, resists scale buildup and expands slightly if water freezes, which helps in colder homes. We use expansion fittings that keep the full inside diameter of the pipe for better flow. Systems can be run as a home-run manifold with a shutoff for each fixture or as a trunk and branch layout. PEX installs faster than copper with far fewer fittings in the walls. It is our most popular repipe material.

    Polybutylene Pipe Replacement in Pahrump

    Gray polybutylene pipe was installed in many homes from the late 1970s into the mid-1990s. It breaks down from the inside when exposed to chlorinated water, and fittings can crack with no warning. Some insurers raise premiums or decline coverage on homes that still have it. We remove polybutylene supply lines and replace them with PEX-A or copper. A completed repipe can also help when selling the home.

    Copper Pinhole Leak Replacement in Pahrump, NV

    Pinhole leaks in copper usually start as a small green stain or a damp spot on drywall.
