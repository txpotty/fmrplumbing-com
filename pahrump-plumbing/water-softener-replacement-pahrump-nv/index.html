    Traditional Salt-Based Water Softener Replacement in Pahrump

    Salt-based water softeners are a common and proven solution for hard water in Pahrump. These systems use ion exchange to remove calcium and magnesium minerals, which are well known for causing scale buildup. We replace old, inefficient salt-based units with modern, high-efficiency models that use less salt and water. Our plumbers confirm proper brine tank installation and regeneration settings for strong performance.

    Brine Tank Replacement in Pahrump

    Cracked brine tanks, salt bridges and failing float assemblies cause softeners to stop regenerating properly. We replace brine tanks and safety floats to restore steady brine draw. We also clean out mushy salt buildup that clogs the tank. A new brine tank is often part of a full system replacement. We recommend the best salt type for your system.

    Metered Softener Replacement in Pahrump

    Older timer-based softeners regenerate on a set schedule whether you used water or not. Metered softeners track actual usage and regenerate only when the resin is nearly used up. This saves salt, water and wear on the system. We replace old timer valves with metered Fleck 5600SXT or Clack WS1 valves. Most homeowners notice lower salt use within the first month.

    To extend the life of your new water softener, use the correct type of salt, if applicable, and keep the brine tank filled. Regular maintenance, such as cleaning the brine tank and periodic inspections by a professional, can also help ensure its longevity and efficiency.

    With proper maintenance, most quality water softeners can last between 10 and 15 years, and sometimes even longer. Factors like water hardness, usage volume and the quality of the original installation can influence its lifespan. Regular servicing helps extend the life of your unit.

    A new, properly sized water softener can sometimes improve water pressure if your old unit was undersized or had heavy mineral buildup restricting flow.
