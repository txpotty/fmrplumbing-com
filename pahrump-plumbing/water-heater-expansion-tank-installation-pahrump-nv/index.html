    Residential Expansion Tank Installation in Pahrump

    For homeowners in Pahrump, a properly installed water heater expansion tank is important, especially with so many closed plumbing systems. This device absorbs the increased volume of water when it heats, keeping excessive pressure from building up in your pipes and water heater. Our team ensures the tank is correctly sized for your water heater's capacity and your home's water pressure, maximizing its protective benefits. We handle all connections and secure the tank according to industry standards, giving you confidence.

    New Expansion Tank Installation in Pahrump

    If your home has a pressure reducing valve and no expansion tank, the water heater is probably seeing a pressure spike every heating cycle. We install a new tank on the cold water supply near the heater using a tee and short nipple. The air side is precharged to match your static pressure, usually between 50 and 70 psi. We then run the heater and watch the gauge to confirm pressure stays stable.

    Expansion Tank Replacement in Pahrump, NV

    Expansion tanks wear out over time as the internal bladder or diaphragm fails and the tank fills with water. A waterlogged tank feels heavy, and tapping it produces a dull sound all the way up instead of a hollow top. Pressing the air valve and getting water instead of air is a sure sign it has failed. We replace failed tanks with a correctly sized new unit and check the precharge before installing.

    Expansion Tank Sizing for Large Water Heaters in Pahrump

    A standard 2 gallon tank works for many 40 and 50 gallon heaters at moderate pressure. Larger heaters, higher incoming pressure or high-capacity systems may need a 4.5 gallon tank or larger. We use manufacturer sizing charts that factor in heater volume, supply pressure and temperature setting. Correct sizing keeps the tank from over-flexing and failing early.

    Replacing Old Expansion Tanks in Pahrump

    Expansion tanks do not last forever, and an old, waterlogged or corroded tank offers no protection. If your existing tank in Pahrump is showing signs of failure, such as a dripping relief valve or fluctuating water pressure, it is time for a replacement. We carefully remove the old unit, dispose of it responsibly and install a new, correctly sized expansion tank. This proactive replacement prevents costly damage to your water heater and plumbing system and restores proper pressure control.
