    Residential Drain Cleaning in Pahrump

    Sewer Camera Inspection in Pahrump, NV

    When you have recurring drain problems, a sewer camera inspection is crucial. We insert a flexible, waterproof camera into your pipes to visually diagnose issues like cracks, breaks, root intrusion, or significant blockages.     French Drain Installation in Pahrump

    A French drain is a gravel-filled trench with perforated pipe that collects water and carries it away from problem areas. It works well along foundations, behind retaining walls and in low spots that hold water after storms. We wrap the gravel in filter fabric so fine soil does not clog the system. The drain is sloped to an outlet, dry well or permitted discharge point.

    Kitchen, Bath and Laundry Drain Clearing in Pahrump

    Slow sinks, gurgling tubs and overflowing laundry standpipes typically come from grease, hair, soap scum and mineral scale. Mineral deposits crust over aerators, showerheads and valves, which is why softeners and regular water heater flushing are popular. Hard water leaves calcium deposits that narrow pipes and catch debris faster than soft water does. We clear lines with the right size cable for each fixture and avoid harsh chemicals that damage pipes. If clogs keep coming back, we camera the line to find the reason.

    Channel and Trench Drains in Pahrump, NV

    Driveways and patios that slope toward the house can send storm runoff directly into a garage or under a door. A channel drain with a grate catches that sheet flow before it reaches the building. We use durable polymer or concrete channels from brands like NDS and set them flush with the surface. The channel ties into solid pipe that carries the water to a proper outlet.
