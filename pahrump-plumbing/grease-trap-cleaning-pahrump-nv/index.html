    Grease Line Jetting in Pahrump

    Grease that slips past a full trap coats the 4-inch line out to the main. It builds up in rings, thickest at the bottom of the pipe.     Large Outdoor Grease Interceptor Cleaning in Pahrump

    Gravity interceptors in the parking lot often hold 750 to 2,000 gallons across two or three compartments. Each compartment gets pumped separately.     Commercial Grease Trap Cleaning in Pahrump
