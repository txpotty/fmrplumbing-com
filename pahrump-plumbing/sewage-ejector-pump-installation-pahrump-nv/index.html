    Basement Bathroom Ejector Systems in Pahrump

    Basement bathrooms sit below the building sewer, so the toilet, shower and sink drain to a sealed basin instead of flowing by gravity. We cut the slab, trench the drain lines to the basin, set it, run a two-inch vent up through the house to the roof and pipe the discharge to the building drain with a check valve and shutoff. The slab gets repoured.

    Sewage Ejector Pump Basin & Pit Installation in Pahrump

    Digging the pit means cutting the slab and excavating below it, deep enough to set the basin flush with the finished floor. We bed the basin on gravel and backfill around it so it cannot float when groundwater rises, then pour new concrete to seal the floor and lock the basin in place. A floating basin breaks pipe.
