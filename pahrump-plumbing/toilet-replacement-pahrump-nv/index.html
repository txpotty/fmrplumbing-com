    Cracked Bowl or Tank Replacement in Pahrump

    Sometimes only the tank is cracked, and if the model is still in production, a matching tank costs less than a whole new toilet. First we find the model number inside the tank. If the bowl is the cracked part, the whole toilet gets replaced, since the bowl carries the seal to the floor and cannot be patched.

    Comfort Height Toilet Installation in Pahrump

    Chair height toilets put the seat about 17 to 19 inches off the floor, roughly two inches higher than a standard bowl, and that makes a real difference for knees and hips. The drain stays the same. We often pair it with an elongated bowl and a soft-close seat, and the swap takes the same couple of hours as any other.

    Replacing Pressure-Assisted Toilets in Pahrump, NV

    Pressure-assisted toilets use a sealed vessel in the tank that stores air under line pressure and pushes water through the bowl hard, which helps in houses with long, low-slope drain runs. They are noticeably louder than gravity toilets. Replacement means matching the vessel brand for parts later, and we check that house pressure is in the range the unit needs.

    Toilet Rough-In Adjustments in Pahrump, NV

    Rough-in is the distance from the finished wall to the center of the drain, usually 12 inches, sometimes 10 or 14 in older homes. Take the measurement from the wall to the closet bolts, not from the baseboard trim. If the new toilet's rough-in does not match, it either sits too far out or will not fit. Offset flanges can fix a small mismatch.

    Toilet Flange Repair During Replacement in Pahrump

    Pulling the old toilet is the only chance to see the flange, and a cracked plastic ring or a rusted cast iron flange will ruin a new install within a year. It gets checked on every replacement. For a cracked flange, a stainless repair ring is often enough, while a flange that sits below the finished floor gets a spacer or extender so the seal compresses properly.

    Toilet Replacement Cost Comparison in Pahrump, NV

    Three repair calls in a year on the same toilet usually cost more than replacing it, especially when the toilet is old enough to waste water on every flush. You get repair and replacement prices next to each other. The decision is yours, and we do not push replacement on a toilet that only needs a flapper.

    Choosing a Toilet Brand in Pahrump, NV

    Kohler, American Standard, TOTO, Gerber and Mansfield all make solid toilets, and each line has a few models we steer people away from because of weak flushes or hard-to-find parts. You get our honest take on which models to skip. Rough-in and bowl shape decide what fits, and we check both before you buy.

    Gravity-Flush Toilet Replacement in Pahrump

    Gravity-flush toilets are the simplest and most forgiving, and a well-made one with a 3 inch flush valve will outflush most of the older toilets it replaces. Replacement parts are on every hardware shelf. We set it level with shims if the floor is out, then caulk the base and test several full flushes.
