    Well Pressure Tank Replacement in Las Vegas

    A well pump that clicks on and off constantly usually points to a failed pressure tank. When the bladder inside the tank ruptures, the tank fills with water, loses its air cushion and forces the pump to start every time someone opens a tap, which burns out pump motors years early. Replacing the tank stops it. We also check the switch.

    Sump Pump and Well Pump Replacement in Las Vegas, NV

    A dead sump pump on a wet night means water on the floor, and a dead well pump means no water at all. In Las Vegas, NV, sump pumps get sized for inflow with a check valve and an optional battery backup, while well pumps get matched to depth, flow and switch settings. Both get tested before we go.

    Pipe Replacement & Whole-Home Repiping in Las Vegas, NV

    Homes in Las Vegas, NV with galvanized steel or polybutylene tend to show the same symptoms: low pressure, rusty water and leaks that keep coming back.
