    Flow Sensor and Thermistor Repair in Las Vegas

    Tankless heaters use flow sensors to start and thermistors to regulate temperature. A dirty inlet filter or failed sensor causes no hot water, fluctuating temperature or the unit turning off mid-shower. We clean filters, test sensor readings and replace faulty parts. Temperature becomes steady again once readings are accurate.

    No Hot Water Tankless Repair in Las Vegas

    When your tankless unit fails to produce hot water, it is often due to issues with the ignition, gas supply or a clogged heat exchanger. Our technicians in Las Vegas will thoroughly diagnose the root cause, whether it is a faulty igniter, a pressure sensor malfunction or mineral buildup. We carry common replacement parts for fast fixes, restoring your continuous hot water supply efficiently. We also check for proper venting, which is essential for the safe operation of your unit.

    Recirculation Pump Repair in Las Vegas

    Many tankless systems use internal or external recirculation to deliver hot water more quickly. A properly tuned system saves both water and gas.

    Condensing Tankless Repair in Las Vegas, NV

PVC venting is also checked for slope and sagging.
