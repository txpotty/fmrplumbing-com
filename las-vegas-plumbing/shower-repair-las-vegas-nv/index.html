    Fixing Showers in Las Vegas, NV

    Low Shower Pressure Repair in Las Vegas, NV

    Low pressure at the shower with good pressure at the sink narrows it down fast. With good pressure elsewhere, the problem is in the head, the restrictor or the cartridge, and that is typically a short repair. The head comes off first. If every fixture is weak, we check the regulator and the main.

    Shower Diverter Repair in Las Vegas, NV

    Diverters send water up to the head instead of out the tub spout. A worn spout gate or a tired diverter stem lets water keep running from the spout, and the head pressure drops off. Usually a new spout fixes it. It is a short job.

    Showerhead Repair and Replacement in Las Vegas

    Showerheads clog from the inside out. Minerals collect behind the face and inside the restrictor, which turns the spray patchy or weak with the valve fully open. A vinegar soak can work. When it does not, we install a new head with soft rubber nozzles that you can wipe clean with a thumb.

    Shower Drain Body Repair in Las Vegas

    Drain bodies can crack or work loose under the pan. When that happens, water goes under the shower instead of down the pipe, and the first sign is often a soft spot in the floor next to the shower or a stain in the ceiling below. We clear and test the drain. We reseal or replace the drain body as needed.

    Shower Pan and Wall Leak Repair in Las Vegas, NV

    Stains on the ceiling below are often the first clue. We use a moisture meter on the walls and a flood test on the pan to tell a leaking valve from a cracked pan, a failed grout line or a loose drain body. The source gets repaired. With slab floors, the water can travel under the flooring of nearby rooms.

    Mixing Valve Repair in Las Vegas

    Mixing valves blend hot and cold inside the wall. Over time the cartridge or spool sticks, and you get a shower that will not stay at one temperature or a handle that turns past where it should before the water warms up. We replace the cartridge. Valves with no parts left get a replacement plan and an honest look at the tile.

    Leaky Shower Valve Repair in Las Vegas

    One drip every few seconds adds up to hundreds of gallons a year. In a single-handle shower the cause is almost always the cartridge or its O-rings, and on an old two-handle valve it is the stem washer and the seat. The parts are on the truck. Moen, Delta, Kohler and Pfister cartridges cover most of the valves we open, so the repair is usually done that same visit.

    Hot Water Pressure Problems in Las Vegas, NV

    Weak hot water with strong cold usually rules out the showerhead. It comes down to the hot side, whether that is a sediment-choked heater outlet or a cartridge with scale packed in the hot port. We test both sides. If the sinks have good hot pressure and the shower does not, the cartridge is the first suspect.
