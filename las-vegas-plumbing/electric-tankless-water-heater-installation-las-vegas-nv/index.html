    Hard Water Protection for Tankless Units in Las Vegas

    Hard water can cause scale buildup in tankless heat exchangers and heating elements. Scale reduces efficiency and can trigger error codes. Water in much of Nevada is very hard, and scale buildup is one of the most common reasons water heaters and fixtures fail early. We recommend a water softener or scale inhibitor to protect your unit, and we install service valves for periodic descaling. Regular maintenance helps extend the life of your tankless heater.
