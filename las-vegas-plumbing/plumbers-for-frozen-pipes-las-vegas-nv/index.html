    Backflow preventers and irrigation valves sit above ground and take the coldest air directly. Brass bodies crack. We repair or replace the damaged assembly, have it tested if your water utility requires it, and show you how to drain it or fit an insulated cover before the next freeze.

    Hose Bib and Outdoor Faucet Repair in Las Vegas, NV

    Outdoor faucets freeze easily, especially with a hose left connected. A split often hides inside the wall. It shows up as a leak the first time you use the faucet, so we install frost-free replacements.

    Heat Cable for Exposed Pipes in Las Vegas

    Some pipes cannot be rerouted or insulated enough on their own, like a supply line under a manufactured home or a pipe through an unheated porch. Heat cable handles those.
