    Cleaning cost depends on trap size, how full it is and how often it is serviced. Small indoor traps generally cost less per visit than large outdoor interceptors that require a vacuum truck.     Outdoor Grease Interceptor Pumping in Las Vegas, NV

    Large in-ground interceptors require a vacuum truck to fully pump out grease, water and settled solids.
