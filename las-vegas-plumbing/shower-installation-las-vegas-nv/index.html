    How We Install Showers in Las Vegas, NV

    Walk-In Shower Installation in Las Vegas

    Walk-in showers depend on floor slope. With no curb, the floor needs a steady quarter-inch-per-foot pitch to the drain and waterproofing that extends past the opening. We set that slope. We add blocking for grab bars while the studs are bare.

    Tub and Shower Combo Installation in Las Vegas

    Setting a tub is mostly about level and support. Acrylic and steel tubs get mortar or foam underneath, while cast iron rests on a level ledger, which keeps the caulk line intact. Then the valve. We set the spout and head heights to your layout and test the overflow gasket with water.

    Curbless Shower Installation in Las Vegas

    Curbless showers need the floor lowered in the shower area. On a wood floor, we drop the joists or use a recessed pan system, and on a slab, we cut and lower the concrete so the finished floor runs flat into the shower. A linear drain often works best. We plan the slope with your contractor before demo.

    Digital Shower Systems in Las Vegas, NV

    Digital shower systems like U by Moen and Kohler DTV need a little more planning. These valves usually sit in a nearby closet or vanity and need low-voltage wiring to the controller and an outlet close by. The outlet is an electrician's job. Our part is the plumbing.

    Custom Shower Enclosure Plumbing in Las Vegas

    Body sprays and steam units add lines and planning. Each spray needs its own supply, and the valve, the diverter and the pipe sizing all have to work together so the last spray in the line still gets decent pressure. Older neighborhoods may have lower pressure. We test static pressure before designing the layout.

    Shower Upgrades Without a Full Remodel in Las Vegas

    Not every upgrade needs demolition. Replacing an old pan, converting a tub to a shower or swapping in a new valve and trim can make a big difference without tearing the bathroom down to the studs. We price the options side by side. Most of these finish in a few days.

    Tiled Walk-In Shower Installation in Las Vegas

    Frameless glass walk-ins are popular, and the plumbing behind them has to be dead on. We install the valve, the head, an optional handheld and the drain, then set the mortar bed or foam pan to slope toward the drain at a quarter inch per foot. Then we test. The valve gets a pressure test and the pan a 24-hour flood test before tile.

    Barrier-Free Walk-In Showers in Las Vegas, NV

    Barrier-free entries need careful drain placement. The membrane and slope get planned with the tile setter, so water reaches the drain instead of spilling out the open side. Water heaters in hot garages wear out sooner, and outdoor pipes and irrigation lines take a beating from sun and heat. Pan material follows from that.
