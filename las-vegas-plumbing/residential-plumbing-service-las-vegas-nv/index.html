    Faucet, Toilet and Shower Valve Repair in Las Vegas, NV

    Hard water chews through faucet cartridges and toilet fill valves.     Faucet and Fixture Repair in Las Vegas

    Most dripping faucets need a cartridge, not a new faucet. We carry cartridges for Moen, Delta, Kohler and Pfister, and when you do want new fixtures we install them, connect new braided supply lines, and check the angle stops under the sink while we are there. Old stops fail when touched.

    Slab Leak Location and Repair in Las Vegas

    Slab leaks hide under concrete until something gives them away. Most homes sit on concrete slabs, so leaks in buried supply lines often show up as warm spots on the floor or higher water bills. Our techs find them with ground microphones and thermal imaging, so the only concrete we break is the small patch right over the leak, not half the kitchen floor. Then you pick the fix. Options include a spot repair through the slab or a reroute through the attic.

    Drain Cleaning and Main Line Clogs in Las Vegas

    Slow drains in more than one fixture point to the main line. A single slow sink is usually a trap or branch clog, but when the tub gurgles as the washer drains, the blockage is further out and needs a machine cable or a jetter run from the cleanout. Older clay and cast iron lines often have roots.

    Water Heater Repair and Installation in Las Vegas

    A water heater works hardest in winter, when incoming water is coldest. We work on gas, electric, tank and tankless heaters from Rheem and Bradford White, and the usual culprits are a heavy layer of sediment or a thermocouple that has quit reading the pilot. A cold shower is often a twenty-minute part swap. New units get an expansion tank and pan when the code requires it.

    Gas Lines for New Appliances in Las Vegas, NV

    Switching from an electric range to gas means a new line and a shutoff. Pipe size is figured from the BTU load, run it in black iron or CSST, bond it where the code requires and pressure-test before the gas company or inspector signs off on the work. Then we light it and check the flame.

    Water Heater Repair and Replacement in Las Vegas, NV

    Thermocouples, gas valves, elements and relief valves are the common water heater repairs. Our techs work on Rheem, Bradford White, Navien and Rinnai heaters, and when a tank is leaking from the shell we replace it with proper venting, an expansion tank and a drain pan, usually the same day. Old tanks get hauled off.

    Drain Cleaning and Clog Clearing in Las Vegas, NV

    Grease is the big one in kitchen lines. Bathroom drains clog with hair and soap scum, and we clear both with hand augers, sectional cable machines or a hydro jetter, depending on how far down the blockage sits and what the pipe is made of. When a clog keeps coming back, we run a camera to look for roots or a cracked hub.
