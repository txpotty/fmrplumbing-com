    Hard-Water Drain Damage Repair in Las Vegas, NV

    Water in much of Nevada is very hard, and scale buildup is one of the most common reasons water heaters and fixtures fail early. Scale grows inside pop-up bodies and on stopper rods until the linkage seizes. Forcing a scaled lift rod usually snaps the pivot and leaves the stopper stuck, with a narrowed tailpiece slowing the drain. The scaled parts all come out. Smooth PVC sheds buildup better.

    Push-Button Drain Installation in Las Vegas, NV

    Push-button drains get rid of the lift rod. A press on the cap opens and closes them, and a slotted body only goes in sinks with an overflow. Cleaning is simpler with no linkage. Hair still collects, so pull the cap now and then.

    Leaking P-Trap Replacement in Las Vegas

    Traps leak at the joints first. Slip nut washers flatten and harden with age, and a trap opened often for clogs usually has stripped threads. A new trap kit costs little. Labor is most of the bill.

    Corroded Drain Replacement in Las Vegas, NV

    Drain cleaners speed up corrosion. Chemicals left sitting in a metal trap eat through it, so many old traps we pull have pinholes at the bottom. PVC does not have that problem. We recommend it for most cabinets.

    Vessel and Grid Drain Replacement in Las Vegas

    Vessel sinks use a different drain. Bowls without an overflow need an unslotted grid or umbrella drain, or water leaks out the slots. We see the wrong drain often. Swapping it takes under an hour.

    Code Updates During Drain Replacement in Las Vegas

    Water in much of Nevada is very hard, and scale buildup is one of the most common reasons water heaters and fixtures fail early. Over years, minerals coat the inside of metal drain pipe and shrink the opening. Scale rarely clogs a drain alone, but it catches grease and hair so clogs come faster. Plastic pipe resists it.

    On a newer drain, just the part. Older chrome or thin brass drains tend to fail joint by joint, so a full replacement is cheaper in the long run. We price both for you.

    About an hour for a bath sink. Kitchens run longer for the disposal and dishwasher, and a crumbling wall stub adds time. We tell you up front.
