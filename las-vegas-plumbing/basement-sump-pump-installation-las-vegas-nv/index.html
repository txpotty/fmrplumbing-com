    Battery Backup Sump Pump Systems in Las Vegas

    Power outages during heavy storms can render a primary sump pump useless, leading to basement flooding. A battery backup sump pump provides crucial protection when the power goes out. We install reliable backup systems that activate automatically, keeping water moving even without electricity.     Sump Pump Basin and Discharge Line Installation in Las Vegas

    A complete sump pump system needs a properly installed basin and discharge line. We handle the excavation and placement of the sump basin, setting it at the lowest point of your basement floor so it collects water effectively. We also install the discharge line, ensuring it is correctly sloped, sealed and routed away from your foundation to a suitable drainage area, so water does not re-enter your Las Vegas property.

    Discharge Line and Check Valve Installation in Las Vegas

    A great pump is useless if the water it moves runs right back toward the foundation. We route rigid PVC discharge pipe out of the basement and extend it at least 10 feet from the house or into a pop-up emitter or dry well. A spring or swing check valve keeps the column of water from dropping back into the pit after every cycle, which protects the motor. Freezing weather is uncommon in the southern desert of Nevada, though occasional cold nights can still damage exposed outdoor pipes. Where lines can freeze, we add a freeze-resistant outlet so ice cannot block the line.

    Pedestal Sump Pumps in Las Vegas

    Pedestal pumps keep the motor above the pit, which makes them easier to service and a good fit for narrow pits in older homes. They usually last a long time because the motor never sits in water. The tradeoff is more noise and a less finished look, so we usually recommend them for utility rooms rather than living spaces. We install them with a rigid discharge, a quality check valve and a float rod adjusted to your pit depth.

    FMR Plumbing offers a comprehensive warranty on our basement sump pump installations, covering both parts and labor. The specific warranty period will be detailed in your service agreement. We stand behind our work and the quality of the products we install in your Las Vegas home.

    We install a wide range of well-built, reliable sump pump brands, including Zoeller, Wayne, Little Giant and Liberty Pumps. We can help you pick the best brand and model for your budget and water management needs in your Las Vegas home, considering horsepower and features.

    Drought is the long-term concern in Nevada, and water conservation rules shape everything from fixtures to irrigation. Because storms and power outages tend to arrive together, a battery backup sump pump is highly recommended. It offers crucial protection by switching on automatically when the main power fails, so your basement stays dry even without electricity. It is a wise investment for peace of mind.

    A primary sump pump is your main defense against basement flooding, built to handle everyday water removal. A secondary sump pump, often a battery-powered backup, serves as an emergency system that activates if the primary pump fails, loses power or is overwhelmed by excess water. Both work together for maximum protection.

    A submersible sump pump sits entirely within the sump pit, making it quieter and less obtrusive. A pedestal sump pump has its motor above the pit, making it easier to access for maintenance and usually more affordable. Both types are effective for removing water from your basement.

    A standard basement sump pump installation typically takes 4 to 8 hours. If a new sump pit needs to be excavated, or there are unexpected challenges with plumbing or electrical access, the job might stretch to a full day. Our team works efficiently to limit disruption to your home.
