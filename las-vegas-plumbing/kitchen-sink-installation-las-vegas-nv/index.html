    Frequently Asked Questions About Kitchen Sink Installation in Las Vegas

    A basic drop-in kitchen sink install typically costs $300 to $600 for labor. Undermount and farmhouse installs usually run $450 to $1,200, and adding a disposal or relocating the drain adds $150 to $600.

    We install Kohler, Blanco, Elkay, Kraus, Franke, Ruvati and many others. You can supply your sink or we can recommend one.

    A simple drop-in swap is doable for experienced DIYers. Undermount, farmhouse and double-bowl installs with disposals are more complex and prone to leaks.

    Top-mount (drop-in) sinks sit on top of the counter, with a visible rim, and are usually easier to install. Under-mount sinks are installed beneath the counter, creating a seamless look, but require more complex sealing and support for a secure installation.

    A like-for-like replacement usually does not. Moving drain lines or adding a new sink location may require a permit, depending on your local jurisdiction.

    Signs you might need a new kitchen sink include persistent leaks around the drain or edges, visible cracks or chips, severe staining that won't clean, rust spots, or a desire to upgrade your kitchen's look. If your current sink is old or damaged, replacement is often the smartest solution.

    Most installs take three to five hours. Undermount sinks may need time for adhesive to set before the drain is fully loaded.
