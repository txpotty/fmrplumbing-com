    What a Water Heater Service Visit Covers in Las Vegas, NV

    Anode Rod Checks in Las Vegas

    The anode rod rusts so the tank does not. Once the rod is eaten down to its core wire, the water goes after the tank itself. It comes out for inspection. Replacing the rod is much cheaper than replacing the heater.

    Descaling Tankless Units in Las Vegas

    Error codes often mean scale. Scale in the exchanger cuts flow and trips the unit's safety shutoff. We descale through the service valves. Yearly descaling is standard advice for hard water.

    Flushing a Tank Water Heater in Las Vegas

    Popping and rumbling mean sediment. Scale on the tank bottom traps heat and causes water to pop and rumble as it heats. The tank drains until the water is clean. Heavy buildup may take several cycles.

    Tank Water Heater Maintenance in Las Vegas

    Flushing is the heart of tank maintenance. Water in much of Nevada is very hard, and scale buildup is one of the most common reasons water heaters and fixtures fail early. The tank gets drained until it runs clear, then refilled, and the anode, relief valve and connections get checked. Plan on an hour. Less sediment means more years.

    Thermostat, Element and Burner Checks in Las Vegas

    Wrong temperature means a part is off. Electric units get thermostat and element tests, and gas units get burner, pilot and control checks.
