    What Our Las Vegas, NV Drain Techs Clean

    Kitchen Sink Drain Cleaning in Las Vegas

    Coffee grounds and eggshells are a sink's worst enemies. Both sink to the bottom of the pipe and pack down hard. We cable from the trap arm or a cleanout and work the cutter back and forth until the head runs free, then checks the disposal and the dishwasher connection, since either one can push food into the line. Most kitchen sinks take less than an hour.

    Hydro Jetting for Drains in Las Vegas

    Jetting does what a cable cannot. A jetter sprays the full circumference of the pipe at high pressure. A cable punches a hole, while a jetter cleans the pipe back to near its original diameter. It works best on a kitchen or main line that keeps clogging every few months no matter how often it gets cabled.

    Outdoor & Storm Drain Cleaning in Las Vegas

    Yard drains and downspout lines fill with leaves, grit and roots. Snowmelt in the northern mountains can raise groundwater in spring, which can affect basements and low-lying properties. Puddles against the slab after rain mean the line is not moving water. The pipe gets cleaned all the way to the daylight outlet, and the catch basin gets emptied, so the next storm runs off instead of soaking into the soil next to the house.

    Commercial Drain Cleaning in Las Vegas, NV

    Restaurants, salons and offices load drains harder than homes do. Grease, hair and paper all show up in volume. Our commercial setup includes larger cable and jetting equipment for 4 to 6 inch pipe, and we can schedule work before opening or after close so you do not lose a lunch rush to a plumber in the kitchen.

    Kitchen Drain Cleaning in Las Vegas

    Grease is the big one in kitchens. It goes down warm and sets up like candle wax in the pipe. Bits of food and soap catch on that coating until the 1-1/2 inch line is down to the size of a pencil, and we clear it with a drum machine that is safe for PVC and old cast iron alike.

    Restaurant and Shop Drains in Las Vegas, NV

    Commercial kitchens fight fats, oils and grease every day. The grease interceptor helps, but lines still coat. A regular jetting schedule, set by how much frying your kitchen does, keeps the line open, and we leave a service report you can show the health inspector or the landlord.

    Camera Inspection After Drain Cleaning in Las Vegas

    A camera shows what the cable felt. Self-leveling heads keep the picture upright. In cast iron we often find scale, channeling along the bottom of the pipe and cracked hubs, while clay lines show offset joints and root entry, and a locator marks the depth on the surface so a repair can be dug in the right spot.

    Jetting Versus Cabling in Las Vegas, NV

    Cabling and jetting solve different problems. A cable cuts through a blockage. A jetter scrubs the pipe wall clean and flushes the debris downstream to the city main or septic tank, so we cable first on a hard root mass and jet afterward when the line has years of buildup on it.
