    Badger Series Installation in Las Vegas, NV

    The Badger line runs from the 1/3 HP Badger 1 up to the Badger 5XP. Every Badger shares one quick-lock mount.     Evolution Series Installation in Las Vegas

    Evolution units grind in two or three stages and carry extra sound insulation around the motor housing, which is why they sit taller and weigh more than a Badger.     First-Time Disposal Installation in Las Vegas

    Kitchens that never had a disposal need some rework. First, the sink gets a disposal flange. The tailpiece and trap have to be reworked, and the cabinet needs a switched outlet, which means an electrician if there is no power under the sink already. We also check the drain slope to the stack.

    Badger Replacement for Las Vegas Homes

    Builder-grade kitchens almost always started with a Badger, and a worn one usually gets replaced with another. First the old unit comes off. A matching quick-lock mount in good shape can stay, which saves time and keeps a seal that already fits the sink. The new Badger goes up and gets tested.

    Upgrading Your InSinkErator in Las Vegas, NV

    Moving up from a 1/3 HP builder unit to a 3/4 HP Evolution changes more than the grind. The new unit is taller. Because of that height, the discharge can end up below the trap arm, and we measure first so we can lower the trap if needed. We also confirm the circuit can carry the larger motor.

    Dishwasher Connection and Air Gap Setup in Las Vegas

    Nearly every InSinkErator has a dishwasher inlet with a factory knockout plug inside. The plug gets knocked loose and pulled out of the grind chamber.
