    Old Toilet Upgrades in Las Vegas, NV

    Bowls from before 1994 were built for 3.5 gallon flushes or more, and pairing one with a new low-flow tank does not work because the trapway and rim are sized for the larger volume.     Bowl Replacement Versus Full Toilet Replacement in Las Vegas, NV

    Freeze-Cracked Bowl Replacement in Las Vegas

    Freezing weather is uncommon in the southern desert of Nevada, though occasional cold nights can still damage exposed outdoor pipes.
