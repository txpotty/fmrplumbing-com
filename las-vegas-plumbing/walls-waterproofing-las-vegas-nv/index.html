    Drainage System Integration in Las Vegas, NV

    Water has to go somewhere once a wall is sealed. Footing drains, interior drain tile and French drains in the yard all do the same job at different depths. We match the drain to where the water comes from, which might be a high water table, a yard that slopes toward the house, or a gutter dumping at the corner. Downspouts get extended at least four feet out.

    Laundry Room and Utility Wall Waterproofing in Las Vegas, NV

    Rubber washing machine hoses burst. We swap them for braided stainless hoses, put a drain pan under the washer with a line to a floor drain where one is available, and install a quarter-turn shutoff or an automatic leak valve on the supply box. Water heaters get a pan too. Behind the heater we can install moisture-resistant board and seal it.

    Crawl Space Encapsulation in Las Vegas

    Encapsulation seals the whole crawl space, walls and floor, with a reinforced poly liner. We run 12 to 20 mil liner up the walls, tape every seam, seal around piers and pipe penetrations, and usually add a dehumidifier to hold humidity under 60 percent. The high desert in the north brings cold winters and big daily temperature swings that stress pipes and fittings. The joists stop rotting. Drier air under the floor also cuts down on the musty smell in the house.

    Shower Wall Waterproofing in Las Vegas

    Tile and grout are not waterproof. Water passes through grout every time you shower, so the layer behind the tile, a membrane over cement board or a foam board system, is what actually keeps the studs dry. We tear out failed tile, install new backer, and apply Schluter Kerdi, RedGard or Hydro Ban at the thickness the manufacturer calls for. We reinforce corners, niches and valve penetrations with fabric or preformed pieces.

    Waterproofing for Older Homes in Las Vegas, NV

    Houses built before modern drainage rules often have no footing drain at all, or a clay tile drain that collapsed decades ago. Hard water and slab construction make slab leaks in copper lines a common repair in older neighborhoods. Fieldstone and old brick foundations need breathable products. Sealing soft old brick with a rigid coating traps water inside it and makes the face spall off over a few seasons, so we use lime-compatible or vapor-open coatings and pair them with interior drain tile. Old mortar joints get repointed first.

    Basement Wall Sealing in Las Vegas, NV

    Concrete block is porous, and its hollow cores fill with water like a sponge. The high desert in the north brings cold winters and big daily temperature swings that stress pipes and fittings. We drill weep holes at the bottom course to drain the cores, then coat the face with a masonry waterproofing product that lets vapor out but stops liquid water. The musty smell usually fades within weeks. The weep holes empty into interior drain tile when there is one.

    Paying once beats paying every two years.     Crawl Space Vapor Barriers in Las Vegas

    Bare dirt in a crawl space releases gallons of water vapor a day into the air under your floor. A 10 mil or heavier polyethylene barrier, lapped 12 inches at the seams and taped, cuts that off and keeps the joists and subfloor dry. We run it up the foundation walls and hold it in place with termination strip. It is a cheap fix that makes a big difference.
