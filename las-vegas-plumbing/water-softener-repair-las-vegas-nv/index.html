    Control Valve Troubleshooting & Repair in Las Vegas, NV

    Stuck pistons, worn seals and failed drive motors cause most control valve trouble, and the symptoms are hard water, constant regeneration or a leak at the head. The valve gets tested in place.     Resin Bed Problems in Las Vegas

    Fouled resin is the usual reason a softener regenerates on schedule but still sends hard water to every faucet in the house. Iron and sediment coat the beads so they cannot grab hardness. We test the resin, clean it with a resin cleaner or replace it. New resin restores full capacity.

    Resin Bed Replacement in Las Vegas

    Chlorine eats resin slowly, and after enough years the beads swell, break and stop exchanging the calcium and magnesium they are supposed to hold. Older systems on chlorinated city water see it most. FMR Plumbing removes the old resin and installs fresh, high-capacity media. Capacity comes back right away.

    Water Softener Leak Detection and Repair in Las Vegas, NV

    Cracked tanks, loose fittings and failed O-rings are behind most softener leaks. A small drip still wastes water and damages flooring. We find the source, whether it is the bypass, the valve-to-tank seal or the drain connection, and replace the seal or fitting that failed. We test it under pressure.

    Bypass and Fitting Leak Repair in Las Vegas, NV

    Bypass valves, the O-rings where the valve meets the tank, cracked fittings and the drain line are the usual leak points. Even a small drip wastes water and can stain a garage floor or rot a cabinet. O-rings and fittings get replaced and pressure tested. A cracked mineral tank usually means replacement.
