    Drain Repair Options for Las Vegas, NV Homes

    Kitchen Sink Drain Repair in Las Vegas, NV

    Kitchen drains take grease, food and soap scum. No drain clogs more often. We clear the branch, replace corroded or cracked tailpieces, fix leaking slip nuts and reset traps that were installed too low or too high for the trap arm to drain properly. We also check the disposal and dishwasher connections.

    Cast Iron Drain Stack Repair in Las Vegas, NV

    Old cast iron stacks rot from the inside out. The bottom goes first. Rust scale catches debris and hairline cracks let water seep into walls and floors, and we replace the bad length with plastic pipe joined to the old iron with shielded couplings. Full stack replacement gets a separate quote.

    Vent Pipe Repair in Las Vegas

    Sewer gas leaking into a room can point to a missing vent. Remodels cause a lot of these. Bird nests, debris and roofing work can block existing vents, while fixtures that never had a vent can sometimes get an air admittance valve if code allows. Proper venting protects every trap in the house.

    Laundry and Floor Drain Repair in Las Vegas

    A washer dumps a lot of water fast. Small standpipes overflow. We correct standpipe height, replace cracked laundry box drains and add venting where it is missing, and for garage and utility floor drains we restore the trap seal so sewer odor stays in the pipe. Trap primers get repaired too.

    Storm Drain Repair in Las Vegas, NV

    Yard and storm drains carry rain away from the foundation. Sudden summer storms can cause flash flooding that overwhelms drainage and washes debris into storm drains. Debris and silt fill them up. We clear the line with a jetter, camera it to look for crushed or separated sections, and replace damaged corrugated or PVC pipe so water flows to the outlet instead of pooling against the house. We check the discharge end every time.

    Exterior Drain Tile Repair in Las Vegas, NV

    Drain tile, sometimes called a French drain, carries groundwater away from the footing. It clogs with silt. Most homes sit on concrete slabs, so leaks in buried supply lines often show up as warm spots on the floor or higher water bills. When a tile line is crushed, clogged or was laid without fabric, water builds up against the foundation, so we camera the tile, find the failed section and replace it with perforated pipe wrapped in filter fabric and clean gravel. The outlet gets checked as well.

    Restaurant and Office Drain Repair in Las Vegas

    Restaurants, salons, offices and medical suites all need working drains during business hours. Grease is the big one. We repair grease-laden kitchen lines, floor sinks, mop sink drains and restroom fixtures, and we can schedule the work early or after closing so you do not lose a day of business. Maintenance plans catch trouble early.

    Leaking Drain Pipe Repair in Las Vegas, NV

    Drain leaks are low pressure, so they drip instead of spray. Most go unnoticed for months. Corrosion, shifting framing and cracked fittings are the usual causes, and we trace the leak by running each fixture and watching the joints, then cut out the damaged section and replace it with matching PVC, ABS or cast iron. Repairs meet local plumbing code.
