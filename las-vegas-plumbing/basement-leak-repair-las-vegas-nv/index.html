    Frequently Asked Questions About Basement Leak Repair in Las Vegas

    Most wet basement repairs fall between $500 and $5,000, and the spread comes from what is causing the water. Regrading or extending downspouts is at the low end. Interior drains cost more since the slab gets cut, and exterior work costs most because of the digging. We give a written price first.

    Crack repairs use two-part polyurethane or epoxy injection resin, depending on whether the crack is actively wet. Wet cracks get polyurethane. Our interior drains use rigid perforated pipe, which keeps its slope and can be jetted later.

    Small repairs usually do not need a permit. Bigger changes often do. Stack replacement and new backwater valves often need one, and we handle the permit and inspection.

    Gutters and downspouts do most of the work. Keep them clear and extended well away from the foundation. Test the sump each spring and swap rubber washer hoses for braided ones every five years.

    Check the base of the walls after a heavy rain. Dark damp bands, puddles at the cove joint or water near the floor drain are the usual signs. For plumbing leaks, turn off every faucet and appliance, then watch the water meter for 30 minutes, and if the dial or leak indicator moves, water is escaping somewhere in the system. Note where the water shows.

    Tightening a loose fitting or replacing a washer hose is a reasonable DIY task. Cracked drain stacks are not. Hidden slab leaks and sump wiring also belong with a pro, since a mistake can flood the basement or leave an outlet energized in a wet space, and those repairs often need a permit.

    Most plumbing leak repairs in a basement are done in one visit. With wall access, plan on two to four hours. Seepage repairs vary more, from half a day for an injected crack to most of a week for a full interior perimeter drain, which includes cutting and removing the slab edge, setting pipe and stone, and pouring new concrete.

    Leak repair stops a specific source, like a cracked pipe or a leaking wall crack. Waterproofing is broader.
