    Costs depend on the project. A standard tank water heater install usually runs $1,600 to $3,500, a water softener $1,500 to $4,000, and a whole-home PEX repipe $5,000 to $15,000 or more depending on home size.

    Equipment carries the manufacturer's warranty, often 6 to 12 years for water heaters. We also warranty our installation workmanship.

    FMR Plumbing installs a wide range of reputable brands, including Moen, Delta, Kohler for fixtures, Rheem, Bradford White, and Rinnai for water heaters, and Zoeller or Liberty Pumps for sump and ejector pumps.     PEX (cross-linked polyethylene) is a flexible plastic tubing used for water supply lines. Its flexibility allows for fewer fittings, reducing potential leak points and making it a durable choice for Nevada homes.

    A water heater or softener install usually takes a few hours. Gas line runs and water line replacements can take a day, while whole-home repipes may take several days.
