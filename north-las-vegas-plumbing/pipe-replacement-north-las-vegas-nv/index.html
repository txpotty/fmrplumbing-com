    Water Line Replacement vs. Repair in North Las Vegas, NV

    One leak on otherwise good pipe is a repair. Several leaks in different rooms, rusty water throughout, or pipe that crumbles when we cut it means the rest is close behind. We show you what we find.

    Polybutylene Pipe Replacement in North Las Vegas

    Polybutylene is gray or blue plastic installed from roughly the late 1970s to mid 1990s. It degrades from the inside. Failures tend to be sudden splits at fittings rather than slow drips, and some insurers refuse to cover homes that still have it. Replace it.

    Drain Pipe Replacement in North Las Vegas, NV

    Drain lines fail too. Old cast iron rusts through. We replace cracked cast iron, failing ABS or old galvanized drains with PVC, checking slope and venting so the new drains flow freely. Camera first.

    Galvanized Pipe Replacement in North Las Vegas, NV

    Galvanized steel was common before the 1960s. The zinc wears off and the steel rusts inside. Over decades the opening shrinks, pressure drops and the water turns brown after sitting, while threaded joints corrode through first. Replacing it is the only fix.

    Slab Leak Pipe Replacement in North Las Vegas

    Rerouting is often better than digging. Breaking a slab is messy and costly. We run new pipe through the attic or walls to bypass the leaking line under the concrete, which leaves the floors alone and prevents the same line from failing again. Fast too.
