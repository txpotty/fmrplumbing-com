    Emergencies go to the top of the board. We aim to have a plumber at the house within a few hours, and for a burst pipe or sewage backup we will walk you through closing the main shutoff on the phone so the damage stops while we drive. Grab towels and a bucket while you wait.

    Hard water carries dissolved calcium and magnesium. A softener swaps those minerals out through ion exchange, and the difference shows up as scale in water heaters, white crust on faucets, spotty dishes and soap that will not lather well. Fixtures last longer on soft water.
