    Slab Leak Repiping Solutions in North Las Vegas

    Slab leaks are a costly and damaging problem for homes built on concrete foundations. Caliche, expansive clay and rocky desert soil are common in Nevada, and caliche can make digging for sewer lines slow and costly. These leaks often require rerouting or repiping entire sections of your plumbing system to bypass the compromised pipes under the slab. Our repipe specialists use modern detection tools to pinpoint slab leaks and then reroute new PEX or copper lines through your attic or walls, completely bypassing the problematic underground sections. This prevents further foundation damage and restores water service without breaking your slab.

    Efficient PEX Repiping in North Las Vegas, NV

    PEX (cross-linked polyethylene) is a flexible, cost-effective, and highly popular choice for repiping projects in North Las Vegas, NV. Its flexibility allows for fewer connections, reducing potential leak points and making installation quicker and less intrusive than traditional rigid pipes. PEX is also highly resistant to freezing, scaling, and chlorine, common issues in many water systems. We use high-quality PEX-A or PEX-B tubing, installed with durable crimp or expansion fittings, to create a dependable and efficient plumbing network throughout your home.

    Copper Repiping in North Las Vegas, NV

    Some homeowners prefer copper for its long track record and rigidity. We install Type L copper, which has a thicker wall than the Type M used in many older homes. Joints are soldered with lead-free solder or pressed with ProPress fittings. Copper repipes usually cost more and take a bit longer than PEX.

    Kitec and Polybutylene Replacement in North Las Vegas

    Kitec, with its orange or blue PEX-AL-PEX pipe and brass fittings, and gray polybutylene pipe are both known for failures. Many homes built in the 1980s through early 2000s contain one or the other. We replace these systems completely so your home is easier to insure and sell later. Visible pipe at the water heater often tells us which type you have.
