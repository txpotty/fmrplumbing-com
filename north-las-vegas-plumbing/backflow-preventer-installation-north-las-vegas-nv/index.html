    Pressure Vacuum Breaker (PVB) Installation in North Las Vegas, NV

    For low-hazard applications, such as lawn irrigation systems in North Las Vegas, NV, a pressure vacuum breaker, or PVB, is a popular and cost-effective backflow prevention device. PVBs protect against back-siphonage by letting air into the system when pressure drops. Our certified plumbers carefully install PVBs above the highest point of downstream piping, as code requires, to maintain effective protection. We also check for proper clearance and drainage.

    Irrigation Backflow Preventer Installation in North Las Vegas

    Landscape irrigation is the most common reason homeowners need a backflow device. Fertilizer, herbicides and stagnant water in sprinkler and drip lines can be siphoned into your drinking water if pressure drops. We install pressure vacuum breakers or double check assemblies based on local requirements and the layout of your system. Above-ground installs are set at least 12 inches above the highest sprinkler head when a PVB is used.

    Backflow Prevention for Irrigation Systems in North Las Vegas, NV

    Irrigation systems in North Las Vegas, NV are a common source of backflow, as stagnant water and lawn chemicals can be drawn back into the potable water supply.     Double Check Valve Assembly Installation in North Las Vegas

    Double check assemblies are used for low hazard connections such as many fire sprinkler systems and some irrigation lines. They have two spring-loaded check valves and test cocks for annual certification. Unlike an RPZ, they do not discharge water, so they can be installed in vaults or below grade where local rules allow. We size them to avoid excessive pressure loss on your system.
