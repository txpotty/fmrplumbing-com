    The pressure tank works together with your well pump to maintain consistent water pressure and reduce pump cycling. A waterlogged tank, a ruptured diaphragm, or a faulty air valve can cause rapid pump cycling, fluctuating water pressure, or even no water at all. Our team accurately diagnoses pressure tank problems and performs necessary repairs or replacements. We make sure your tank is properly sized and charged for optimal system performance in North Las Vegas.

    Control Box and Electrical Repair in North Las Vegas, NV

    Three-wire submersible pumps rely on a control box with a start capacitor and relay.     Jet Pump System Repair in North Las Vegas, NV

    Jet pumps, typically found above ground, use an ejector assembly to draw water from the well. Typical problems include air leaks in the suction line, clogged nozzles, or a failing motor. These issues can lead to intermittent water flow or a pump that runs continuously without building pressure. FMR Plumbing can reliably troubleshoot and repair these components, ensuring your jet pump system in North Las Vegas operates at peak efficiency. We also check for proper priming, which is essential for jet pump function.

    Variable speed systems like the Grundfos SQE and Franklin SubDrive keep pressure steady by adjusting motor speed. When they fault out, the drive usually shows a code that guides diagnosis. We troubleshoot drives, sensors and wiring and repair or replace components. These systems need a smaller pressure tank but careful setup.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Well Pump Repair in North Las Vegas

    Minor repairs like a pressure switch or capacitor typically cost $150 to $500. Pressure tank replacement often runs $600 to $1,500, and replacing a deep submersible pump can range from $1,800 to $5,000 or more. We quote the price before starting work.

    Most pump replacements take four to eight hours once parts are on site. Very deep wells or stuck pumps can take longer.
