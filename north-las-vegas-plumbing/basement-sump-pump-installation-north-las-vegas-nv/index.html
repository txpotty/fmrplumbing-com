    Discharge Line and Check Valve Installation in North Las Vegas

    A great pump is useless if the water it moves runs right back toward the foundation. We route rigid PVC discharge pipe out of the basement and extend it at least 10 feet from the house or into a pop-up emitter or dry well. A spring or swing check valve keeps the column of water from dropping back into the pit after every cycle, which protects the motor. Freezing weather is uncommon in the southern desert of Nevada, though occasional cold nights can still damage exposed outdoor pipes. Where lines can freeze, we add a freeze-resistant outlet so ice cannot block the discharge.

    Sump Pump Alarms and Smart Monitoring in North Las Vegas, NV

    A high water alarm is an inexpensive part that gives you warning before water reaches the floor. We install standalone alarms or Wi-Fi monitors that send a text if the water level rises, the pump fails or power drops. These are a favorite with homeowners who travel or own second homes. We test the float trigger in front of you so you know how the alarm sounds.

    A complete sump pump system needs a properly installed basin and discharge line.     Pedestal Sump Pumps in North Las Vegas

    Pedestal pumps keep the motor above the pit, which makes them easier to service and a solid fit for narrow pits in older homes. They usually last a long time because the motor never sits in water. The tradeoff is more noise and a less finished look, so we usually recommend them for utility rooms rather than living spaces. We install them with a rigid discharge, a quality check valve and a float rod adjusted to your pit depth.
