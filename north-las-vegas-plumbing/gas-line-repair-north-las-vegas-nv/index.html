    Gas Line Relocation and Upgrades in North Las Vegas, NV

    A kitchen remodel or a new tankless water heater can push a gas system past what its original pipe can deliver. Tankless units need a lot of gas. Many tankless heaters are rated at 150,000 to 199,000 BTU, which can be more than double what an old tank heater used, so the line feeding it often has to be upsized back toward the meter. We calculate the full load first. Then we size the new piping so every appliance in the house still gets full pressure at the same time.

    Pool Heater, Fire Pit and Grill Lines in North Las Vegas, NV

    Black Iron Pipe Repair in North Las Vegas, NV

    Black iron is the standard interior gas pipe in a lot of homes, and it lasts a long time indoors. Joints are the weak point. Years of vibration from a furnace or a house settling can loosen threaded fittings, and pipe in a damp garage or an exterior wall can rust from the outside in. Bad sections get replaced with new pipe. Rusted pipe that runs outside gets painted or replaced with coated steel so it does not come back as the same problem.

    Gas Line Extensions and Relocations in North Las Vegas

    Gas Leak Repair at Fittings and Valves in North Las Vegas
