    Frequently Asked Questions About Garbage Disposal Replacement in North Las Vegas

    A standard disposal replacement with the unit usually costs $275 to $650. Premium models like InSinkErator Evolution usually run $500 to $1,000 installed.

    Key signs you need a new garbage disposal include persistent leaks from the unit's housing, a humming noise without grinding, frequent clogs despite proper usage, foul odors that won't go away, or if the unit simply won't turn on. These symptoms often indicate internal motor or component failure.

    Leaks from the body, frequent resets, constant jams, loud grinding and age over ten years are common signs.

    To prolong the life of your new disposal, avoid putting fibrous foods like celery, corn husks, or onion skins down it. Also, do not dispose of grease, coffee grounds, or non-food items. Always run cold water before, during, and after use, and grind small amounts of citrus peels or ice cubes now and then to clean the unit.

    Most disposals last 8 to 12 years. Hard water, heavy use and grease can shorten that life.

    We install and replace all major brands, including InSinkErator, Moen, Waste King, KitchenAid, and Badger. Our plumbers are familiar with the installation requirements for each brand, ensuring a perfect fit.

    For light use in a single-person household, 1/3 HP might be enough. Most homeowners opt for 1/2 HP or 3/4 HP for general use, which handles common food waste well. For large families, frequent cooking, or tougher waste like small bones, a 1 HP unit offers the best performance and durability.

    If your disposal is old, frequently leaking, or making grinding noises without grinding food, replacement is usually more cost-effective and provides a longer-lasting solution. Minor issues like a simple jam can often be repaired.
