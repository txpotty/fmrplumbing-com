    Hose Bib and Outdoor Faucet Repair in North Las Vegas, NV

    Outdoor faucets freeze easily, especially with a hose left connected. A split often hides inside the wall. You will not see it until the next time you turn on the faucet in spring and water pours into the basement or crawlspace, so we replace split hose bibs with frost-free models that shut off inside the heated wall.

    Pipe Insulation and Heat Cable Installation in North Las Vegas, NV

    Insulation slows heat loss, but it does not add heat. Exposed runs get heat cable. We install self-regulating cable that adjusts its output to the pipe temperature, plugged into a GFCI outlet, and cover it with insulation so it uses less power and keeps the pipe above freezing on the coldest nights.

    Backflow Preventer and Irrigation Freeze Repair in North Las Vegas

    Backflow preventers and irrigation valves sit above ground and take the coldest air directly. Brass bodies crack. We repair or replace the damaged assembly, have it tested if your water utility requires it, and show you how to drain it or fit an insulated cover before the next freeze.

    Heat Cable for Exposed Pipes in North Las Vegas

    Some pipes cannot be rerouted or insulated enough on their own, like a supply line under a manufactured home or a pipe through an unheated porch. Heat cable handles those. We replace old constant-wattage tape with listed self-regulating cable.

    Preventive Pipe Insulation in North Las Vegas

    Foam pipe insulation is cheap and stops most freezes in attics and crawlspaces. It has to fit. Seams get taped and corners get mitered, because one bare fitting is often where the freeze starts.

    Freeze Protection for Commercial Buildings in North Las Vegas, NV

    Vacant suites and warehouses freeze when thermostats are set too low. Property managers get a freeze plan. That plan lists the shutoffs, the at-risk runs, the heat cable and the minimum thermostat setting for each space, so building staff know exactly what to do when a cold front is in the forecast.

    Thawing Drain Lines and Sewer Pipes in North Las Vegas

    Drain lines can freeze too, usually where a line runs through an unheated crawlspace or an exterior wall. You will see the backup first. Hot water through the cleanout thaws it, and then we check for a sag that holds water.

    Attic and Exterior Wall Pipe Repair in North Las Vegas

    Many homes run supply lines through attics or crawlspaces to reach bathrooms and kitchens. Those are the first to freeze. After repairing a split, we look at whether the line can be moved into a heated space, buried under attic insulation on the warm side, or wrapped with heat cable, since the same pipe will freeze again otherwise.
