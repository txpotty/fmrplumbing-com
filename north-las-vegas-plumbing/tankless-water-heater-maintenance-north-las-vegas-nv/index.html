    Noritz Tankless Tune-Ups in North Las Vegas, NV

    Noritz builds a solid heater, but solid does not mean maintenance-free. We start a Noritz tune-up at the exhaust, checking for soot and any blocked termination. Next comes the combustion air intake and the fan. Last, we check the temperature setting and run a few fixtures at once, which shows whether the unit holds temperature between back-to-back showers.

    Tankless Error Code Diagnosis in North Las Vegas, NV

    An error code is the heater telling you what it saw. On a Rinnai an LC code means scale, on a Navien E003 means no ignition, and on a Noritz a 90 usually means an air or exhaust problem. We pull the code history before touching anything. If a part has failed, you hear the repair and the price before we start.

    Navien Condensing Tune-Ups in North Las Vegas

    Navien NPE units are condensing heaters with a second heat exchanger that pulls extra heat out of the exhaust before it leaves the house. That second exchanger makes acidic condensate. We clear the condensate trap and check the neutralizer media. A-series models with built-in recirculation also get a pump and buffer tank check.

    Rinnai Tankless Service in North Las Vegas

    Rinnai units tell you a lot through the controller. Code 11 means the burner never lit, and code 12 means the flame went out after it did. We clean the Rinnai air intake filter and look over the flame rod for soot or cracks. The LC codes are scale warnings, and they usually clear once the exchanger has been flushed and the unit is reset.

    Yearly Tankless Inspection in North Las Vegas

    A yearly inspection costs less than one service call for a dead heater. We soap-test the gas connections and try the pressure relief valve. We pull the air filter and check the igniter. Anything that is wearing out goes on a written list with a plain price, and you decide what gets done now and what can wait until next year.

    Commercial Tankless Maintenance in North Las Vegas, NV

    Commercial kitchens and salons burn through hot water all day, so their tankless units scale up much faster than a house unit. Gas pressure gets checked with every burner lit, not at idle. Inlet screens and air filters get pulled and cleaned. Downtime is the real cost. We can book it before you open or after you close.

    Condensate and Neutralizer Service in North Las Vegas

    Condensing tankless heaters drip acidic condensate, often around pH 3 to 4, and it needs to pass through a neutralizer before it reaches a drain. We clean the trap and look at the neutralizer media. We replace spent media. An outdoor run of condensate line gets checked for support and protection.

    Multi-Unit Tankless Checks in North Las Vegas, NV

    Restaurants and salons often run two or more tankless heaters on a shared manifold, and one weak unit can drag the whole bank down. We check gas pressure with all units firing at once. We take a flow and temperature reading on each heater. Then we compare them.
