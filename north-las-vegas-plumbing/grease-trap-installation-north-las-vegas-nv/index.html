    Hydromechanical Grease Trap Installation in North Las Vegas

    Hydromechanical grease traps sit under or near sinks and use baffles and flow control to separate grease from water. They are common in small cafes, coffee shops and quick-service restaurants with tight space. We install units from Zurn, Watts, Schier and Endura sized by gallons per minute. Each install includes a flow control fitting and proper venting to keep the trap working as designed. These units need frequent cleaning, so we place them where staff can reach the lid easily.

    Gravity Grease Interceptor Installation in North Las Vegas, NV

    Gravity grease interceptors, or GGI units, are larger, typically located outdoors, and rely on the natural difference in specific gravity between FOG and water. These are common for high-volume commercial kitchens. Our team handles the complex excavation, concrete work, and plumbing connections required for proper GGI installation, maintaining compliance with all local Nevada ordinances for waste management.

    Hydro-Mechanical Grease Interceptor Installation in North Las Vegas

    Hydro-mechanical grease interceptors, often called HGI units, are compact and designed for indoor use, typically under a sink or on the floor. These units use baffles to slow down wastewater flow, allowing grease to separate and accumulate. FMR Plumbing installs various sizes of HGI units, ensuring proper venting, leveling, and connection to your drainage system in North Las Vegas for the best efficiency.

    Under-Sink Grease Trap Setup in North Las Vegas, NV

    Smaller establishments or specific kitchen stations in North Las Vegas, NV may benefit from compact under-sink grease traps. These units are ideal for capturing FOG at the source, preventing it from entering the main plumbing lines. Our team expertly installs these smaller units, ensuring proper sizing, sealing, and connection to your existing plumbing system for effective waste management and odor control.
