    Frequently Asked Questions About Storm Drain Cleaning in North Las Vegas

    The cost of storm drain cleaning in North Las Vegas usually ranges from $200 to $600, depending on the severity of the clog, the size of the drain, and the equipment required. More complex blockages or those requiring extensive hydro jetting may be at the higher end of this range. We provide upfront pricing before any work starts.

    It is generally recommended to have your storm drains inspected and cleaned at least once a year. Properties with many trees or those that see frequent heavy rains may need twice-yearly cleaning to prevent buildup and blockages.

    Water pooling around grates, overflowing downspouts, standing water against walls and mud stains near inlets are typical signs.

    We use a range of professional-grade equipment, including powerful hydro jetting machines that use high-pressure water to scour pipes, drain snakes with various cutting heads for root removal, and waterproof camera inspection systems to precisely diagnose issues and confirm thorough cleaning.

    In North Las Vegas, storm drains are often clogged by natural debris such as leaves, twigs, dirt, and silt, especially after heavy winds or storms. Other culprits include grass clippings, trash, sediment from construction, and sometimes even tree roots getting into older pipes. Most homes sit on concrete slabs, so leaks in buried supply lines often show up as warm spots on the floor or higher water bills.

    Hydro jetting is a powerful drain cleaning method that uses high-pressure water streams to blast away stubborn blockages and thoroughly clean the interior of drain pipes. For storm drains, it is highly effective at removing compacted soil, heavy silt, grease, and roots, restoring the drain to its full flow capacity much better than snaking.

    Our technicians are trained to service all types and brands of storm drain systems commonly found in residential and commercial properties. This includes standard PVC, corrugated HDPE, concrete, and cast iron drainpipes, as well as many types of catch basins and grates from manufacturers like NDS, ADS, and Zurn.

    To prevent storm drain clogs, regularly clear leaves and debris from grates and catch basins, avoid planting trees directly over drain lines, and keep landscaping in good shape to prevent soil erosion. Consider installing grates with smaller openings in high-debris areas and schedule annual professional cleanings.
