    Sump Pump Replacement in North Las Vegas, NV

    Sump pumps often sit idle for long stretches between storms, which can lead to stuck floats and seized motors. We replace old pumps with new models and inspect the pit, check valve and discharge line. Upgrading to a higher capacity pump may help if your old one could not keep up. We test the new pump by filling the pit with water. Replacement before storm season is a smart move.

    Sump Pump Installation Cost in North Las Vegas, NV

    A pump replacement in an existing pit usually costs much less than a new pit installation. New pits require slab cutting, excavation and concrete patching, which adds labor.
