    Floor-Mount and Wall-Hung Toilet Installation in North Las Vegas, NV

    Wall-Hung Carrier Systems in North Las Vegas

    In-wall carriers from Geberit and Grohe each mount a little differently, and the instructions matter. We follow the maker's frame height chart exactly. Set the frame too low and the bowl ends up at child height, too high and nobody's feet touch the floor, and neither can be fixed once the wall is tiled. Measure twice here.

    Wall-Hung Toilet Installation in North Las Vegas, NV

    Wall-hung toilets need a wall at least 2 by 6 deep, or a furred-out chase built in front of an existing 2 by 4 wall. Floors stay clear underneath. Cleaning gets easier, and the bowl height can be set anywhere within the carrier range, useful for a tall family. Service happens through the flush plate.

    ADA-Compliant Toilet Installation in North Las Vegas

    ADA toilets put the seat 17 to 19 inches off the floor, and the centerline sits 16 to 18 inches from the side wall. That spacing leaves room for a grab bar. Before anything is ordered we measure the drain, because shifting it to meet the ADA centerline means cutting into the floor. Elongated bowls are standard here.

    High-Efficiency Toilet Installation in North Las Vegas, NV

    High-efficiency toilets flush on 1.28 gallons or less, against 3.5 gallons or more for toilets from before the early 1990s. Look for the WaterSense label on the box. A MaP rating of 800 grams or better is what we look for, since a weak low-flow toilet that needs double flushing saves no water at all. Dual-flush models add a lighter half flush.

    Rough-In Toilet Installation in North Las Vegas, NV

    Rough-in is the plumbing that goes in before walls and floors close up. For a toilet that means a 3 inch drain with a vent, plus a cold supply stub. The drain goes 12 inches out from the finished wall line, which means we add the drywall thickness to the framing measurement. Then the inspector sees it open.

    Toilet Brands We Install in North Las Vegas

    We install Kohler, TOTO, American Standard, Gerber and most other brands sold at supply houses and home centers. Every brand has its quirks. Some skirted TOTO models use a Unifit adapter, and certain Kohler tanks take a gasket that hardware stores rarely stock. We keep those parts on hand.
