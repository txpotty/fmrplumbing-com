    Inlet and Outlet Baffle Replacement in North Las Vegas, NV

    Baffles hold back the scum layer on top and the sludge on the bottom so only clear effluent leaves the tank. Concrete ones get eaten by sulfuric acid from sewer gas and eventually fall off into the tank. Once the outlet baffle is gone, solids head straight for the field. We replace them with PVC sanitary tees. Usually a short job.

    Septic Repair Costs Compared in North Las Vegas, NV

    Baffle or tee replacement often runs $300 to $900. Leach field restoration or replacement can cost $5,000 to $20,000 or more. The cheapest fix that actually works is the one we push for.

    Septic Pump and Alarm Repair in North Las Vegas

    Pumped systems move effluent uphill or to a far field, and the pump sits in its own chamber with a float tree and an alarm. The alarm sounds because liquid climbed past the high float. Our tech meters the pump and tests each float before replacing anything. Zoeller and Liberty parts ride on the truck.

    Drain Field Repair in North Las Vegas, NV

    Green stripes over the trenches and a wet lawn in dry weather are the classic signs of a field in trouble. The cause is usually biomat, a black layer of slime that seals the soil. Compaction and hydraulic overload from a running toilet can do it too. Testing always comes first. Resting or jetting can bring a field back.

    Leach Field Restoration in North Las Vegas, NV

    Before we condemn a leach field, we open the distribution box and look at how evenly it splits the flow. A box that has settled or cracked can push all the effluent into a single trench. A new or leveled box brings a lot of fields back. Some fields are past saving. For those, we explain the design and permit steps.

    Effluent Pump Replacement in North Las Vegas, NV

    Effluent pumps in a dosing chamber last roughly 8 to 15 years, and they usually quit without much warning at all. A pump that hums but moves no water has a stuck impeller or a bad capacitor. Our tech pulls the old unit and checks the discharge before the new one goes in. Floats get replaced at the same time.
