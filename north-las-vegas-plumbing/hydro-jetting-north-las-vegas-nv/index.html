    Frequently Asked Questions About Hydro Jetting in North Las Vegas

    The cost of hydro jetting in North Las Vegas, NV usually ranges from $300 to $800, depending on the severity of the clog, the length and diameter of the pipe, and ease of access. More complex jobs, like main sewer line descaling or extensive root removal, may be on the higher end. We provide a clear quote after an initial inspection.

    Hydro jetting is highly effective against a wide range of stubborn clogs that traditional snaking cannot fully clear. This includes heavy grease buildup, mineral scale, sludge, soap scum, hair clogs, and even invasive tree roots that have gotten into your sewer lines. It is designed to scour the entire pipe surface.

    It is safe for most pipes in fair condition when pressure is set correctly. We camera the line first and avoid jetting pipe that is broken or collapsed.

    While hydro jetting effectively removes existing tree roots from your sewer line, it does not prevent new roots from growing back. For a long-term solution to recurring root intrusion, we might recommend trenchless sewer repair or pipe replacement, which we can discuss during our assessment.

    Key signs you might need hydro jetting include frequently recurring clogs in the same drain, multiple slow drains throughout your home, persistent foul odors coming from drains, or gurgling sounds from toilets or drains. These often point to a significant buildup or blockage that needs more than just a snake.

    For most homes in North Las Vegas, hydro jetting every 1 to 3 years can be a good preventative measure, especially if you have recurring drain issues or older pipes. Businesses, particularly restaurants, may benefit from quarterly or twice-yearly hydro jetting to manage grease buildup. We can recommend a schedule based on your needs.

    For most modern and well-maintained pipes, hydro jetting is very safe. However, for very old, corroded, or severely damaged pipes, the high pressure could cause further damage. That is why FMR Plumbing always performs a camera inspection first to check your pipe's condition and confirm hydro jetting is appropriate.

    Repeat clogs in the same line, slow drains after snaking, sewer odors and known grease or root problems are solid reasons to consider jetting.
