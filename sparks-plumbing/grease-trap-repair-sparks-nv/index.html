    Baffle Repair and Replacement in Sparks

    Inside a concrete gravity interceptor the baffle is usually a poured or precast wall with a crossover opening. When that wall spalls, grease slips through to the second compartment.     Grease Interceptor Clog Removal in Sparks

    Grease does not stay liquid for long once it cools in a pipe. Within a few feet of the sink it hardens like candle wax along the top of the pipe. Chemical drain openers soften it for a day and then it re-forms farther downstream, sometimes past the trap entirely, where it becomes a sewer problem instead of a kitchen problem. A jetter actually takes it out of the line.

    Grease Trap Drain Line Repair in Sparks

    Commercial Grease Interceptor Repair in Sparks, NV

    In-ground gravity interceptors run from about 500 gallons to several thousand, usually precast concrete with two compartments. Nobody goes in without a gas monitor, a tripod and a second person topside.
