    Frequently Asked Questions About Grease Trap Installation in Sparks

    The cost of grease trap installation in Sparks, NV varies significantly based on the size, type, and complexity of the system. Small under-sink units might range from $1,000 to $3,000, while large outdoor interceptors requiring excavation can cost anywhere from $5,000 to $20,000 or more. We provide transparent, upfront pricing after a thorough on-site assessment.

    The lifespan of a grease trap depends on its type, material, and how well it's maintained. Steel grease traps may last 10 to 15 years, while durable fiberglass or polyethylene units can last 20 to 30 years or even longer. Regular cleaning and proper usage are key to lengthening its lifespan.

    An indoor under-sink trap can often be installed in a day. Outdoor interceptors usually take several days to a week or more, depending on excavation, concrete work and inspection scheduling.

    We install all common types of grease traps, including gravity grease interceptors, hydro-mechanical grease traps (automatic grease removal units), and compact under-sink grease traps. Our team helps you determine the best system for your commercial kitchen's specific needs and local code requirements.

    Signs you might need a new grease trap include frequent drain clogs, slow drainage in multiple fixtures, persistent foul odors coming from drains, or if your current unit is old, corroded, or no longer meeting local code requirements. An inspection by a professional plumber can confirm the need for replacement.

    Indoor traps should be close to the fixtures they serve and easy to open for cleaning. Outdoor interceptors are typically placed where a pump truck can reach them without blocking customer traffic.

    Manufacturer warranties vary, with many polyethylene units carrying long structural warranties. We also guarantee our installation workmanship and connections.

    The timeline for grease trap installation depends on the project's scope. A simple under-sink unit might take a few hours. Larger outdoor gravity interceptors, which involve excavation and more extensive plumbing, can take anywhere from one to several days. We always aim to limit disruption to your business operations.
