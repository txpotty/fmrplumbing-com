    Grease Trap Repair in Sparks, NV

    Cracked lids, missing baffles and rusted inlet tees all let grease pass straight through a trap. We inspect each part during cleaning and replace what has failed, usually on the same visit.     Burst Supply Lines in Sparks, NV

    Supply lines with plastic coupling nuts split more often than braided stainless, especially after years of pressure swings.     Grease Trap Cleaning in Sparks

    A grease trap that smells sour is usually past due for cleaning.     Recurring Kitchen Clogs in Sparks, NV

    Grease, rice, pasta and coffee grounds build up in the trap and branch line over months, not days.     InSinkErator Models We Service in Sparks

    Badger units are the basic line, with a single grind stage and a 1/3 to 3/4 horsepower motor.     Disposal Jams and Hums in Sparks, NV

    Moen, Delta, Kohler, American Standard and Pfister faucets all get installed and repaired by our plumbers, along with InSinkErator, Waste King and Moen disposals and most other supply house brands.     Restaurants typically clean every one to three months, though a busy fryer kitchen may need it every few weeks. Homes rarely need one. A common rule is to pump it before grease and solids take up about 25 percent of the trap, and some sewer districts set the interval for you.

    Tightening a trap is easy enough to do yourself. Leave disposal wiring, dishwasher drains and grease traps to a pro, because errors there cause leaks and code problems.

    Watch for slow drains, a sour smell from the sink, gurgling when the dishwasher drains, or a disposal that hums but will not grind. Check the cabinet floor too. A warped or stained cabinet base means a slow leak has been going for a while.
