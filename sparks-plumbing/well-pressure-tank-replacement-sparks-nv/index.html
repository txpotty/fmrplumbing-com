    What Goes Into a Well Tank Swap in Sparks, NV

    Flex-Lite Pressure Tank Replacement in Sparks

    Flex-Lite tanks have a composite shell. They will not rust. That makes them a good pick for damp basements, crawlspaces and well houses where steel tanks tend to corrode from the bottom up, and they are lighter to carry into tight spaces. Same plumbing as steel.

    Pressure Switch and Gauge Integration in Sparks, NV

    The pressure switch tells the pump when to start and stop. It works with the tank, not apart from it. A switch set at 40/60 needs a tank pre-charged to 38 psi, and a gauge that reads correctly so you can see what the system is doing, so we replace a worn gauge rather than trusting a sticky needle. Small parts, big role.

    Preventative Well Pressure Tank Maintenance in Sparks, NV

    Check the air charge once a year. Shut off the pump, drain the system to zero, and put a tire gauge on the valve at the top of the tank, then add air with a small compressor if it has dropped. Water out of the valve means a torn bladder.

    Bladder-Style Pressure Tank Replacement in Sparks, NV

    A bladder tank holds water inside a rubber balloon surrounded by compressed air. The air pushes water out when you open a tap. Bladders crack with age, especially if the tank ran with low pre-charge, which waterlogs the tank. Replacement is the fix.

    Whole-House Tank Replacement on Older Wells in Sparks

    Older wells sometimes have a tank, switch and plumbing that were all installed together decades ago. Replacing just the tank can expose other weak spots. We look at the whole tank area, from the check valve to the house shutoff, and give you options for doing the tank alone or the tank plus its fittings. Your call.

    Diaphragm Pressure Tank Replacement in Sparks, NV

    Diaphragm tanks use a flexible sheet clamped inside the shell. Air sits above it, water below. When it fails, symptoms match a bladder tank, including fast cycling and water at the Schrader valve. Replacement is the usual answer.

    Tank Tee, Gauge and Relief Valve Replacement in Sparks

    The tank tee is the brass manifold that connects tank, switch, gauge, drain and relief valve. Old ones leak at threads. The relief valve should open before the system reaches a dangerous pressure, and a corroded valve may not open when needed. Whole set, one visit.

    Amtrol Pressure Tank Replacement in Sparks

    Replacing an Amtrol tank with another Amtrol is often straightforward. Fittings tend to line up. Even so, we confirm the model is still the right size for your pump, since many original tanks were undersized from the start. Check before swapping.
