    Frequently Asked Questions About Bathtub Faucet Replacement in Sparks

    Most tub faucet replacements run $250 to $600 with a standard faucet. Trim and cartridge work costs the least, and full valve replacement behind tile costs the most. You get a written estimate first.

    Water in much of Nevada is very hard, and scale buildup is one of the most common reasons water heaters and fixtures fail early. Calcium and magnesium build up on cartridge seals and inside the spout diverter. That buildup makes handles stiff, wears the rubber seals faster than normal, and eventually causes drips or a diverter that will not lift, which shortens the life of the whole valve. Regular cleaning slows it down.

    A drip that comes back after a new cartridge is the big one. Watch for stiff or spinning handles, temperature swings, and crust that keeps coming back. Any two point toward a new valve.
