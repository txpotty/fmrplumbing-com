    Softener Not Regenerating in Sparks

    If hard water symptoms return, the softener may not be regenerating. Causes include a failed timer motor, a bad circuit board, a stuck meter turbine or incorrect settings after a power outage. We test each component, replace the failed part and program the valve for your household size and hardness. You get soft water again by the next cycle.

    Water Softener Repair vs. Replacement in Sparks, NV

    Deciding whether to repair or replace your water softener can be a tough choice. FMR Plumbing provides honest, expert advice based on the age of your system, the extent of the damage, and the cost-effectiveness of repairs. We'll give you a plain breakdown of options, helping you make the best decision for your budget and long-term water quality needs in Sparks, NV.

    Salt Usage and Settings Repair in Sparks

    A softener using far too much salt or water usually has settings that do not match your hardness or household. Older timer units may regenerate on a schedule regardless of use. We retest hardness, reprogram the valve or upgrade the head to a metered control. Proper settings can significantly cut salt and water use.

    Resin Bed Replacement & Regeneration Issues in Sparks

    Over time, the resin beads inside your water softener can become fouled, damaged, or depleted, reducing their ability to remove hardness minerals. If your system is regenerating but still delivering hard water, the resin bed may be the culprit. We can test the resin, flush the system to remove iron or sediment buildup, or perform a complete resin replacement. This process restores your softener's core function, ensuring effective hard water treatment for years to come.

    Injector and Brine Line Cleaning in Sparks, NV

    The injector, sometimes called a venturi, creates suction to draw brine into the resin tank. Sediment and salt residue clog its tiny ports. When it clogs, the brine tank fills with water instead of emptying. We clean or replace the injector and screen and check the brine line for leaks.

    Salt Bridge and Brine Tank Repair in Sparks, NV

    A salt bridge is a hard crust that forms in the brine tank and keeps salt from dissolving. The tank looks full, but the softener cannot make brine. We break up bridges, clean out mushy salt, inspect the float and brine well and repair the safety valve. We also recommend salt types that bridge less in warm or humid spaces.

    Salt Bridge & Brine Tank Repair in Sparks

    A common issue with salt-based water softeners is a salt bridge forming in the brine tank, preventing the salt from dissolving properly. This leads to poor regeneration and hard water. Our technicians are skilled at breaking up these bridges, cleaning the brine tank, and making sure the float switch and brine well are functioning correctly. We also inspect for any leaks or cracks in the tank itself that could be compromising your system's performance, offering targeted repairs or replacements.

    Water Softener Leak Repair in Sparks, NV

    Leaks in a water softener can range from minor drips around fittings to significant puddles from a cracked tank or faulty valve. These leaks can cause water damage and waste water. Our plumbers promptly pinpoint the source of leaks, whether from seals, O-rings, hoses, or internal components, and perform durable repairs.
