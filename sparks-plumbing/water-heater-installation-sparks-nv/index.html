    Water Heater Expansion Tank Installation in Sparks

    Many plumbing codes require the installation of an expansion tank with a new water heater, especially in closed-loop systems. Plumbing contractors must be licensed by the state contractors board, and the state treats unlicensed work seriously. An expansion tank protects your water heater and plumbing system from excessive pressure buildup, which can be caused by the heating process. Our installation service includes correctly sizing and installing the expansion tank to support proper system operation and prevent early wear on your new water heater and other plumbing fixtures in Sparks. This small component offers real long-term protection.
