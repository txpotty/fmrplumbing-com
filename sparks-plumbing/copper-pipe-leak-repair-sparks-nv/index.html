    Frequently Asked Questions About Copper Pipe Leak Repair in Sparks

    The cost of copper pipe leak repair in Sparks can vary a lot depending on the leak's location, severity and accessibility. Minor repairs, like a simple solder patch, might range from $200 to $500. More complex issues, such as a slab leak requiring specialized detection and repair, could range from $800 to $3,000 or more. We provide a detailed estimate after our initial inspection.

    Copper pipe leaks are commonly caused by corrosion, especially from hard or acidic water. Water in much of Nevada is very hard, and scale buildup is one of the most common reasons water heaters and fixtures fail early. Other causes include excessive water pressure, improper installation, shifting foundations putting stress on joints and freezing temperatures that can cause pipes to burst. Pinhole leaks are a common symptom of internal corrosion.

    Most accessible repairs take one to two hours. Leaks inside walls or ceilings can take two to four hours, and slab leak repairs may take a full day.

    Signs of a hidden copper pipe leak include unexplained increases in your water bill, damp spots on walls or ceilings, musty odors, the sound of running water when no fixtures are in use or visible changes in your home's foundation or landscaping. Don't ignore these indicators.

    Type K, L and M copper pipes differ mainly in their wall thickness and thus their pressure ratings. Type K has the thickest walls and is used for underground service and main water lines. Type L is common for interior water supply and radiant heating. Type M has the thinnest walls and is typically used for less demanding applications. We are skilled in repairing all types.

    Common causes include aggressive water chemistry, high water velocity, excessive pressure, poor original soldering and stray electrical current from grounding issues. These factors erode the pipe wall from the inside over time.

    We usually use Type L copper, which has thicker walls than Type M and lasts longer. In some situations we use ProPress fittings or a PEX transition.

    If you discover a copper pipe leak, the first step is to locate and shut off the main water supply to your home to prevent further water damage.
