    Kitec and Polybutylene Replacement in Sparks

    Polybutylene Plumbing Replacement in Sparks, NV

    Polybutylene, a gray or blue plastic pipe installed in many homes from the late 1970s into the mid 1990s, breaks down with exposure to chlorine and other disinfectants in tap water. It fails without warning. The pipe gets brittle from the inside, and fittings crack, so a home that has had one polybutylene leak is usually at risk everywhere else in the system. Full replacement is the fix. Insurers often ask about it at renewal.

    Copper Repiping in Sparks, NV

    Some homeowners prefer copper for its long track record and rigidity. Most copper jobs run a day longer than PEX, since each turn needs its own fitting.
