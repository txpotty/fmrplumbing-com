    Tank Leaks and Pressure Problems in Henderson

    Even gas water heaters can develop tank leaks, often due to corrosion from hard water or high water pressure. Mineral deposits crust over aerators, showerheads and valves, which is why softeners and regular water heater flushing are popular. While some leaks may call for a replacement, minor leaks or pressure relief valve issues can often be repaired. Our team checks the extent of the damage, inspects the anode rod and addresses pressure imbalances to prevent further problems in your Henderson home.

    Pilot Light Repair in Henderson

    A pilot that will not light or will not stay lit is the most frequent gas water heater call. Causes include a worn thermocouple, a clogged pilot orifice, a bad piezo igniter or a tripped thermal cutoff. We test the thermocouple output with a meter and clean or replace the pilot assembly. Most pilot repairs are completed in about an hour.

    Pilot Light and Ignition System Repair in Henderson

    A frequent culprit for no hot water in gas heaters is a faulty pilot light or ignition system. Our technicians are skilled at diagnosing issues like a dirty pilot tube, thermocouple failure or a bad igniter.
