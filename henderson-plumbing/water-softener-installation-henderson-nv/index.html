    Salt-Based Water Softener Systems in Henderson, NV

    Salt-based softeners use ion exchange, swapping calcium and magnesium for sodium on a bed of resin beads. FMR Plumbing installs these with a properly sized resin tank and brine tank. We program regeneration by metered water use so the system only recharges when the resin is actually spent, which saves a lot of salt. Most effective option.

    Ecodyne Water Softener Installation in Henderson

    Ecodyne softeners are sold at many home stores, and we install them for homeowners who already bought one. Install follows the manufacturer manual for bypass, drain and overflow, then we set hardness and regeneration. Bring your own unit. We set it up for you.

    Restaurants, hotels and laundromats feel hard water in their equipment first. We install bigger tanks or twin alternating units for businesses, which keeps soft water flowing during regeneration. Dishwashers and boilers last longer. We schedule around your hours.
