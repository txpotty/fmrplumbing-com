    Frequently Asked Questions About Faucet Repair in Henderson

    The cost of faucet repair in Henderson can range from $120 to $350, depending on the type of faucet, the specific parts needed, and the difficulty of the repair. A simple washer replacement will be on the lower end, while a cartridge replacement in a high-end faucet might be more. We provide a written estimate before any work begins.

    Water in much of Nevada is very hard, and scale buildup is one of the most common reasons water heaters and fixtures fail early. Hard water contains high levels of minerals like calcium and magnesium. Over time, these minerals can build up inside your faucets, corroding internal parts, clogging aerators, and stiffening moving components. This often leads to leaks, reduced water pressure, and a shorter faucet lifespan, making regular maintenance or timely repair important.

    Faucet repair involves fixing the existing fixture by replacing specific worn or broken internal components like washers, O-rings, or cartridges. Faucet replacement means removing the old faucet entirely and installing a brand new one. We recommend replacement when a faucet is severely damaged, very old, or if you simply want an upgrade.

    While some minor leaks, like a simple washer replacement on a compression faucet, can be a DIY project, most modern faucets involve specific tools and delicate parts like cartridges or ceramic discs. Incorrect repairs can lead to further damage, more significant leaks, or voided warranties. It's often best to call a professional for a proper and lasting fix.

    Most repairs take 30 to 60 minutes. If a special part must be ordered, we might need a second short visit.

    Many brands, including Moen and Delta, offer limited lifetime warranties on cartridges and finishes for the original owner. We can help you check whether parts might be covered.

    Generally, simple faucet repairs like replacing internal components, handles, or the entire faucet (if it's a like-for-like replacement) do not require a permit in Henderson. However, if the repair involves altering the existing plumbing lines or significantly changing the fixture type, a permit might be necessary. Our plumbers know local codes and will advise you if a permit is required.

    Our plumbers are skilled at repairing all major faucet brands commonly found in Nevada homes, including Delta, Moen, Kohler, American Standard, Pfister, and many others. We keep a stock of universal and common brand-specific parts to speed up repairs.
