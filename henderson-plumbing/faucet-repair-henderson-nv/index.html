    Common Faucet Repairs in Henderson, NV

    Compression Faucet Repair in Henderson

    Two-handle faucets with separate hot and cold handles are often compression faucets, which seal by pressing a rubber washer down onto a brass seat inside the valve. Washers harden and seats get pitted. We replace the washer and dress or replace the seat with a seat wrench, and the packing gets redone if the stem leaks.

    Specialty Faucet Repair in Henderson, NV

    Pot fillers, laundry tub faucets and wall-mount utility faucets each have their own valve styles, and many use compression stems or quarter-turn cartridges that hardware stores do not stock. We bring the common ones. Filtered water faucets at the kitchen sink get new cartridges or check valves as needed.

    Commercial Faucet Repair in Henderson, NV

    Restaurant and office faucets cycle hundreds of times a day, so cartridges, handle springs, metering valves and sensor solenoids wear out far faster than they do in a house. Commercial cartridges are on the truck. We set metering faucets to run long enough for a full rinse without wasting water, and we can work after hours.

    Ball Faucet Repair in Henderson, NV

    Ball faucets have a single lever over a domed cap, and the drip usually comes from the two small rubber seats and springs that press against the ball. A seats and springs kit fixes most of them. Loose handles happen too. If the handle leaks, snugging the adjusting ring under the cap often fixes it.

    Disc Faucet Repair in Henderson

    Ceramic disc faucets last a long time, but grit from a water main repair or scale from hard water can scratch the discs or wear out the rubber inlet seals underneath. Inlet seals cost very little. A scratched disc means a new cartridge, since the two discs are lapped to fit each other and cannot be resurfaced.

    Outdoor Hose Bib Repair in Henderson

    Outdoor spigots take a beating from sun and weather. Freezing weather is uncommon in the southern desert of Nevada, though occasional cold nights can still damage exposed outdoor pipes. Packing and seat washers get replaced on standard hose bibs, and we install frost-free sillcocks with the valve seat inside the warm wall when an old one has split. Every new spigot gets a vacuum breaker for backflow protection.

    Pull-Down Kitchen Faucet Repair in Henderson

    Pull-down kitchen faucets wear at the hose, the spray head and the docking magnet or counterweight that pulls the hose back into the spout. The hose usually splits near the quick-connect fitting. Brand-matched hoses, heads and weights go in, then check for drips under the sink where the hose connects.

    Hard Water Faucet Repair in Henderson

    Scale is the biggest reason faucet seals wear early, because the mineral crust scratches rubber and keeps ceramic discs from closing flat. Hard water speeds it up. The valve body gets cleaned while the old part is out, so the new cartridge or washer seals against a smooth surface.
