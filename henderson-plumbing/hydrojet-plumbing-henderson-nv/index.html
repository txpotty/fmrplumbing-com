    Main Sewer Line Hydrojetting in Henderson

    The main sewer carries every drain in the house, so a blockage there backs up the lowest fixture first. Jetting the main clears roots, grease and sludge all the way to the city connection. On a 4-inch line we use a heavier nozzle with more flow, which moves the loosened debris downstream instead of leaving it to settle in a low spot.

    Emergency Hydrojet Services in Henderson, NV

    A full sewer backup needs a truck today. First, we open the line. A cable opens it, then the jetter removes the grease, roots or wipes that caused it.

    Grease Trap Line Cleaning in Henderson, NV

    Restaurant kitchen lines send warm grease downstream where it cools and sticks. Much of it coats the pipe before it ever reaches the interceptor. We jet the line from the kitchen to the interceptor in short passes, which keeps loosened grease from plugging the pipe farther down, and we coordinate with your pump-out company. Quarterly works for most.

    Hard Water Scale Removal in Henderson, NV

    Scale builds slowly inside drain pipe, narrowing it a little more each year. Water in much of Nevada is very hard, and scale buildup is one of the most common reasons water heaters and fixtures fail early. A jetter with a scouring nozzle strips that scale from the walls, and on heavily scaled cast iron we may follow up with a descaling chain head to finish the job. You can see the change on video.

    Hydrojetting vs Snaking Cost Comparison in Henderson, NV

    Snaking a main line typically costs $200 to $450, while residential hydrojetting usually runs $400 to $900 with a camera inspection. Jetting costs more up front. Because jetting removes the buildup that causes repeat clogs, many homeowners spend less over a few years than they would on repeated snaking visits for the same line.
