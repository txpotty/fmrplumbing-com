    Some homeowners dig their own trench, but connecting to the meter, sizing the pipe and passing inspection are best handled by a licensed plumber. A poor joint underground can leak for months before anyone notices.

    Most residential main water line installations or replacements in Henderson can be completed within one to three days. This timeline can vary based on the length of the line, the complexity of the excavation, weather and permit processing times.

    A typical residential water line takes one to two days, plus time for utility locates and inspection. Rocky ground or long runs can add a day or two.

    For most homes, PEX and copper are generally the best materials. PEX is flexible, corrosion-resistant and handles minor ground shifts well.
