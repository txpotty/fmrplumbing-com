    How We Install Faucets in Henderson, NV

    Wall-Mount Faucet Installation in Henderson, NV

    Wall-mount faucets start before the drywall closes. The rough-in valve gets set at a height and depth that suit your sink and the finished thickness of the tile, so the spout lands where it should. Trim goes on after tile. You get a clear deck with no faucet base to wipe around.

    Commercial and ADA Faucets in Henderson, NV

    ADA rules set faucet reach and handle force. Handles have to work with one hand and no tight grip, which is why lever and sensor faucets dominate commercial restrooms. Installs happen before you open or after you close. Fixtures go in with the spec sheet left for the inspector.

    Outdoor Faucet and Hose Bib Installation in Henderson

    Hose bibs get more abuse than any indoor faucet. Freezing weather is uncommon in the southern desert of Nevada, though occasional cold nights can still damage exposed outdoor pipes. We match the hose bib to that, using a frost-free sillcock with its seat set back inside the heated wall wherever freezing is a real risk. Every hose bib needs a vacuum breaker under code. Most frost-free models include one, so the backflow protection is built right into the body.

    Kitchen Faucet Installation in Henderson

    Pull-down sprayers get yanked a thousand times a year. That is why we clip the hose weight at the right spot on the hose and check that the hose does not snag on the shutoffs or the disposal when it pulls back, which is where most early kitchen leaks start. We connect new braided lines to both stops. Last thing, we run hot and cold for a few minutes and wipe the cabinet floor with a dry towel to look for any drip.

    Restaurant and Office Faucets in Henderson, NV

    Restaurant pre-rinse units mount to the wall and the sink deck. We bolt the wall bracket into solid backing and fit check valves on both supplies, since a pre-rinse unit gets pulled hard all shift. Hand-wash sinks get wrist blades. Night installs keep the kitchen open.

    Kitchen Faucet Swaps in Henderson

    Removing the old kitchen faucet is often the slow part. Rusted mounting nuts behind a double sink sometimes need cutting, which beats prying on a cast iron bowl until it chips. Then the deck gets scraped clean. We mount the new faucet, connect fresh supplies and make sure the sprayer hose clears the disposal.

    Touchless Faucet Installation in Henderson

    Touchless faucets run on a solenoid valve. We mount the control box high on the cabinet wall, away from where water collects, and power it with batteries or a plug-in adapter. Scale is hard on solenoids, and we install the inline filter when the maker calls for one. We test the sensor range last.

    Pot Filler Installation in Henderson

    A pot filler needs its own cold line. We tap a cold supply, run it up the wall behind the range and set a shutoff where you can reach it, which code and common sense both want. The arm folds flat when not in use. We pressure-test the line before the backsplash goes up.
