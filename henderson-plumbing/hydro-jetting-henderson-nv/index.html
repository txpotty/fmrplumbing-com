    Frequently Asked Questions About Hydro Jetting in Henderson

    Residential jetting in Henderson usually costs $350 to $800. Commercial jobs often bill hourly. The final number depends on how long the line is, how easy it is to reach and how much buildup is inside.

    Residential drains: 1 to 2 hours. Mains take longer. A major sewer line or a commercial job can take 3 to 4 hours or more, depending on how severe the blockage is and where it sits in the line.

    Small rental jetters exist. They lack real power. A pro unit has much more pressure and flow, and running one without training risks injury and pipe damage.

    Buildup responds best. Coatings on the pipe wall. Soap scum, hair mats and fine roots that have worked into the sewer also come out under high pressure, since the jet scours the entire pipe surface instead of just one path.

    Small consumer jetters exist, but they lack the pressure and flow of professional units. Untrained use can hurt people and pipes.

    Sound PVC, cast iron and concrete handle it fine. Damaged pipe does not. We always check pipe condition on camera first, so we never jet a line that cannot take the pressure.

    Most residential jobs take 1 to 3 hours including inspection. Long commercial lines or heavy grease can add time.

    Snaking punches a hole. Jetting cleans the pipe. Snaking restores some flow, but jetting removes the buildup entirely and keeps the line clear longer.
