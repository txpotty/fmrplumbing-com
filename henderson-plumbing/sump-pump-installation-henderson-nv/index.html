    Battery Backup Sump Pump Installation in Henderson, NV

    Storms knock out power at the exact moment the pit is filling fastest. A battery backup pump sits beside the primary and runs on its own float. A good battery carries it through most outages. We mount the charger on the wall, off the floor.

    Exterior Drainage and Yard Sump Pumps in Henderson

    Sunken patios, below-grade driveways and deep window wells collect water with nowhere to go. For those areas we set an exterior basin with a submersible pump and run the discharge to a safe point, often a pop-up emitter in the yard. Slopes toward the house are the usual cause.     Sump Pump Replacement in Henderson, NV

    Floats stick and motors seize when a pump sits idle for months between storms. We pull the old pump and inspect the pit. If the old one could not keep up, we size up. Testing is done with a full pit.

    Combination Sump Pump System Installation in Henderson, NV

    Two pumps on one riser cover both kinds of failure, a dead motor and a dead outlet. The primary handles daily water. People with carpet downstairs buy these. We install them in homes where a flood would ruin flooring and drywall.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Systems in Henderson, NV

    Backup systems we install use sealed AGM batteries that do not need water. They matter most below finished rooms.

    Emergency Sump Pump Replacement in Henderson, NV

    A dead pump during a storm means the water is already climbing. We carry common pumps on the truck. Figure on two hours for a typical swap. We test it with a full pit before leaving.

    Combination Sump Pump Systems in Henderson

    Combination units pair a primary pump and a battery backup on one pre-plumbed discharge assembly. They fit pits where two separate pumps would crowd each other. Install time is shorter too. They suit homes that want backup protection but do not want to enlarge the pit.
