    Durable Pipe Replacement Services in Henderson, NV

    Polybutylene Pipe Replacement in Henderson

    Gray polybutylene pipe was installed in many homes from the late 1970s into the mid-1990s. It breaks down from the inside when exposed to chlorinated water, and fittings can crack with no warning. Some insurers raise premiums or decline coverage on homes that still have it. We remove polybutylene supply lines and replace them with PEX-A or copper. A completed repipe can also help when listing the home.

    PEX Repiping in Henderson

    PEX-A is flexible, resists scale buildup and expands slightly if water freezes, which helps in colder homes. We use expansion fittings that keep the full inside diameter of the pipe for stronger flow. Systems can be run as a home-run manifold with a shutoff for each fixture or as a trunk and branch layout. PEX installs faster than copper with many fewer fittings in the walls. It is our most requested repipe material.

    Whole-Home Repiping in Henderson

    If your home in Henderson is older and experiencing frequent leaks, low water pressure throughout, or discolored water from multiple fixtures, a whole-home repiping might be the most sensible long-term solution. We can replace all the old galvanized steel or polybutylene pipes with modern, durable PEX or copper piping. This upgrades your entire water delivery system, greatly improving water quality and pressure while eliminating future leak concerns from aging infrastructure.

    Copper Pipe Replacement in Henderson, NV

    Copper piping is a long-standing, reliable choice known for its durability and resistance to corrosion. While it's a premium material, its longevity often makes it a cost-effective solution over time. FMR Plumbing offers expert copper pipe replacement for sections or entire homes in Henderson, NV. Our plumbers are skilled in the precise soldering and installation techniques required for copper, ensuring a leak-proof and robust plumbing system that holds up year after year.

    PEX Pipe Replacement in Henderson

    PEX (cross-linked polyethylene) piping has become a common choice for residential pipe replacement due to its flexibility, affordability, and resistance to freezing. Freezing weather is uncommon in the southern desert of Nevada, though occasional cold nights can still damage exposed outdoor pipes. Unlike rigid pipes, PEX can expand, reducing the risk of burst pipes during sudden cold snaps. FMR Plumbing specializes in PEX installation, which often allows for less invasive replacement methods compared to traditional materials. This modern solution offers excellent performance and water quality for your home.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Pipe Replacement in Henderson

    A whole-home PEX repipe for a typical single-story home generally costs $5,000 to $12,000, while copper repipes and larger homes can run $10,000 to $18,000. Replacing a single section or branch line often costs $500 to $3,000. We give a written price after inspection.

    We use well-known, high-quality brands for all our pipe replacement projects. For PEX, we often use products from Uponor (Wirsbo) or Viega, known for their reliability. For copper, we source from reputable manufacturers like Mueller Streamline. Our fittings are always top-grade brass or approved PEX crimp or expansion fittings to ensure tight connections in your Henderson home.

    We primarily use high-quality PEX (cross-linked polyethylene) and copper piping for replacements. PEX is flexible, corrosion-resistant, and good for freeze protection, while copper is highly durable, long-lasting, and offers excellent water quality. We will discuss the best option for your home's specific needs and budget.

    Modern PEX pipes typically last 25 to 50 years, and copper pipes can last 50 to 70 years or even longer, depending on water quality and installation. This is a major improvement over older materials like galvanized steel, which often fail within 20 to 50 years.

    We install PEX-A from brands like Uponor and Rehau, plus Type L copper. Both are code approved and made to last for decades.

    PEX is more flexible, resists scale and does not develop pinhole leaks. Copper is rigid, heat tolerant and favored by some homeowners, so we price both.

    Common signs include frequent leaks, low water pressure, discolored or foul-smelling water, banging noises in the walls, and visible corrosion on exposed pipes. If you notice any of these issues in your Henderson home, it's wise to have your plumbing system inspected.
