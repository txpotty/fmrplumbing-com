    CSST Repair and Bonding in Henderson, NV

    Newer homes often have corrugated stainless steel tubing running through the attic. Nails, rodents and lightning can all damage CSST, and older yellow-jacket tubing must be bonded to the home's grounding system to lower arc risk. It takes about an hour. We repair damaged sections with manufacturer-approved fittings and verify bonding before we leave. Snowmelt in the northern mountains can raise groundwater in spring, which affects basements and low-lying properties.

    Gas Appliance Connector Repair in Henderson, NV

    Weak flames or a smell near one appliance often point to the connector or shutoff behind it. Uncoated brass connectors from decades ago crack at the flare fittings and should be replaced on sight. We replace them with coated stainless connectors. Each appliance also gets a shutoff you can reach and a sediment trap where required, and we check that the connector length and diameter match the appliance rating.

    Propane Gas Line Service in Henderson, NV

    Propane systems run at different pressures than natural gas and use a two-stage regulator setup between the tank and the house. Regulators fail too. Our work covers propane yard lines, regulator replacement and fitting repairs, plus a check that every appliance has its propane orifices installed. Wrong orifices make a burner run dirty and unsafe. The test procedure is the same as for natural gas.

    Gas Permits and Inspections in Henderson

    Repairs that change piping, and all new gas lines, usually need a permit and inspection from Henderson building officials.     Gas Appliance Line Installation in Henderson, NV

    A new range, dryer or water heater needs a gas line sized for its BTU rating and the length of the run. Undersized lines starve burners. We install the branch line, add an accessible shutoff valve and a sediment trap where the code calls for one, then connect the appliance with a new coated flexible connector. If the existing appliance line is leaking or damaged, we fix it on the same visit.
