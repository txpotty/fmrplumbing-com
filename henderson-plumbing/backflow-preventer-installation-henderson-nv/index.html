    Reduced Pressure Zone (RPZ) Assembly Installation in Henderson

    Two independent checks and a relief valve make up an RPZ. If the zone between the checks loses its pressure margin, the relief valve opens, dumping water to the floor or a drain so nothing can pass upstream, which is why we never bury one in a pit in Henderson. We install Watts and Febco units.

    Reduced Pressure Zone (RPZ) Device Installation in Henderson, NV

    High-hazard connections in Henderson, NV call for a reduced pressure zone assembly. Think chemical injection or medical equipment, where a backflow event could put something toxic in the supply, so the RPZ adds a relief valve between two checks that dumps water if either check leaks.
