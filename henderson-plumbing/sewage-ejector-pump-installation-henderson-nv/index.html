    Frequently Asked Questions About Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Henderson

    The cost of sewage ejector pump installation in Henderson, NV usually ranges from $2,500 to $6,000, depending on pump type, basin size, excavation needs and whether new electrical circuits are required.     We recommend having your sewage ejector pump inspected annually, especially if it is in a high-use area. Regular inspections can catch potential issues early, extend the life of your pump and prevent unexpected failures and costly backups. This is particularly important for older units.

    Signs that you might need a new sewage ejector pump include unusual noises from the pump or pit, frequent cycling on and off, slow drainage from basement fixtures or a sewage odor coming from the area. If you notice any of these issues, it is best to have a professional inspect your system to prevent a complete failure and costly backups.

    A standard sewage ejector pump installation usually takes one to two days. This includes excavating the pit, setting the basin and pump, connecting the plumbing and completing the electrical hookups. More complex installations, like those requiring significant trenching or electrical panel upgrades, may take longer.

    New ejector installations usually require a plumbing permit and inspection. Straight pump replacements may not, and we confirm local requirements for every job.
