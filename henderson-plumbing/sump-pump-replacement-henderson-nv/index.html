    Combination Sump Pump System Replacement in Henderson, NV

    Combination units put a primary AC pump and a DC backup on one discharge manifold, so you get two pumps and two floats in a single assembly. They are handy when the basin is too small for two pumps side by side. We replace the whole assembly or just the failed side.

    Emergency Sump Pump Replacement in Henderson, NV

    Pit filling, pump not running? Call (725) 360-1766 and we will send someone.     Light Commercial Sump Pump Replacement in Henderson, NV

    Light commercial work, think retail basements and small office buildings, usually means a 1/2 HP or larger cast iron pump with a 2-inch discharge and a panel alarm. We replace like for like or upsize. Grinder and ejector pumps are a separate job.

    High-Capacity Sump Pump Upgrades in Henderson

    If your pump runs every 30 seconds during a storm and still loses ground, it is undersized for the water coming in. We time the cycles and measure how fast the pit refills. The rest is simple arithmetic. A half-horse or three-quarter-horse pump with a higher gallons-per-minute rating at your lift height may be the answer, sometimes paired with a 2-inch discharge.

    Submersible Sump Pump Replacement in Henderson

    A submersible pump sits at the bottom of the pit, sealed in an oil-filled housing, and it runs quieter than anything else you can put down there. Out comes the old pump, out comes the muck at the bottom of the basin, and the new one goes in on a brick to stay clear of silt. The check valve gets replaced at the same time. A worn valve lets water fall back and makes the pump run twice as often.

    Commercial Sump Pump Replacement in Henderson

    Commercial sumps run harder than residential ones, and a restaurant or office basement often has a duplex system with two pumps that alternate on a controller. We replace the pumps and the alternating relay. Work can be scheduled early or after closing so nobody is stepping around an open basin.

    Wayne Sump Pump Replacement in Henderson

    Wayne makes everything from a thermoplastic quarter-horse pump at the hardware store to cast iron models with vertical switches, and the cheap ones are what we replace most. The usual failure is a burned-out motor or a float that hangs up on the cord. You get a matching Wayne or a sturdier cast iron model, your call. Figure on a couple of hours.

    Elevator and Garage Pit Pumps in Henderson

    Elevator pits and parking garage sumps often require oil-sensing controls that stop the pump from sending hydraulic fluid or motor oil into the storm drain. Pumps and oil-sensing panels get replaced to code. Two alternating pumps on one controller mean one can fail without flooding the pit. Some insurance carriers ask for test records.
