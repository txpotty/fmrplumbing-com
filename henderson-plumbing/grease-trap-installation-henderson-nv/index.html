    Grease Trap Sizing and Placement in Henderson, NV

    Correct sizing and smart placement are critical for a grease trap's effectiveness. Installing a unit that is too small will lead to frequent clogs and overflow, while an oversized one can be an unnecessary expense. Our plumbers meticulously calculate the required capacity based on your kitchen's water flow, fixture count, and operating hours, ensuring optimal performance for your Henderson, NV business.

    Large Outdoor Grease Interceptor Installation in Henderson

    For high-volume commercial kitchens in Henderson, large outdoor grease interceptors are often required by code. These substantial units are installed underground and designed to manage significant amounts of FOG before wastewater enters the municipal sewer system. We handle the entire process, including excavation, precise placement, connection, and final inspection, delivering a robust and compliant installation.

    Hydro-Mechanical Grease Trap Installation in Henderson, NV

    Hydro-mechanical grease traps, also known as automatic grease removal units, offer an active solution for FOG management in Henderson, NV. These units often include internal baffles and flow control mechanisms to improve separation efficiency. We provide expert installation of various hydro-mechanical models, ensuring all electrical and plumbing connections are correctly made for optimal performance and automated FOG removal.

    Grease Trap Installation Cost in Henderson, NV

    Small under-sink hydromechanical traps generally cost much less to install than large outdoor interceptors. Indoor units are usually a few thousand dollars installed, while in-ground interceptors can run well into five figures with excavation and concrete work. Pricing depends on unit size, location, soil conditions and whether we need to cut and patch floors. We give you a written estimate that includes permits and inspection. That way there are no surprises during construction.

    Gravity Grease Interceptor Installation in Henderson, NV

    Gravity grease interceptors, or GGI units, are larger, usually located outdoors, and rely on the natural difference in specific gravity between FOG and water. These are common for high-volume commercial kitchens. Our team handles the complex excavation, concrete work, and plumbing connections required for proper GGI installation, ensuring compliance with all local Nevada ordinances for waste management.

    Hydromechanical Grease Trap Installation in Henderson

    Hydromechanical grease traps sit under or near sinks and use baffles and flow control to separate grease from water. They are common in small cafes, coffee shops and quick-service restaurants with tight space. We install units from Zurn, Watts, Schier and Endura sized by gallons per minute. Each install includes a flow control fitting and proper venting to keep the trap working as designed. These units need frequent cleaning, so we place them where staff can reach the lid easily.
