    Why Choose FMR Plumbing for Trenchless Sewer Line Repair in Henderson, NV?

    Cutting a driveway costs you twice, first to saw and break the slab and then again to form and pour new concrete over the patch. Lining from the cleanout avoids both of those bills. For a line under concrete or pavers, trenchless is often the cheaper job once restoration is counted.

    • Lower Restoration Cost, Skipping the trench means no new driveway slab and no resodding the front lawn.
    • Less Restoration, Small access points mean less concrete cutting and less sod to replace.
    • Jointless Pipe, A cured liner has no joints, so roots and ground water have nowhere to get in.

    Cured-In-Place Pipe Relining in Henderson, NV

    CIPP stands for cured-in-place pipe. We wet out a felt tube with epoxy resin, invert it into the cleaned line with air pressure, and hold it against the old pipe wall until the resin hardens. Ambient cure takes a few hours, and hot water or steam shortens it. The finished liner seals cracks and leaking joints in the original pipe.

    Pipe Bursting in Henderson, NV

    Two pits are standard for a burst, one at the house end near the foundation and one out near the street or the city tap. The new HDPE pipe is butt-fused into one length first. The winch or hydraulic puller then drags the bursting head and the new pipe through. It goes quickly.

Over time it softens, goes oval and blisters on the inside, so a liner has nothing round to bond to and bursting is usually the better fix. Clay is different.
