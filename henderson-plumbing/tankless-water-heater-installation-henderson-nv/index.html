    Hard Water Protection for Tankless Units in Henderson, NV

    Calcium scale is what kills most tankless units early. Mineral deposits clog aerators, showerheads and valves, which is why softeners and regular water heater flushing are popular. Once scale coats the heat exchanger, efficiency drops and error codes start, and over time it can crack the exchanger and void the warranty. A yearly flush prevents it. We install isolation valves for that.

    Tankless Water Heater Relocation in Henderson

    Moving the heater is common on a tankless job since the tank's location may not suit the new unit. A garage or outside wall near the gas meter can shorten the gas run and simplify venting, but it may lengthen the hot water run to the bathrooms. We weigh both. Long hot runs may call for recirculation.

    Recirculation Systems for Tankless Heaters in Henderson

    Tankless heaters can take a few extra seconds to get hot water to a far bathroom, because the unit has to sense flow and fire first. A recirculation pump on a timer or a motion sensor keeps the hot line primed, so the wait at the farthest sink drops from a minute to a few seconds. Less water goes down the drain.

    Electric Tankless Water Heater Installation in Henderson, NV

    Electric tankless heaters make sense in Henderson, NV homes without gas, or for a single sink or a guest bath far from the main heater. Whole-house electric units pull a lot of current. Some need two or three 40 to 60 amp double-pole breakers, and a 100-amp panel often cannot carry that on top of the air conditioner and range. We check the panel first.

    Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Henderson, NV

    Replacing an old tankless unit is usually simpler than a first-time conversion. The gas line is already sized and the vent path already exists, so in many homes it is a matter of matching the connections, checking the vent for damage and hanging the new unit. Different brands vent differently. We confirm fit first.

    Residential Tankless Water Heater Installation in Henderson

    Sizing a home unit comes down to two numbers: peak gallons per minute and temperature rise. A shower head flows about 2 gallons per minute and a kitchen faucet a bit less, so a house that runs two showers and a sink together needs roughly 5 to 6 gallons at the rise your inlet water calls for. It shortens the list quickly.

    Upgrading from Tank to Tankless in Henderson, NV

    Switching from a tank takes more than unbolting one and hanging the other. A typical 40-gallon gas tank uses around 40,000 BTU, while a whole-home tankless unit may need 150,000 to 199,000, so the gas line from the meter almost always gets upsized. Venting changes too. We plan both on the quote.

    Propane Tankless Water Heater Installation in Henderson

    Propane homes in Henderson are good candidates for tankless, using a model built or converted for propane. Propane runs at a higher pressure than natural gas, so the regulator, gas line sizing and the unit's gas valve all have to match, and the propane supplier may need to change the regulator at the tank. Tank size matters too.
