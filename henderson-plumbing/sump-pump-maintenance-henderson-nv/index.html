    Float Switch Repair and Adjustment in Henderson, NV

    The float switch is the most frequent reason a sump pump fails to start. Tethered floats can tangle on the pump body, and vertical floats can stick from mineral crust or debris. We clean or replace the switch and set the on and off levels so the pump does not short cycle. Electronic sensor switches are an option for tight pits where floats get stuck.

    Sump Pump Cleaning and Debris Removal in Henderson

    Over time, sump pits can collect dirt, gravel and other debris, which can clog the pump's intake or jam the float switch. Our maintenance process includes carefully cleaning the sump pit and removing any obstructions. This lets the pump operate freely and efficiently, preventing breakdowns and extending its lifespan for your Henderson property.

    Pedestal Sump Pump Check-Ups in Henderson, NV

    For homes equipped with pedestal sump pumps, FMR Plumbing provides specialized maintenance services in Henderson, NV. We focus on inspecting the external motor, confirming proper lubrication and checking the impeller for any obstructions. This type of pump, with its motor above the water, requires specific attention to its shaft and bearings to prevent premature wear and ensure reliable performance during heavy rains.

    Battery Backup Pump Maintenance in Henderson

    Severe storms often bring power outages along with the rain. A battery backup pump keeps water moving when the main pump loses power or fails. We load test the battery, check the charger and alarm and run the backup pump on its own. Most backup batteries need replacing every three to five years.

    Emergency Sump Pump Maintenance in Henderson

    If your sump pump is acting up or has stopped working, FMR Plumbing provides emergency maintenance services in Henderson. We focus on rapid response to diagnose and address urgent issues, whether it's a jammed impeller, a faulty float switch or a complete pump failure. Our goal is to quickly restore your sump pump's function to prevent immediate flooding and protect your property from water damage.

    Discharge Line and Check Valve Service in Henderson

    The discharge line carries water away from your basement, and the check valve prevents pumped water from flowing back into the pit. We inspect these components for blockages, leaks or damage. A faulty check valve can cause the pump to short-cycle, leading to premature wear. We confirm these vital parts are functioning correctly to maintain an effective water removal system for your Henderson residence.

    Maintenance Versus Replacement Cost in Henderson, NV

    Routine maintenance typically costs a fraction of replacing a burned-out pump and cleaning up a flooded basement. A new installed sump pump often runs $400 to $1,200 depending on horsepower and backup options. Most pumps last seven to ten years with care.
