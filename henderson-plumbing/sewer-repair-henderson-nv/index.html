    Sewer Repair Methods for Henderson, NV Homes

    Sewer Line Backups and Blockages in Henderson

    Water coming up in the tub when the washer drains is a classic main line symptom. The blockage sits downstream of every fixture. We clear it from the nearest cleanout, then run the camera to see what caused it, since a grease clog and a root mass need very different fixes. Older Nevada homes with clay lines often show roots right at the joints. You get the footage either way.

    Cracked and Broken Pipe Repair in Henderson

    Clay and cast iron crack from settling soil, tree roots and vehicles parked over the line. The crack lets soil in and sewage out. We excavate at the marked spot, cut out the damaged length and install new Schedule 40 PVC with shielded stainless couplings rated for the transition between materials, then test the repair with water and video before closing the hole. Most of these are one-day jobs.

    Sewer Pipe Relining in Henderson, NV

    Relining builds a new pipe inside the old one. The old pipe gets jetted clean before anything else. A flexible liner saturated with epoxy is pulled or inverted into place, inflated against the old wall and left to cure, which seals cracks and root entry points in a single continuous tube. The finished pipe loses a little diameter. For most houses the flow difference is negligible.

    Video Camera Sewer Inspection in Henderson

    A self-leveling camera goes in at the cleanout or through a pulled toilet. We record the whole run. Cracks, root intrusion, offset joints and standing water all show up on the screen, and the sonde in the camera head lets us mark the exact spot and depth on the ground above, so any excavation stays small and targeted. Older homes in Henderson often hide two or three problems in one line, and the video catches all of them.

    Root Intrusion Removal in Henderson, NV

    Roots enter through hairline cracks and loose joints, then grow into a mat that catches paper and grease. Cut roots regrow within a year or two. We clear the line with a root cutting head or a jetter with a chain flail, camera it afterward to see the condition of each joint, and show you whether a liner or a spot repair would stop the cycle for good. Mature trees in Henderson yards make this a common call.

    Sewer Line Hydro-Jetting in Henderson

    A hydro-jetter pushes water at several thousand psi through a nozzle that scours the full inside of the pipe. A cable only punches a hole. That makes jetting the better choice for grease, scale and fine root growth, and we always jet a line before lining it. Brittle or broken pipe is a different story. We check the pipe on video before we jet it.

    Bellied Sewer Line Correction in Henderson, NV

    A belly is a low spot where the pipe has sagged and holds standing water. Paper and solids settle there. Clay soils that swell when watered and shrink when dry can shift slabs and buried drain lines over time. We measure the depth of standing water on camera, dig out that section, re-bed the pipe on compacted gravel and set it back to a quarter inch of fall per foot, which is the typical code minimum for a four-inch line. Not every sag needs digging, and we will tell you which yours is.
