    Foundation Crack Repair in Henderson

    Crack injection takes about two hours per crack. We set injection ports every 8 to 12 inches along the crack, seal the surface with epoxy paste, and pump in resin from the bottom port up until it pushes out the next port. Polyurethane stays flexible if the wall moves a little, and epoxy bonds the two sides back together. The surface paste gets ground off once it cures.

    Plumbing Leak Behind Walls in Henderson

    A drip from a shower valve cartridge, a sweated copper stub or a loose drain slip nut can soak a wall cavity for months before paint bubbles. We find it with a moisture meter, a thermal camera that shows the cool wet spot, and a pressure test on the supply lines. Then we cut a clean access hole. The bad fitting or section of pipe gets replaced. Wet insulation comes out, and fans dry the cavity before we close it up.

    Crawl Space Vapor Barriers in Henderson

    Bare dirt in a crawl space releases gallons of water vapor a day into the air under your floor. A 10 mil or heavier polyethylene barrier, overlapped a foot at every seam and taped, cuts that off and keeps the joists and subfloor dry. We run it up the foundation walls and hold it in place with termination strip. Cheap fix, big difference.

    Below-Grade and Basement Wall Waterproofing in Henderson

    Some homes from the 1980s and 1990s may still have polybutylene supply pipe, which is prone to sudden failures and is usually replaced. We inject cracks, coat the wall inside or out, and install drain tile where water pools along the footing. A sump goes in if groundwater sits high. Not every basement needs one.

    Laundry Room and Utility Wall Waterproofing in Henderson, NV

    Rubber washing machine hoses burst.     Concrete block is porous, and its hollow cores fill with water like a sponge. The high desert in the north brings cold winters and sharp daily temperature swings that stress pipes and fittings.     Interior Walls Waterproofing in Henderson, NV

    Interior drain tile handles water that already got through the wall.
