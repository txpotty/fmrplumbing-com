    Pipe Bursting and Pulling in Carson, NV

    Bursting fits old cast iron or galvanized lines that will shatter under the head. Pulling suits softer pipe like poly or copper. Either way the new pipe follows the old path, which matters when the route runs under a patio or a neighbor's fence, and the yard work shrinks to two pits. Rock can stop it.

    PEX Water Line Replacement in Carson

    PEX costs less than copper and goes in faster, which matters on a long run. Scale and corrosion are not a problem with it. We use PEX-a or PEX rated for direct burial, use only fittings listed for burial, and lay tracer wire on top so a locator can find it later, since plastic pipe does not show up on a metal detector. Cheap insurance.

    Trenchless Bores Under Driveways in Carson

    Directional boring drills a path under the obstacle and pulls the pipe back through it. No old line is needed to follow. We use it when the new line has to take a different route, say around a new addition or under a driveway poured after the house was built, leaving just two small pits in the yard. Heavy rock is the exception.

    Commercial Water Service Replacement in Carson

    Strip centers, churches and small apartment buildings each come with their own wrinkles. Some have a shared meter feeding several units. We map where each branch goes before cutting anything, bring the utility in for the meter side, and handle the permit, backflow test and inspection so the owner gets one point of contact. Paperwork included.

    Copper Water Line Installation in Carson

    Type K copper is the thick-wall grade made for burial. Street pressure does not bother it. We bed it in sand, keep it away from sharp rock, and sleeve it where it passes through a foundation wall, because concrete and copper rubbing together for twenty years will wear through the pipe. PEX is cheaper.

    Main Water Line Repair vs. Replacement in Carson

    One leak on a twenty-year-old copper line? We will usually just fix that spot. Three leaks on a galvanized line from the 1950s is different, because the pipe is thin everywhere and the next leak is already forming, so the full line costs less than a string of repairs. We tell you which camp you are in and show you the pipe we cut out.

    Pressure Regulator and Shutoff Upgrades in Carson, NV

    Street pressure varies a lot from one neighborhood to the next. Code says no more than 80 psi inside the house. If your new line comes in above that, we add a pressure reducing valve right after the main shutoff, set it around 55 to 65 psi, and replace the gate valve with a quarter-turn ball valve. A quarter turn and the water is off.

    Burst Main Line Response in Carson, NV

    A burst main is a fast-moving problem. We get the water off, dig to the break, and decide on site whether a clamp or a short section of new pipe will hold until a full replacement can be scheduled, or whether the pipe crumbles when we touch it. You will not be without water overnight if we can help it.
