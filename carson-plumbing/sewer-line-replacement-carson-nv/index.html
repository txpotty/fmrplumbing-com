    Frequently Asked Questions About Sewer Line Replacement in Carson

    Sewer line replacement in Carson typically ranges from about $4,000 to $15,000 for a typical residential lateral, and can run higher for long, deep or hard-to-reach lines. Trenchless work is often priced around $80 to $250 per foot based on conditions.

    Traditional replacement involves digging a trench to access and replace the entire pipe, which can be more disruptive to your yard. Trenchless methods, like pipe bursting or pipe lining, require minimal digging, often just two access points, preserving your landscaping while installing a new pipe.

    Most policies do not cover wear and tear or root damage to sewer lines. Some offer service line coverage as an add-on, so review your policy.

    Modern sewer lines made from materials like PVC or HDPE, when properly installed, can last 50 to 100 years or longer. This is a big improvement over older materials like cast iron or clay, which typically have a shorter lifespan.

    FMR Plumbing stands behind the quality of our sewer line replacement work. We offer solid warranties on both the materials used and our labor. The specific warranty details are provided in writing with your project estimate, so you know your investment in a new sewer line for your Carson home is covered.

    We primarily use high-quality PVC (polyvinyl chloride) or HDPE (high-density polyethylene) pipe for sewer line replacements. These materials are durable, corrosion-resistant, flexible, and long-lived, making them well suited for the soil conditions in Nevada.

    We typically use PVC for open-trench work and HDPE for pipe bursting. Both resist corrosion and root intrusion far better than clay or cast iron.

    The duration depends on the complexity and method. A trenchless replacement might take 1 to 3 days, while a traditional trenched replacement could take 3 to 5 days, sometimes longer for larger projects. We will give you a clear timeline estimate during your consultation.
