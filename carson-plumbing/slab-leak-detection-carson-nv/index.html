    Slab Leaks in Older Homes in Carson, NV

    Older homes in Carson, NV often have soft copper or galvanized steel under the slab, and both corrode with age. Copper fails at bends and where it rubs concrete, while galvanized rusts from the inside until it splits. We know those failure points. It lets us test the most likely spots first.

    Direct Access Slab Leak Repair in Carson, NV

    A single leak on sound pipe can be fixed in place. We cut a small opening at the marked spot, dig down to the pipe, replace the bad section and hold a pressure test. Then we backfill and patch the concrete level, ready for flooring. A precise mark keeps the opening tight.

    Non-Invasive Slab Leak Locating in Carson, NV

    We touch the floor last. Pressure tests, line tracing, thermal scans and acoustic listening all happen from the surface in your Carson, NV home, so nothing gets drilled or cut during the search. Only after two methods agree do we mark the spot and talk about repair options, including ones that skip the concrete entirely.

    Supply and Drain Leaks Under the Slab in Carson, NV

    Supply leaks and drain leaks act differently. Supply leaks are pressurized, so they move the meter, warm the floor on the hot side and run whether or not anyone uses water. Drain leaks only leak when water goes down, showing up as smells, sluggish drains or wet spots near bathrooms. We test both kinds in your Carson, NV home.

    Slab Leak Reroutes in Carson

    A reroute abandons the leaking line under the floor. New pipe runs through the walls or attic instead, so there is no concrete work and the cost is often lower than going through tile. We usually use PEX because it handles heat and resists corrosion. Tile and hardwood floors make this the favorite.

    Post-Tension Slab Leak Detection in Carson

    Many newer homes sit on post-tension slabs. These slabs have steel tendons stretched under tens of thousands of pounds of force, and cutting one by mistake is dangerous and can damage the foundation. We scan for tendons with ground-penetrating radar or a rebar locator before opening anything, or suggest a reroute that skips the slab.

    Slab Leak Price Ranges in Carson, NV

    Slab leak detection typically costs $150 to $450. Repipes run roughly $4,000 to $15,000. They end the problem for good.

    Hot Water Slab Leak Detection in Carson

    The hot side fails more than the cold. Heat speeds up corrosion, and the pipe expands and contracts every time someone showers, wearing against the gravel and concrete until a pinhole opens. Warm floors and a water heater that runs nonstop are the signs. Gas or electric bills may jump too.
