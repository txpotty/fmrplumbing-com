    Emergency Sump Pump Diagnostics in Carson, NV

    Grinding, clicking and a hum with no water moving each point to a different failure. Our plumber works from the outlet and cord to the float and then the motor. A tripped GFCI is a quick fix. A seized motor means a new pump, and we keep replacements on the truck so the pit is never left unprotected overnight.

    Float Switch Repair and Adjustment in Carson, NV

    Float trouble causes more no-start calls than anything else. A tethered float can tangle on the pump body or catch on the side of the basin. Vertical floats stick when mineral crust builds up on the rod. In tight pits where nothing has room to swing, we can switch you to an electronic sensor switch with no moving float at all.

    Discharge Line and Check Valve Service in Carson

    The check valve sits on the discharge pipe a foot or so above the pump and keeps the water in the line from falling back into the pit. When it fails, the pump pumps the same water twice. Motors wear out faster. We test the valve, look for a weep hole between the pump and the valve, and check every joint up to the wall.

    Wayne Sump Pump Maintenance in Carson, NV

    Some Wayne models draw water through a top suction intake, which helps them avoid pulling in the sludge that settles at the bottom of a pit. That intake gets cleaned, and so does the float mechanism. Any brand can rattle its discharge fittings loose over time. Easy fix.

    Commercial Sump Pump Maintenance in Carson, NV

    Commercial pits are often bigger and deeper, and many have two pumps on an alternating controller. We test both pumps, the alternator and the high-water alarm. You get a written record of each reading, which comes in handy for insurance renewals and inspections. Quarterly visits are common.

    Check Valve and Discharge Pipe Repair in Carson, NV

    A bad check valve can nearly double how often the pump starts. Every startup puts heat into the motor. We swap worn valves for quiet, spring-loaded models and inspect the discharge pipe for cracks and loose couplings all the way to the exit point. Discharge needs to land far enough from the slab or footings that it does not seep right back into the pit.

    Crawlspace and Window Well Pumps in Carson, NV

    Not every sump sits in a basement. Plenty of crawlspaces and deep window wells use a small pump to clear storm runoff. Our plumber clears the screen, tests the float and traces the discharge to daylight or an approved drain, since a line that ends under the house only relocates the water. The northern part of the state sees regular hard freezes, so pipes in exterior walls and crawlspaces need protection.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Maintenance in Carson, NV

    Backup pumps sit in the pit next to the main pump and run off a 12-volt battery when the power drops or the primary fails. We check the battery with a load tester, not just a voltmeter. Next, we cut power to the main pump and watch the backup clear the basin by itself. Alarms get tested too.
