    Electrical Problems and Resetting in Carson

    No hum at all usually means no power reaching the motor.     Grinding Noise Repair in Carson

    A metal-on-metal grind may mean a loose mount, a foreign object or worn impellers. We inspect the chamber, tighten the mounting ring and remove any debris, then run the unit empty to hear whether the motor itself is making the noise. Bad bearings mean replacement.

    Disposal Odor and Buildup Cleaning in Carson, NV

    Food buildup and grease on the chamber walls and under the splash guard are behind most disposal odors. Cleaning the guard and chamber, checking the drain and swapping a torn guard often solves a smell that lemon peels never fixed.

    Preventive Care and Longevity in Carson, NV

Repeat jams point us to the trap arm.
