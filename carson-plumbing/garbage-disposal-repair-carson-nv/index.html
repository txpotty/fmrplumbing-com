    When a disposal will not turn on at all, the thermal overload may have tripped or the switch may be bad. We check the reset button, breaker, wall switch and wiring. Air switches and batch-feed lids are tested too. Most electrical issues are easy to fix once found.

    Slow Draining Disposal Repair in Carson, NV

    A disposal that grinds but drains slowly usually has a clogged discharge tube or trap arm. Grease, rice and coffee grounds are the usual culprits. We clear the line with the right tools and check for proper slope. We also explain what not to put the disposal.

    Leaking Disposal Repair in Carson, NV

    Leaks can come from the sink flange, the dishwasher inlet, the discharge tube or the disposal body itself. In that case, replacement is the smarter option.
