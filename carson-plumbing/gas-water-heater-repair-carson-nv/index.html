    Pilot Light and Ignition Service in Carson

    No hot water and a dark pilot window usually point to the ignition side of the heater. We pull the pilot assembly and look at the thermocouple tip, the pilot orifice and the piezo igniter wire. A thermocouple that tests weak on the millivolt meter will not hold the gas valve's magnet open, so the pilot drops out a minute after you relight it by the label. Common pilot kits ride on most of our trucks.

    Gas Water Heater Noise Diagnosis in Carson

    Rumbling or popping from the bottom of the tank is water boiling under a layer of sediment. Hard water and years without a flush both make it worse. We drain the tank through the bottom valve, stir up the settled scale with short bursts of cold water and keep going until it runs clear, which on a neglected tank can take a while. Whistling points somewhere else. That usually means a relief valve or a partly closed supply valve.

    Gas Control Valve Replacement in Carson, NV

    Honeywell and White-Rodgers valves report faults with a blinking LED, and the flash count matches a chart on the label. When the control itself is the problem, we replace it with an exact-match valve or a listed universal model, set the temperature and leak test every joint on the gas train before relighting. We leave the old valve with you if you ask.
