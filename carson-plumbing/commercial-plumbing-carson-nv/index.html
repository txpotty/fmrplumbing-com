    Backflow Testing and Certification in Carson

    Each backflow test takes about 30 minutes per assembly and requires a short water shutoff to that line. Plan for a brief outage. We read each check valve and the relief valve on a calibrated gauge and record the numbers on the standard test form. We tag every passing device. The provider gets the report from us, and we keep a copy for your next test.

    Commercial Restroom Plumbing in Carson

    A broken flushometer in a busy restroom gets noticed by every customer who walks in. We repair and replace Sloan and Zurn flush valves, sensor faucets, wall-hung toilets and carriers.     Kitchen and Floor Drain Cleaning in Carson

    Floor drains in commercial kitchens collect grease, rice, mop water and the odd utensil. Most kitchens see the same drain back up every few months. A sectional machine opens the line, and a jetter cleans grease off the walls of the 2-inch and 3-inch branches so it stays open longer. We also check trap primers.

    Commercial Sewer Line Repair in Carson, NV

    The building sewer on a commercial property carries everything from every tenant, so a root intrusion or a belly in the line affects the whole property. We camera the line, locate the problem depth with a sonde and then decide between a spot repair, pipe lining or pipe bursting. Trenchless saves parking lots. Lining a 6-inch lateral can often be done in a day without tearing up asphalt or closing an entrance.

    Backflow tests often run $75 to $175 per assembly, and kitchen line jetting usually costs $350 to $900 depending on length and buildup. After-hours calls cost more.     Backflow Prevention Assembly Service in Carson

    A backflow assembly keeps water from your building, including irrigation lines, boiler water and chemical feeds, from flowing back into the public supply. Most providers want a yearly test. We test RPZ, double check and PVB assemblies with calibrated gauges and send the results straight to the water provider. A failed test usually means new rubber and springs, not a whole new assembly. We carry Watts, Wilkins and Febco kits for that reason.

    Commercial Water Heater Repair and Installation in Carson, NV

    A commercial water heater is judged by recovery rate, the gallons it can reheat per hour, not by tank size alone. Undersized heaters run cold by mid-shift. We service AO Smith, Rheem, Bradford White and State commercial units, gas and electric, tank and tankless, and we check thermostats, gas valves, flue draft and T and P valves on every call. For a replacement, we size from fixture count and peak demand. Proper sizing is what keeps a busy kitchen in hot water all night.

    Commercial Pipe Repair and Repiping in Carson, NV

    Older commercial buildings often have galvanized supply lines that are rusted nearly shut, or copper that has pinholed along hot water runs. Patching works for a while. When pinholes keep moving around the building, repiping the worst zones in Type L copper or PEX usually beats paying for more emergency visits. We map the system first. Work gets phased by floor or suite so tenants keep their water most of the day.
