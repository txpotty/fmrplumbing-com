    Hydro-jetting scours the pipe with water at several thousand psi. The whole inside gets cleaned. A snake only punches a hole through a clog, but a jet nozzle strips buildup off the entire pipe wall. We camera first on old or brittle pipe.

    Tightening a slip nut or replacing a plastic trap is fair game. A plunger is always worth a try. Anything inside a wall, under a floor or involving cast iron, or a repeat leak at the same joint, should go to a plumber. Chemical cleaners can make things worse.

    Age and corrosion lead the list. Settling ground plays a part. Tree roots, temperature swings, heavy loads over buried pipe, plus bad installation such as skipped primer or unsupported runs, can all break a drain early. Most failures have more than one cause.

    Cleaning removes a blockage from a sound pipe. Repair changes the pipe itself.     Repeat clogs come from something in the pipe catching debris. Usually it is buildup. Poor pipe slope, a missing vent or a cracked fitting with a rough edge will snag paper and hair until the line closes off again a few weeks after each cleaning. A camera shows which one you have.

    Slow drains, gurgling and sewer smell are the big three warnings. Drips in the cabinet count too.
