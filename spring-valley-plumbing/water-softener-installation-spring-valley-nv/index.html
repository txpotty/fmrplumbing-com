    Salt-Free Water Conditioner Installation in Spring Valley

    Salt-free conditioners do not soften water, and we say so up front. They change the minerals into crystals that do not stick to pipe walls and heating elements, so scale drops off without adding sodium or using a drain line. Little upkeep is needed. They suit moderate hardness.

    Water Softener Installation for New Construction in Spring Valley, NV

    Building a home in Spring Valley, NV? Adding a loop during framing is easy and cheap, since the plumber can route the main through the right spot before drywall goes up. We work with builders. We keep outdoor taps off the softener.

    Whole-House Water Softener Systems in Spring Valley

    On a whole-house system, the softener goes on the main line after the shutoff and before the water heater. We add a bypass valve so you can isolate the softener for service, and we install a pressure regulator if your street pressure runs above 80 psi. Soft water reaches every fixture. We can skip the hose bibs.

    Salt-Based Water Softener Installation in Spring Valley

    Ion exchange is the only method that truly removes hardness from water. Every salt-based install includes a sized resin tank, a brine tank, a bypass and a drain to an air gap. Then we program it. We set regeneration overnight.

    Water Hardness Testing and Programming in Spring Valley

    Testing is what makes the settings right. Our crew checks hardness, iron and chlorine, then sets the regeneration and salt dosage to match. Iron adds to the setting. We review the settings with you before we leave.

    Pelican Water Softener Installation in Spring Valley, NV

    Pelican sells both salt-based softeners and salt-free conditioners, often direct to homeowners. When a homeowner has already bought one, we handle the plumbing, the bypass and the startup flush the manual requires. Skipping the flush causes trouble. Every step gets done.

    Reverse Osmosis Drinking Water Systems in Spring Valley

    Reverse osmosis pushes water through a fine membrane that removes most dissolved solids, including the sodium a softener adds. Our RO installs go under the sink with their own faucet, and we can tie in the fridge or ice maker too. It complements a softener. We can handle filter changes.

    Combination Softener and Carbon Filter Systems in Spring Valley, NV

    City water often carries chlorine or chloramine that affects taste and smell. Putting a carbon tank in front of the softener takes out chlorine and keeps it from wearing down the resin. Taste improves fast. We install twin-tank setups or dual-stage units.
