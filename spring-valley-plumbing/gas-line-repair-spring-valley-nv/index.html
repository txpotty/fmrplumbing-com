    What Our Gas Line Plumbers Fix in Spring Valley, NV

    Gas Leak Repair at Fittings and Valves in Spring Valley

    Most residential gas leaks are at a threaded joint, a valve stem or a flexible connector, not in the middle of a pipe. That keeps repairs small. We back out the leaking joint, clean the threads, apply gas-rated thread sealant and reassemble it, or replace the fitting or valve if the threads are damaged. We soap-test the repaired joint and pressure test the system before leaving.

    Gas Permits and Inspections in Spring Valley

    Repairs that change piping, and all new gas lines, usually need a permit and inspection from Spring Valley building officials. We file the permit, schedule the inspection and are on site when the inspector checks the work and the test gauge. Inspectors often come within the week. Once it passes, the utility can restore service and we come back to relight appliances if needed.

    Natural Gas Leak Detection and Repair in Spring Valley

    Finding a gas leak starts at the meter. We shut off every appliance and watch the meter's test dial, then close branch valves one at a time to find which section is leaking. A combustible gas detector narrows it further. A spray of soap solution on the fitting shows bubbles at the leak, and we fix that joint or section and retest the whole system.

    Emergency Gas Line Repair in Spring Valley

    If you smell gas, leave the building, skip the light switches and call your gas utility or 911 from outside. Then call us. Once the utility has shut off the meter and made the area safe, we locate the leak, make the repair and run the pressure test needed before service can be restored. We relight pilots when service returns. Most emergency repairs are done in a single visit with parts from the truck.

    Gas Shut Off by the Utility in Spring Valley

    When your utility shuts off gas for a leak, you may be without hot water, heat or cooking until a licensed plumber fixes it. We respond quickly, locate the leak, make the repair and run the pressure test the utility needs to see before it will restore service. Most are one-visit repairs. We stay until the system holds its test pressure and the meter is ready to be unlocked.

    Corroded Gas Pipe Replacement in Spring Valley

    Buried steel gas line corrodes once its coating cracks, and moisture, roots and ground movement speed it up. Most homes sit on concrete slabs, so leaks in buried supply lines often show up as warm spots on the floor or higher water bills. We replace corroded sections with polyethylene pipe laid with tracer wire, using an approved transition riser where the line comes up out of the ground at the house. Exposed sections get coated steel. Everything is tested before the trench is backfilled, so a bad joint gets caught while it is still easy to reach.

    Interior Black Iron Pipe Repair in Spring Valley

    Threaded black iron pipe can leak at joints after years of vibration or settling. We tighten, reseal or replace fittings and sections with new pipe and gas-rated thread sealant. Rusty runs get swapped out. Pipe in a garage, crawlspace or exterior wall that shows heavy rust gets replaced before it turns into a leak, and the whole system gets a pressure test after the repair.

    Appliance Connector and Shutoff Repair in Spring Valley

    Old flexible connectors, especially uncoated brass ones, crack and leak with age. New coated stainless connectors go on, sized to the appliance, with a reachable shutoff valve if the old one is stuck or missing. Usually under an hour per appliance. Furnaces, water heaters, ranges and dryers each need a sound connection, and most need a drip leg before the appliance valve.
