    Steam Shower Plumbing in Spring Valley

    Steam showers need four things: a generator, a water supply, a drain line and an electrical circuit. The generator goes within the maker's distance limit, and we plumb its supply, drain and steam line to the head. The circuit is an electrician's job. The enclosure needs a sealed door and a sloped ceiling.

    Shower Stall Installation in Spring Valley

    A one-piece stall needs a level, fully supported base. We set fiberglass and acrylic bases in a bed of mortar or setting compound so the floor of the unit does not flex underfoot, since flex is what cracks a base within a couple of years. The drain connects from below. We check the flow at the head once the trim is on.

    Curbless Shower Installation in Spring Valley

    Curbless showers need the floor lowered in the shower area. On a wood floor, we drop the joists or use a recessed pan system, and on a slab, we cut and lower the concrete so the finished floor runs flat into the shower. A linear drain often works best. Slope and waterproofing get planned before demo.

    Prefab Shower Unit Installation in Spring Valley, NV

    Prefab units are the fastest route to a new shower. The base goes on mortar, the drain and valve get connected and the walls are fastened so they stay rigid. They suit guest baths well. Most installs finish in a day.

    Shower Pan Installation in Spring Valley

    Shower pans fail quietly. A pan with a pinhole in the liner or a poorly clamped drain will hold water for years while it slowly soaks the subfloor, so we flood-test every tiled pan for 24 hours before tile goes down. We level prefab pans carefully. Both kinds get slope toward the drain.

    Emergency Shower System Replacement in Spring Valley, NV

    Water under a failed pan can rot a wood subfloor in a season. A cracked pan or a leaking valve means pulling the shower, checking the framing and getting a new unit in as soon as it is safe. Dry framing first. After that, the new unit goes in.

    Barrier-Free Walk-In Showers in Spring Valley, NV

    Barrier-free entries need careful drain placement. The membrane and slope get planned with the tile setter, so water reaches the drain instead of spilling out the open side. Intense summer heat and a dry climate are the main stresses on plumbing in Nevada, especially in the southern part of the state. Pan material follows from that.

    Custom Shower System Installation in Spring Valley, NV

    Multi-head showers depend on valve capacity. A standard pressure-balance valve can feed one head, but rain heads, body jets and handhelds need a thermostatic valve with separate volume controls and supply lines sized up to 3/4 inch. Layout comes first. The water heater gets checked for capacity too.
