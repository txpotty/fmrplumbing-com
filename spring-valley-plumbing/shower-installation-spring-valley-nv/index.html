    Prefab Shower Unit Installation in Spring Valley, NV

    Fiberglass and acrylic shower units are budget-friendly and quick to install. We set the base, connect the drain and install the valve and trim. Prefab units are ideal for guest baths, rentals and guest houses. We make sure the base is fully supported so it does not flex or split.

    Curbless Shower Installation in Spring Valley

    Curbless showers have no step, which makes them easier to use and gives a modern look. They require a recessed floor and careful drain placement, often with a linear drain. On a slab, we may need to lower the floor in the shower area. We work closely with your contractor to plan the slope and waterproof layers.

    Standard Shower Stall Installation in Spring Valley

    FMR Plumbing installs all types of standard shower stalls, from fiberglass units to acrylic enclosures. We ensure proper leveling, sealing, and connection to your existing plumbing system. Our team handles the entire process, including securing the base and connecting the drain line, to prevent future leaks and ensure stability. We also verify that water pressure is optimal for your new showerhead.

    Accessible and ADA Shower Installation in Spring Valley, NV

    Accessible showers need low or no thresholds, handheld showers on slide bars and properly placed controls. We install grab bar blocking while walls are open and set valves within easy reach of the seat. These features help seniors and people with mobility needs bathe safely. We follow ADA guidelines when requested.

    Emergency Shower System Replacement in Spring Valley, NV

    Steam Shower Plumbing in Spring Valley

    Steam showers need a steam generator, a dedicated water supply, a drain line and an electrical circuit. We install the generator, plumb the supply and drain, and connect the steam head. A licensed electrician handles the circuit. Steam showers also need sealed enclosures and sloped ceilings.
