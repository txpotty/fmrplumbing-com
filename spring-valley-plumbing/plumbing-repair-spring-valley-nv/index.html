    Water Heater Parts Repair in Spring Valley

    Rumbling or popping from a tank means sediment on the bottom. A rotten-egg smell in the hot water usually means the anode rod is reacting with the water. We replace thermocouples, thermostats, elements, gas valves, anode rods and relief valves from truck stock, which covers the large majority of tank repairs we see in a year. Rust at the seams is different. No part fixes that, and we tell you plainly.

    Well Pump Repair in Spring Valley, NV

    No water at the tap on a well system narrows the list quickly. It is usually the pressure switch, the capacitor in the control box, the pump motor or a waterlogged pressure tank. We check voltage and amp draw at the control box before pulling anything out of the well. Pulling a submersible pump is a two-person job.

    Tank and Tankless Water Heater Repair in Spring Valley

    Tank and tankless heaters fail in different ways. FMR Plumbing repairs both, across all the major brands, gas and electric. On a tank we check the thermocouple, gas valve, elements and anode rod. On a tankless unit, the culprit is often scale in the heat exchanger or a dirty inlet filter, so we flush and clean before swapping parts.

    Under-Slab Line Repair in Spring Valley, NV

    Much of the housing in Nevada was built in recent decades, with copper or PEX supply lines and plastic drains.     Water Softener Repair in Spring Valley, NV

    Homes around Spring Valley that rely on private wells should test for hardness and other minerals before installing treatment equipment. FMR Plumbing repairs the common brands and tests the water hardness before and after the fix so you know it is working again. Resin lasts ten to fifteen years.

    Sewer Pipe Repair in Spring Valley, NV

    Clay and Orangeburg laterals crack and separate as the ground shifts, and roots find those gaps within a season or two.     Drain Line Repair in Spring Valley

    Slow drains that come back right after a snake usually mean the pipe itself is damaged. Cast iron that has cracked or a run that sags will hold debris no matter how often it is cleared.     Slow Drain Repair in Spring Valley

    Gurgling toilets and a tub that drains slow often trace to a blocked vent or a sagging drain line. After clearing it, we camera the line and fix whatever is broken.
