    Galvanized Pipe Replacement in Spring Valley

    Older homes were plumbed with galvanized steel, which rusts from the inside out. Pressure fades over years. Every time a fitting is disturbed, the next one tends to leak, so repairs on galvanized are usually short-term and replacing the run with PEX or copper is the lasting fix. Transitions get dielectric fittings.

    Frozen and Burst Line Repair in Spring Valley

    Pipes in exterior walls, attics and crawlspaces freeze first. Frozen water cracks it. The split usually does not leak until the ice thaws, which is why many burst pipes are found hours after the cold snap. We repair the split and add insulation. Heat tape is an option on problem runs.

    Ceiling and Wall Leak Repair in Spring Valley, NV

    When water is pouring through a ceiling or spraying under a sink, you need help right away. Find the main valve.     Pipe Leak Detection in Spring Valley, NV

    A repair starts with finding the leak. Guessing wastes drywall. We use electronic listening devices, thermal cameras and moisture meters, and we isolate sections of the plumbing with pressure tests so the search narrows to one wall or one floor before anything gets opened. Then we make one opening.

    Drain and Waste Pipe Repair in Spring Valley

    Drain pipe made of ABS, PVC or cast iron can crack, separate or leak at joints. Look for ceiling spots. A leaking drain often shows up as a stain below a bathroom or a musty smell in a cabinet, so we find it, swap the bad length and run water at full volume to prove it. We look at venting as well.

    PEX Pipe Repair & Installation in Spring Valley

    PEX is flexible and handles freezing better than rigid pipe. It still fails. Most PEX leaks happen at a crimp ring that was not fully seated, an early brass fitting that dezincified or a section damaged by sunlight in an attic, so we replace the failed joint with the right fitting for that brand of PEX. Most take under an hour.

    Corroded Pipe Replacement in Spring Valley

    Galvanized steel and old copper both corrode with age. Brown water is a clue. Low pressure, discolored water and leaks at threaded joints tell us the pipe is closing off from the inside, and we replace the worst sections or the whole run with PEX or copper and dielectric fittings at every transition. Pressure comes right back.

    Slab Leak Pipe Repair in Spring Valley, NV

    Slab leaks start when pipe under the concrete wears through or gets stressed by soil movement. Clay soils that swell when watered and shrink when dry can stress slabs and buried drain lines over time. Warm floors are a clue. We isolate the leaking line with pressure tests and find it with acoustic and thermal equipment, and you pick between a targeted floor repair and a reroute overhead. We quote each way.
