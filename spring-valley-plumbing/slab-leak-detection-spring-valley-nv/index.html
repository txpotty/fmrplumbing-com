    Acoustic Slab Leak Detection in Spring Valley

    Water escaping a pressurized pipe makes a hiss that carries up through concrete. Our ground microphones hear it.     Hot Water Slab Leak Detection in Spring Valley

    Hot lines leak under slabs more often than cold ones. Heat speeds up corrosion, and the pipe expands and contracts every time someone showers, rubbing against concrete and gravel until it wears through. Warm floors and a water heater that runs nonstop are the signs. Energy bills often rise.

    Repiping After Repeat Slab Leaks in Spring Valley, NV

    Two slab leaks usually means more are coming. The rest of the pipe has likely aged the same way, so repiping replaces every supply line with new PEX or copper through the walls and attic, which ends the pattern of new leaks and new floor openings. A full repipe takes about two to four days.

    Cold Water Slab Leak Detection in Spring Valley, NV

    Cold-side leaks leave no warm spot. That makes them harder to spot. They usually show up as a running meter, damp carpet, a musty smell or water seeping at baseboards, so we isolate the cold side, map the pipe with a line tracer and listen along the route with a ground microphone.

    Camera Inspection for Under-Slab Drains in Spring Valley, NV

    Drain lines under the slab can leak too. A broken cast iron or PVC drain will not move the water meter, but it can cause odors, soggy floors and slow drains, so we run a camera through the line to find cracks, separated joints and bellies. We show you the video before discussing any repair in your Spring Valley home.
