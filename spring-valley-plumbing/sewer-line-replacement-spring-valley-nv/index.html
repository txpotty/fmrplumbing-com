    What Goes Into a Sewer Lateral Replacement in Spring Valley, NV

    Clay and Orangeburg Sewer Replacement in Spring Valley, NV

    Orangeburg is tar-soaked wood fiber. It was installed in a lot of homes in the decades after the war, and over time it softens, blisters and squashes into an oval that catches everything going by. Clay holds up better, except at the joints. Several bad spots on one run usually make full replacement the better buy.

    Replacing a Collapsed Sewer by Trench in Spring Valley

    Collapsed pipe cannot be burst or lined. When the camera stops at a pile of broken clay or a flattened section of Orangeburg, the only fix is to dig down, clear the debris and lay new pipe through that stretch. Being in the trench shows us what the surrounding soil is doing. Often there is a void or a root mat right at the failure point.

    Keeping a New Sewer Line Healthy in Spring Valley

    New pipe still needs a little care. Plant thirsty trees away from the line, since big roots can push on even a sound pipe. Clay soils that swell when watered and shrink when dry can stress slabs and buried drain lines over time. Flushable wipes still clog. A camera check every few years on an older house catches problems at the city tap or the cleanout early.

    Commercial Sewer Lateral Work in Spring Valley, NV

    Apartment buildings, offices and restaurants in Spring Valley, NV need lateral work too. Commercial lines carry more volume and more grease, so we size the pipe, place cleanouts for regular jetting, and coordinate grease interceptor connections with the inspector. Some jobs need traffic control. Tenant notices and work windows get planned ahead.

    Trenchless Sewer Replacement in Spring Valley, NV

    Two holes instead of one long trench. Pipe bursting drags a cone-shaped head through the old line, cracking it outward while new fused HDPE follows right behind. Lining is the other trenchless route. Lining puts a resin tube inside the old pipe and cures it hard, but only if the host pipe is still mostly round and has no major sags.

    Sewer Camera Inspection in Spring Valley

    No replacement quote without video. The camera goes in at the cleanout and travels out to the main, recording every crack, offset and root ball as it goes. A transmitter in the head lets us paint the spot and depth above ground. That one step decides between a spot repair and a full run.

    Emergency Sewer Replacement in Spring Valley, NV

    Sewage coming up a floor drain is an emergency. We answer the call, get a camera down the line, and tell you within the first visit whether the pipe has collapsed or just needs to be cleared for now. A collapse means we put in the permit and utility locates right away. FMR Plumbing can often run a temporary bypass. That keeps one toilet and the kitchen sink usable while the dig gets scheduled.

    Open-Trench Sewer Replacement in Spring Valley

    A collapsed run or a long belly usually means we dig. The crew trenches the full length of the lateral, removes the failed pipe and sets new PVC on compacted bedding with steady fall. Where we can, the sod is cut and set aside before the digging starts. The inspector sees open pipe. Backfill goes in compacted lifts so the trench line does not sink into a ditch a year later.
