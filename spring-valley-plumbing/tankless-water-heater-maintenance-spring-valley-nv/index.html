    Why Choose FMR Plumbing for Tankless Water Heater Maintenance in Spring Valley, NV?

    Sediment and scale do different damage. Grit lands on the inlet screen and starves the flow sensor of water. Scale bakes onto the hottest exchanger tubes, and only a pump-driven flush moves it, not a garden hose rinse.

    • Lower Gas Bills, A clean exchanger moves heat into the water faster, so the burner fires for less time per gallon.
    • Fewer Repair Calls, Worn igniters and weak flame rods get caught early, before they leave you without hot water.
    • Longer Heater Life, Removing scale keeps the exchanger from overheating, which is what usually ends a tankless unit early.

    Tankless Error Code Diagnosis in Spring Valley, NV

    An error code is the heater telling you what it saw. Rinnai LC codes point to scale, Navien E003 is a failed ignition and Noritz 90 points at combustion air or exhaust trouble. We pull the code history before touching anything. If a part has failed, you hear the repair and the price before we start.

    Service Valve Kit Installs in Spring Valley, NV

    Plenty of tankless heaters went in without service valves, and without them there is no clean way to hook up a descaling pump. A pair of isolation valves with purge ports goes on the hot and cold lines. Most kits include a relief valve port as well. After that, every flush takes minutes to set up.

    Step-by-Step Tankless Flushing in Spring Valley

    First we shut off the gas and power, then close both isolation valves so the heater is cut off from the house plumbing. Solution goes from a bucket into the cold port, out the hot port and back to the bucket. Once the cycle ends, we rinse with clean water and pull the inlet screen. Then we check for leaks.

    Inlet Screen and Air Filter Cleaning in Spring Valley

    Grit gets in. A small screen at the cold inlet catches sand and scale flakes, especially after a main break. Units with an air intake filter collect dust and lint, which can push the burner into a combustion code. We clean both on every visit.
