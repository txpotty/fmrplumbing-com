    Emergency Shower Repair in Spring Valley, NV

    A shower that will not shut off is an emergency. Shut the main, or use the shower's integral stops if your valve has them behind the trim or an access panel. Then call us. FMR Plumbing sends a plumber with cartridges on the truck, and we can often get your water back on within the hour.

    Same-Day Shower Leak Repair in Spring Valley, NV

    Shut off the main first if water is getting where it should not. A sudden leak behind the wall or a valve that will not close can soak framing fast, so the main valve, usually near the meter or where the line enters the house, is your first stop. Then call us. Our plumber stops the water, fixes it or makes it safe and checks for wet areas.

    Clogged Shower Drain Clearing in Spring Valley, NV

    Hair and soap scum cause nearly every slow shower drain. A hand auger through the strainer clears most of them, and a drum machine handles clogs that have moved past the trap. Drain cleaner usually misses the clog. We recommend a hair catcher once it is clear.

    Commercial Shower Repair in Spring Valley, NV

    Gyms and apartment buildings run showers dozens of times a day. Commercial valves get heavy-duty cartridges, and we schedule around your busy hours so the showers stay open when people need them. We stock tamper-resistant heads. Limit stops are set to code on each valve.

    Shower Diverter Repair in Spring Valley, NV

    Diverters send water up to the head instead of out the tub spout. When the gate in a lift-style spout wears or a three-handle diverter stem loses its washer, water keeps pouring from the spout and the shower gets weak. We replace the spout or stem. Spout replacements take about twenty minutes.

    Showerhead Repair and Replacement in Spring Valley

    Showerheads clog from the inside out. Scale builds behind the face plate and in the flow restrictor, so the spray goes uneven, shoots sideways or drops to a trickle even when the valve is wide open. Soaking helps sometimes. When it does not, we install a new head with soft rubber nozzles that you can wipe clean with a thumb.

    Leaky Shower Valve Repair in Spring Valley

    One drip every few seconds adds up to hundreds of gallons a year. In a single-handle shower the cause is almost always the cartridge or its O-rings, and on an old two-handle valve it is the stem washer and the seat. We carry the common parts. Moen, Delta, Kohler and Pfister cartridges cover most of the valves we open, so the repair is usually done that same visit.

    Dripping Showerhead Repair in Spring Valley

    Drips from the head itself almost always come from the valve, not the head. Water that keeps coming out after the handle is off has to be getting past a worn cartridge or seat, so replacing the showerhead alone will not stop it. We fix the valve. Leaks at the arm threads are different, and those get new thread tape and a snug turn.
