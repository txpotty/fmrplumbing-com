    Trenchless Sewer Line Repair in Spring Valley, NV

    Trenchless repair means we fix the pipe without opening a trench along its full length. Usually two small pits.     Root Intrusion Removal in Spring Valley, NV

    Roots enter through hairline cracks and loose joints, then grow into a mat that catches paper and grease. Cut roots regrow within a year or two.     Tree Root Invasion Solutions in Spring Valley

    Roots in a sewer line are not the tree's fault. A leaking joint invited them in. We cut them out, then talk about the long-term fix. A liner or new pipe at that joint is what actually stops regrowth.
