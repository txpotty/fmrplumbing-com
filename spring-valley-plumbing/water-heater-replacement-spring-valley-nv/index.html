    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Heater Replacement in Spring Valley

    Common signs include a lack of hot water, rust-colored water, a metallic taste in hot water, strange noises like rumbling or popping from the tank, visible leaks around the base of the unit or a water heater that is over 10 to 12 years old. If you notice any of these, it is time to call a professional.

    Manufacturer tank warranties typically range from 6 to 12 years depending on the model. We also warranty our installation labor.

    Tank water heaters often last about 8 to 12 years, and hard water and sediment buildup can shorten that. Water in much of Nevada is very hard, and scale buildup is one of the most common reasons water heaters and fixtures fail early. Annual flushing and anode rod checks can help extend that life.

    Most new water heaters come with a manufacturer's warranty ranging from 6 to 12 years on the tank and components, depending on the model and brand. FMR Plumbing also provides a labor warranty on our installation, so your investment is covered.

    A traditional water heater stores and heats a limited amount of water in a tank, whereas a tankless water heater heats water on demand as it flows through the unit. Tankless units offer endless hot water and can be more energy-efficient, but usually have a higher upfront cost and may require gas line upgrades.

    Choosing the right size depends on the number of people in your household, their hot water usage habits and peak demand times.
