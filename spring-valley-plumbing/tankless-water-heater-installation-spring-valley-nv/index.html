    Converting from a tank requires more than swapping units.     Hard Water Protection for Tankless Units in Spring Valley, NV

    Calcium scale is the leading cause of tankless heater failure. Mineral deposits crust over aerators, showerheads and valves, which is why softeners and regular water heater flushing are popular. Scale coats the heat exchanger, reducing efficiency and triggering error codes. We install isolation valves so the unit can be flushed with a vinegar or descaling solution once a year. A scale inhibitor cartridge or whole-home softener provides added protection.
