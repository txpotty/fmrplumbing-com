    What a Tank Tune-Up Covers in Spring Valley, NV

    Standard Tank Flushing in Spring Valley

    Flushing is the one job every tank needs, gas or electric, new or old. Hard water makes it more urgent. We drain, partly refill and drain again until the hose runs clear, sometimes four or five rounds. Then we close the valve and check that it reseals without a drip.

    Emergency Tank Troubleshooting in Spring Valley

    No hot water at all is usually a pilot, a gas valve, a tripped high-limit or a dead element. Most of those are quick fixes. Water on the floor is a different story, and the first question we ask is whether it is coming from a fitting on top or from under the jacket. Leaks from under the jacket mean the tank itself has failed.

    Anode Upgrades for Odor in Spring Valley, NV

    A rotten egg smell from the hot side only is almost always sulfate-reducing bacteria reacting with a magnesium anode. A different rod usually solves it. We put in an aluminum-zinc or powered anode and sanitize the tank. Powered anodes never wear out and draw very little electricity.

    Anode Rod Inspection and Replacement in Spring Valley, NV

    The anode rod is a sacrificial metal rod that corrodes so the steel tank does not. Mineral deposits clog aerators, showerheads and valves, which is why softeners and regular water heater flushing are popular. We break the hex head loose, pull the rod and check how much bare wire shows. If six inches of wire is bare, it is time for a new one.

    Maintenance Versus Replacement Costs in Spring Valley, NV

    A yearly flush costs a small fraction of a new installed tank, which often runs $1,400 to $3,000 or more depending on size and venting. We tell you which one makes sense. Past 10 to 12 years, rust at the base or a seam leak means replacement. Younger tanks with a sound liner are worth maintaining.

    Relief Valve Testing in Spring Valley

    Temperature and pressure relief valves on residential tanks are typically rated to open at 150 psi or 210 degrees, and they can stick shut after years of mineral buildup. We lift the test lever and watch it reseat. If it keeps dripping, we replace it. We also confirm the discharge pipe runs full size to within six inches of the floor or to an approved drain, which is what plumbing codes ask for.

    Commercial Tank Maintenance in Spring Valley, NV

    Restaurants, salons, laundromats and small offices run tanks much harder than a household does, often 75 to 100 gallons cycling all day. Sediment builds up two or three times faster. We usually set commercial tanks on a quarterly or twice-yearly flush, with a dated log taped to the jacket that your insurance inspector can read. Downtime gets scheduled outside business hours.

    Anode Rod Replacement in Spring Valley, NV

    Water chemistry changes from one street to the next, and so does anode life. Three to five years is typical. A softened water supply can chew through a magnesium rod in two. A new rod costs a fraction of a tank and often adds years.
