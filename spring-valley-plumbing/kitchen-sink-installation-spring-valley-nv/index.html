    How We Install Kitchen Sinks in Spring Valley, NV: The Details

    Undermount Sinks on Stone in Spring Valley, NV

    Stone shops vary on how they mount undermounts. Some epoxy threaded studs into the stone, others use a rail system, and we work with whichever one your countertop shop prefers. Either way, the bowl gets braced. We do not connect the trap until the adhesive has cured.

    Farmhouse Sink Plumbing in Spring Valley, NV

    Fireclay and cast iron farmhouse sinks can weigh well over 100 pounds empty, before a single drop of water goes in. We build a support frame inside the cabinet and set the sink level before the counter is installed. A deep bowl means a lower trap. We plan the drain for that depth from the start.

    New Construction Sink Rough-In in Spring Valley, NV

    Rough-in sets the drain height and the stops long before the cabinets arrive. We usually set the kitchen drain stub around 16 to 20 inches off the floor, then adjust for deep bowls or a disposal so the trap does not end up below the wall outlet. Vents go in now. Having the sink and faucet specs early saves moving pipe later.

    Stainless Steel Sink Installation in Spring Valley

    Stainless sinks come in gauges from 16 to 22, and the lower the number, the thicker the steel. Thin 22 gauge bowls flex and drum when the disposal runs, so we add sound pads or suggest an 18 gauge or heavier sink if you have not bought one yet. Mounting style changes the plumbing. Either way, we hook up the faucet, strainers and disposal.

    Apron-Front Sinks in Spring Valley, NV

    Apron sinks need a cabinet cut to fit the front panel, and the sink has to be in place before the counter is templated. We set the sink and frame early, so the fabricator measures from the actual bowl instead of a spec sheet. It prevents a crooked reveal. A cast iron apron sink can weigh 150 pounds or more, so two of us show up.

    Composite Sink Installation in Spring Valley

    Granite and quartz composite sinks resist scratches on the surface but can chip at the drain hole if a strainer is overtightened. Hand tight comes first. Composite makers usually call for silicone instead of plumber's putty under the strainer, since some putties stain the material, and we follow that sheet.

    Drop-In Kitchen Sink Installation in Spring Valley

    Sitting on the counter by their rim, drop-in sinks are a natural fit for laminate, butcher block and tile. Usually the fastest upgrade. We check the cutout size, seal the rim and tighten the clips evenly, and in most rentals and older homes the job is done in a couple of hours.

    Same-Day Sink Replacement in Spring Valley, NV

    A sink with a crack through the bowl or a strainer that has rusted out needs replacing, not patching. We stock the most common double-bowl stainless sizes, which makes same-day replacement common, and we replace the strainers and trap at the same time. Old stops come out too.
