    Septic Baffle and Tee Repair in Summerlin South

    Baffles and tees keep scum and solids in the tank so they do not flow out into the leach field. Concrete baffles crumble from hydrogen sulfide gas, and plastic tees can break off. When they fail, solids clog the field fast. We replace damaged baffles with durable PVC tees and add an effluent filter where appropriate.

    Emergency Septic Repair in Summerlin South

    Sewage backing up into tubs or floor drains is a health hazard that needs fast attention. We respond to emergency septic calls, find the blockage and restore flow.     Septic Inlet Line Repair in Summerlin South

    The line from your house to the tank can crack, sag or separate from soil movement and roots. Symptoms include gurgling drains and backups even after pumping. We camera the line to locate the problem and repair or replace the damaged section. A cleanout near the tank makes future maintenance easier.

    Septic Tank Lid and Riser Repair in Summerlin South

    Cracked or deteriorated concrete lids are a serious safety hazard. We replace failing lids and install risers that bring access up to grade. Risers make pumping and inspection faster and cheaper over time. Secure, child-resistant lids are standard on our installs.
