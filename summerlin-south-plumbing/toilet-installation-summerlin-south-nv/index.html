    Frequently Asked Questions About Toilet Installation in Summerlin South

    The cost of toilet installation in Summerlin South, NV typically ranges from $200 to $500, not including the toilet fixture itself. This price can vary based on factors like the type of toilet, any necessary plumbing modifications, or the difficulty of removing an old unit.

    While it's possible for a homeowner to attempt a DIY toilet installation, it's usually best left to professionals. Improper installation can lead to leaks, wobbling, inefficient flushing, and potential water damage. Professional plumbers deliver proper sealing, secure mounting, and adherence to codes.

    When choosing a new toilet, consider factors like bowl shape (round vs. elongated), height (standard vs. ADA-compliant), flushing system (gravity-fed vs. pressure-assisted), water efficiency (gallons per flush), and overall style to match your bathroom. We can help you navigate these choices.

    FMR Plumbing stands behind our workmanship. We offer a warranty on all our toilet installations, covering labor and making sure your new toilet works as expected. Specific warranty details will be provided at the time of service.

    Common signs you might need a new toilet include frequent clogs, cracks in the porcelain bowl or tank, constant running water, a wobbly base, or a toilet that is very old and inefficient. Upgrading can save water and improve the look of your bathroom.

    Toilets carry manufacturer warranties, often 1 to 5 years on parts and longer on porcelain. We also guarantee our installation workmanship and will come back to fix any leak caused by the install.

    FMR Plumbing installs all major toilet brands, including Kohler, Toto, American Standard, Delta, and Gerber. We can help you choose a model that fits your budget, style preferences, and water efficiency goals.
