    Gas Water Heater Burner and Venting Check in Summerlin South

    Anode Rod Inspection and Replacement in Summerlin South

    The anode rod is a sacrificial part in your water heater designed to corrode instead of the tank itself. Depending on your water chemistry, these rods can wear down faster than you might think. During maintenance, we inspect the anode rod's condition and recommend replacement if necessary. A healthy anode rod is key for preventing rust and extending the lifespan of your water heater tank, saving you from a costly full replacement.

    Common signs include strange noises like rumbling or popping, rusty or discolored hot water, a rotten egg smell, reduced hot water supply, or a noticeable drop in water pressure. If you observe any of these, it's time for a professional check.

    Tank water heaters commonly last about 8 to 12 years, and tankless units often last longer with regular descaling. Hard water can shorten life without maintenance.

    Sediment buildup consists of mineral deposits, mainly calcium and magnesium, that settle at the bottom of your water heater tank. Sediment cuts efficiency, causes strange noises, and can lead to premature tank failure if not flushed regularly.

    Popping or rumbling noises, lukewarm water, rusty or smelly hot water, and slow recovery times are typical signs.
