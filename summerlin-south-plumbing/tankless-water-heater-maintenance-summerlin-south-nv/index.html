    Grit gets in. The small screen on the cold inlet catches sand and loose scale after a water main repair. On units with an air intake filter, dust and lint load it up and the burner runs rich or throws a combustion code. We clean both on every visit.

    Noritz Tankless Tune-Ups in Summerlin South, NV

    Noritz builds a solid heater, but solid does not mean maintenance-free. We start a Noritz tune-up at the exhaust, checking for soot and any blocked termination.     Step-by-Step Tankless Flushing in Summerlin South

    First we shut off the gas and power, then close both isolation valves so the heater is cut off from the house plumbing.
