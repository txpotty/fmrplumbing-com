    Disposal Repair in Summerlin South, NV: Symptom by Symptom

    After-Hours Disposal Service in Summerlin South

    A disposal that quits while you are cooking stops everything, especially if the sink is full of water. Do not try drain chemicals. Drain chemicals can eat at the seals and splash on whoever opens the trap, so leave the water where it is and give us a call.

    Noisy Disposal Repair in Summerlin South

    Grinding, rattling or a hum that sounds rough usually mean trouble inside the unit. Loose mounting rings rattle too. We check the mount, then look for debris, then run the motor empty, since a whine with an empty chamber means the bearings are going.

    Leak Detection and Repair in Summerlin South, NV

    Disposal leaks come from four places: the sink flange, the dishwasher inlet, the discharge tube and the housing itself. We dry everything, run water with a little food coloring and watch to see which joint shows color first, since water runs down the outside of the unit and hides the real source. Most leak points can be repaired. A leak from the housing means it is time for a new unit.

    Flange and Inlet Leak Repair in Summerlin South, NV

    The flange, dishwasher inlet, discharge tube and body are where leaks start. We trace the leak, reseal the flange with plumber's putty and replace gaskets or tubes as needed. A leak from the bottom of the unit usually means the internal seal has failed, and in that case replacement is the better option. It is not a repairable part.

    Humming Disposal Repair in Summerlin South

    Humming with no spin almost always means a jam. Spoons, glass, bones and pits cause most of them. With the power off, we free the flywheel using the hex socket underneath, pull the object, and test the motor and reset with water running.

    Preventive Care and Longevity in Summerlin South, NV

    Keep cold water running before, during and after use, and let it go about 15 seconds after the motor stops so the food clears the trap. Grease poured in with hot water coats the chamber and the pipe, while cold water keeps it solid so the impellers can break it up. Fibrous scraps jam it most. Throw out celery, corn husks and artichoke leaves.

    Noise and Odor Diagnosis in Summerlin South

    A rattle that stops after a few seconds is usually a spoon or a pit bouncing in the chamber. A steady whine is different. That whine often comes from worn motor bearings, and once bearings start to fail the noise only gets worse until the motor seizes. Most smells start at the splash guard. The guard comes out, the chamber gets cleaned and a torn guard gets replaced.

    Cost-Effective Disposal Repair in Summerlin South, NV

    A disposal repair that costs half of a new unit rarely makes sense, and we will say so. Many fixes are cheap. A reset, a cleared jam, a new switch or a resealed flange usually costs far less than a new unit and can keep a younger disposal going for years.
