    Frequently Asked Questions About Clogged Sewer Line in Summerlin South

    The cost to clear a clogged sewer line in Summerlin South, NV can range from $250 to $700, depending on the severity of the clog, the method required (e.g., snaking vs. hydro-jetting), and whether camera inspection is needed.     While minor clogs in individual drains can sometimes be resolved with DIY methods, a main sewer line clog is best handled by professionals. Attempting to clear it yourself without the right tools can push the blockage further or damage your pipes, especially with older plumbing systems.

    Multiple drains backing up at once, gurgling toilets when the washer drains and sewage odors in the house are classic signs. Water rising in the shower when you flush a toilet is another strong clue. Wet spots or sewage smell near the cleanout outdoors can also point to a clog.

    Most main line clearings take one to two hours. Severe root intrusion or lines without a cleanout can take longer. Camera inspection adds about 30 to 60 minutes.

    Attempting to clear a main sewer line clog yourself is not recommended. Over-the-counter drain cleaners can damage your pipes, and without the right tools like sewer cameras or hydro-jetters, you risk pushing the clog further or causing more damage. Professional plumbers have the specialized equipment and experience to safely and effectively clear these blockages.
