    Jet Pump System Repair in Whitney, NV

    Jet pumps sit above ground and pull water up through a suction line with an ejector. Air leaks on the suction side cause most failures. FMR Plumbing checks the foot valve, the ejector nozzle and every suction fitting, then reprimes the pump and watches it reach cut-out pressure. Nonstop running without pressure usually means lost prime or an air leak.

    Grundfos Well Pump Repair in Whitney

    Grundfos SQ and SQE pumps have built-in electronics that protect the motor and report faults. Most Grundfos calls involve a sensor or a drive fault. FMR Plumbing reads the fault code, checks the wiring and tests the pressure sensor before replacing anything in a Whitney, NV well. Usually the electronics are fine.

    No water at all means a household on hold. FMR Plumbing handles emergency well pump calls in Whitney at the top of the schedule.     Franklin Electric Well Pump Service in Whitney, NV

    Franklin Electric motors and control boxes are in a lot of local wells. We work on Franklin motors, QD control boxes and SubDrive units regularly. Common Franklin parts are on the truck. A motor that tests bad on a megohm meter usually gets replaced rather than rewound, since a new motor costs less than shipping and rebuilding the old one.

    Low Water Pressure Diagnosis in Whitney

    Weak pressure on a well is not always the pump. Common causes include a plugged sediment filter, a low switch setting or a yard line leak. Sometimes the pump is fine and the well is simply producing less water than the house is asking for during peak use. We test each one.

    Well Pressure Tank Repair in Whitney

    Bladder tanks hold a cushion of air that lets the pump rest between cycles. When the bladder fails, the tank fills with water and the pump starts and stops every time someone opens a faucet. The bladder, the switch and the tank fittings all get checked. Proper sizing adds years to a pump.

    Control Box and Surge Repair in Whitney, NV

    Three-wire submersible pumps rely on a control box with a start capacitor and a relay. Sudden summer storms can cause flash flooding that overwhelms drainage and washes debris into storm drains. Lightning and power surges damage those parts more than anything else. We test capacitors, relays and overload protection, replace what failed and recommend a surge arrestor at the box if one is missing. An arrestor costs little.

    Well Pump Electrical Troubleshooting in Whitney, NV

    Electrical faults cause a big share of well calls, from a tripped breaker to a burned contact in the pressure switch. The control box capacitor and relay are next on the list. Our technicians test circuits with the power locked out. All wiring and splices meet Nevada electrical code before the pump restarts.
