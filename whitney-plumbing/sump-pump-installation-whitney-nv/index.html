    High Water Alarms and Smart Monitoring in Whitney

    A high water alarm is a float switch that sounds when the pit rises past normal. Wi-Fi models send a text too. Landlords like them. We add them to new installs and to pumps already in place.

    Combination Sump Pump Systems in Whitney

    Combination units pair a primary pump and a battery backup on one pre-plumbed discharge assembly. They fit pits where two separate pumps would crowd each other. That saves some labor. We set them in homes that want two layers without a bigger pit.

    Pedestal Sump Pump Installation in Whitney, NV

    Pedestal pumps put the motor on a column above the pit, so the motor never sits in water. That makes service easy. They are louder than submersibles. We set the float rod and the discharge so the pump cycles cleanly.

    New Sump Pit Installation in Whitney

    No pit yet? We cut one at the low corner. Washed gravel goes around the basin so water can reach it. The slab gets patched with new concrete. The pump, lid and discharge go in last.

    Submersible Sump Pump Installation in Whitney

    Submersible pumps live at the bottom of the pit with a sealed, oil-cooled motor. Because the motor is cooled by the surrounding water, they run quieter and can push more gallons per minute up a tall lift than most pedestal pumps. The pit lid sits flush. Each install gets a sealed lid and a grommet around the cord to keep out radon and odor.

    Commercial Sump Pump Solutions in Whitney

    Commercial basements, elevator pits and loading dock drains need pumps rated for more volume. We install duplex systems with alternating pumps. Downtime costs money. Work is scheduled around your operating hours.

    Crawl Space Sump Pump Installation in Whitney, NV

    Crawl spaces with standing water breed mold and rot sill plates within a few seasons. We set a basin at the lowest point, run perforated drain pipe to it where needed and seal the vapor barrier around the lid so the space stays dry. Clearance is usually under two feet. The discharge exits through the rim joist and drains away from the house.

    Liberty Pumps Installation in Whitney, NV
