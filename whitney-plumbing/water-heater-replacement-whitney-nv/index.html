    What Goes Into a Water Heater Replacement in Whitney, NV

    Electric Water Heater Replacement in Whitney

    Electric water heaters in Whitney usually fail from a burned-out heating element, a bad thermostat or a tank that has finally rusted through at the bottom. A bad element can be replaced. A leaking tank cannot, so if water is coming from the tank body itself, we replace the heater and match the voltage, wattage and breaker size to what your panel already supplies. Hot water is back the same day.

    Emergency Water Heater Replacement in Whitney, NV

    A leaking tank can empty itself onto the floor faster than most people expect. If water is coming from the heater, shut off the cold supply valve on top, turn the gas valve to off or flip the breaker, and open a hot tap to relieve pressure, then call and we will try to have a plumber and a new tank at your home that day. Leaks only get worse.

    Attic Water Heater Replacement in Whitney

    Much of the housing in Nevada was built in recent decades, with copper or PEX supply lines and plastic drains. Attic water heaters take more work to replace than ones in a garage or closet. Moving a 50-gallon tank through a pull-down ladder or small opening takes two people, and code wants a pan with a drain line, a platform and a light. We bring the help.

    Gas Water Heater Replacement in Whitney, NV

    Gas water heaters recover faster than electric, and in Whitney, NV homes they fail most often from a corroded tank, a bad gas valve or a burner clogged with rust flakes. We shut off the gas, disconnect and cap the line, drain the old tank and set the new one, then install a new flex connector, a sediment trap and a vent connector sloped up toward the flue. Then we leak test.

    Upsizing Your Water Heater in Whitney

    Running out of hot water often means the family has outgrown it. Recovery rate counts too.
