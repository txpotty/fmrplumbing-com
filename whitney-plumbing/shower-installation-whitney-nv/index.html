    Steam Shower Plumbing in Whitney

    Steam showers need four things: a generator, a water supply, a drain line and an electrical circuit. The generator goes within the maker's distance limit, and we plumb its supply, drain and steam line to the head. An electrician runs the circuit.     Rain and Multi-Head Shower Installation in Whitney

    Rain heads pull up to 2.5 gallons per minute each. Add a handheld and a couple of body sprays and you can be running more water than a standard 40-gallon water heater can keep hot for a long shower. The water heater gets sized up. A thermostatic valve manages each outlet.

    Walk-In Shower Installation in Whitney

    Walk-in showers depend on floor slope. Without a curb to hold water back, the floor has to pitch a quarter inch per foot toward the drain, and the waterproofing has to run well past the shower opening. The slope is our job. We add blocking for grab bars while the studs are bare.

    Accessible and ADA Shower Installation in Whitney, NV

    Accessible showers start with blocking in the wall. Blocking goes in for grab bars, the valve goes where a seated person can reach it and a handheld goes on a slide bar. Thresholds stay low or flat. We follow ADA guidelines when you ask.

    Digital shower systems like U by Moen and Kohler DTV need a little more planning.
