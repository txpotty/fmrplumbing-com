    PEX Water Line Repair in Whitney, NV

    PEX (cross-linked polyethylene) piping is a flexible, durable, and corrosion-resistant option increasingly used in homes across Whitney, NV. While PEX is less prone to leaks than older materials, damage can still occur from improper installation, rodent chewing, or impact. Our team is fully experienced in PEX repair, using specialized crimping tools and fittings to make strong, leak-proof connections. We make sure your PEX system continues to perform well.

    Copper Water Line Repair in Whitney

    Copper pipes are known for their durability and long life, but they can still develop leaks due to corrosion, excessive water pressure, or physical damage. In Whitney, we regularly see pinhole leaks in older copper lines. Our skilled plumbers are adept at repairing copper water lines, whether it involves soldering a small patch or replacing a damaged section. We make sure all repairs meet current plumbing standards for a reliable, leak-free connection.

    Pressure Regulator and Shutoff Repair in Whitney

    Many water line problems are made worse by high incoming pressure. A failed pressure reducing valve can push well over 80 psi into your plumbing, which stresses the service line and every fixture. We test static pressure with a gauge and replace worn regulators from brands like Watts and Zurn Wilkins. We also swap seized gate valves for full-port ball valves so you can shut the water off quickly in an emergency.

    Emergency Water Line Break Repair in Whitney, NV

    A full break can flood a yard, wash out soil under walkways and leave your home without water. Our emergency crews shut off the supply, locate the break and set up a temporary bypass when the repair will take more than a few hours. Most emergency breaks are repaired on the same day. We also document the damage with photos for your insurance or HOA.

    Trenchless Water Line Replacement in Whitney

    If your service line runs under a driveway, pool deck or mature landscaping, trenchless replacement can save a lot of restoration cost. We dig small pits at each end and pull new HDPE or PEX pipe through using a pneumatic boring tool or pipe pulling equipment. The process usually takes one day. You keep your pavers and trees, and the new line carries a lengthy manufacturer warranty.

    Water Pressure Restoration in Whitney

    Low water pressure can be a frustrating symptom of an underlying water line problem, such as blockages, leaks, or a failing pressure regulator. In Whitney, if your water pressure has dropped suddenly, it's important to have your main water line inspected. We diagnose the cause of low pressure, whether it's mineral buildup restricting flow or a hidden leak, and perform the needed repairs or replacements to restore strong, consistent water pressure throughout your home.

    Main Water Line Leak Repair in Whitney

    Most main line leaks show up as a soggy strip of yard, a sudden jump in your water bill or a meter dial that moves with every fixture off. We confirm the leak by isolating the house shutoff and watching the meter. Then we use ground microphones to find the exact spot. A single section is cut out and replaced with repair couplings or a new length of pipe, and the line is pressure tested before we backfill.

    Underground Water Line Leak Repair in Whitney

    Underground water line leaks are often hard to detect until significant damage has occurred. Common signs include sudden spikes in your water bill, damp spots in your yard, or a clear drop in water pressure. Our team uses acoustic leak detection and ground-penetrating radar to precisely locate these hidden leaks in Whitney, minimizing the need for extensive excavation. We repair breaks caused by shifting soil, tree root intrusion, or aging pipes, restoring your water service quickly.
