    Resin Tank and Media Replacement in Whitney, NV

    Softener resin can break down after 10 to 15 years, especially with chlorinated city water. When resin fails, you will see hard water symptoms even though the system is regenerating. Sometimes replacing resin and the tank is more cost effective than a whole new system. We inspect the valve and brine tank to decide. If the valve is old, a full replacement often makes more sense.

    Salt-Free Water Conditioner Replacement in Whitney, NV

    For homeowners in Whitney, NV looking for an alternative to traditional salt-based systems, salt-free water conditioners offer a different approach. These units, sometimes called descalers, condition water to prevent mineral scale without removing the minerals or adding salt. While they do not produce soft feeling water, they effectively protect pipes and appliances from scale. We can replace your existing salt-free system or help you transition from a salt-based unit.

    Salt-Free Conditioner Alternatives in Whitney, NV

    Some homeowners prefer a salt-free scale reduction system that uses template-assisted crystallization media. These systems do not remove hardness minerals but change them so they are less likely to stick. They require no drain and no salt. We explain the differences honestly, since they work differently than true softeners. They can be a good fit when drain access is limited or sodium is a concern.
