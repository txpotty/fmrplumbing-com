    Wall-Hung Toilet Replacement in Whitney

    Wall-hung toilets mount to a steel carrier inside the wall, so replacing one means checking the carrier bolts, the gasket on the outlet and the condition of the wall finish around it.     Commercial Toilet Replacement in Whitney, NV

    Cracked Toilet Replacement in Whitney

    Porcelain cracks below the waterline are not safely repairable, and epoxy fixes on a tank under constant water pressure rarely last more than a few months. It needs to be replaced.
