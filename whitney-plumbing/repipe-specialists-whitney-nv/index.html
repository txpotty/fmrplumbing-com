    Slab Leak Reroutes in Whitney

    When one line under the slab leaks, a full repipe is not always the first step.     Copper Repiping in Whitney, NV

    Some homeowners prefer copper for its long track record and rigidity. Copper repipes usually cost more and take a day longer than PEX, mostly because every turn needs a fitting.

    Slab Leak Repiping in Whitney

    A slab leak shows up as a warm spot on the floor, a running meter or a wet patch of carpet with no visible cause. Caliche, expansive clay and rocky desert soil are common in Nevada, and caliche can make digging for sewer lines slow and costly. Rather than jackhammer to reach one leak, we run new lines through the attic and walls and leave the old under-slab pipe out of service. No jackhammer needed. That stops the current leak and removes the chance of the next one under the same floor.

    PEX Versus Copper Repipe Pricing in Whitney, NV

    PEX repipes usually cost less than copper because the material is cheaper and installation goes faster. Copper costs more. For a typical house, copper often runs 20 to 40 percent above PEX, though some buyers and homeowners like the look and feel of metal pipe and are willing to pay for it. Both prices include permits, fittings, patching and cleanup.

    PEX-A Whole-Home Repiping in Whitney

    PEX-A is flexible, resists scale and handles thermal expansion well, which suits hard water and long attic runs. We use Uponor or similar PEX-A with cold expansion fittings that open the pipe end and let it shrink back over the fitting for a full-bore joint. Fewer joints, fewer leaks. Two or three days covers most houses.
