    What We Fix on Faucets in Whitney, NV

    Cartridge Faucet Repair in Whitney, NV

    Single-handle faucets from Moen, Delta, Kohler and Pfister mostly use replaceable cartridges, and a worn one shows up as a drip, a stiff handle or water that will not hold a steady temperature. Each brand uses its own part number. We match it. Swapping a common cartridge like the Moen 1225 takes about half an hour, cleanup included.

    Bathroom Pop-Up Drain Repair in Whitney

    Pop-up drains on bathroom sinks fail at the pivot ball, the clevis strap, the lift rod or the stopper itself, and a slow drain often turns out to be hair wrapped around the stopper. Try lifting out the stopper first. A new pop-up assembly goes in, matched to your faucet finish when the old one is corroded or the pivot leaks.

    Bathroom Faucet Repair in Whitney, NV

    Bathroom sinks see less wear than kitchen sinks, but scale still builds up in cartridges, and pop-up drains break at the pivot rod under the sink. Both are quick fixes. Cartridges get matched for widespread, centerset and single-hole faucets, and pop-up assemblies are replaced to fit the finish.

    Low Water Flow Faucet Repair in Whitney

    Sputtering flow, where the water spits and surges, often means air or debris in the line after a water main repair, or a partly clogged cartridge screen. Flushing usually clears it. Pull-down faucets also have check valves in the hose that can stick, and we clean or replace those.

    Faucet Handle Repair and Replacement in Whitney, NV

    Stripped handle splines, cracked handle bodies, worn adapters and loose set screws make a faucet hard to turn or impossible to shut tight. Many brands sell the handle on its own. The replacement handle gets matched to both the stem and the finish, and if the stem itself is stripped we replace it at the same time.

    Disc Faucet Repair in Whitney

    Ceramic disc faucets last a long time, but grit from a water main repair or scale from hard water can scratch the discs or wear out the rubber inlet seals underneath. Inlet seals cost very little. A scratched disc means a new cartridge, since the two discs are lapped to fit each other and cannot be resurfaced.

    Tub Spout and Diverter Repair in Whitney, NV

    Water still running from the tub spout while the shower is on means the diverter is worn, either the gate inside the spout or a diverter stem in the valve. There are slip-on and threaded spouts. The pipe stub gets measured so we bring the right spout, and the fix gives you full pressure at the showerhead again.

    Hard Water Faucet Repair in Whitney

    Scale is the biggest reason faucet seals wear early, because the mineral crust scratches rubber and keeps ceramic discs from closing flat. Hard water speeds it up. We clean the valve body when the old part comes out, so the new cartridge or washer seals against a smooth surface.
