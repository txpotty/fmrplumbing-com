    Backflow Preventer Testing in Whitney

    Backflow preventers protect the drinking water supply from contamination, and many water providers require annual testing for irrigation and commercial devices. Our certified testers check each valve and file results with your water utility as required. If a device fails, we repair or replace the internal parts. We also check that devices are protected from heat and freezing where necessary. Keeping up with testing avoids fines and water shutoffs.

    Tankless Water Heater Maintenance in Whitney

    Tankless water heaters need specialized maintenance, mainly descaling to remove mineral buildup from the heat exchanger. This is particularly important where the water is hard. Our technicians use approved descaling solutions to keep your tankless unit running at peak efficiency, preventing reduced hot water flow and possible breakdowns. We also check gas lines, ventilation, and water connections.

    Water Softener Maintenance in Whitney

    Water softeners fight hard water, protecting your pipes and appliances from mineral scale. Our maintenance service includes checking salt levels, cleaning the brine tank, confirming the regeneration cycle works correctly, and inspecting valves and resin beds. Proper softener maintenance prolongs its life and keeps soft water flowing to your home, preventing costly scale damage.

    Hot Water Tank Maintenance in Whitney, NV

    Whether gas or electric, your hot water tank benefits greatly from annual maintenance. We check burner operation, flue venting, and potential gas leaks on gas models, and inspect electrical connections and heating elements on electric units. Sediment flushing is a key part of our service. It removes mineral deposits that can reduce heating efficiency and lead to early tank failure.
