    Preventive Copper Pipe Inspection in Whitney, NV

    An inspection catches thin spots before they leak. It takes about an hour.     Hot Water Copper Line Repair in Whitney, NV

    Hot water lines corrode faster than cold lines. Temperature drives it. Recirculation pumps that run around the clock push water through the copper at high velocity and wear it from the inside, so we repair leaking hot lines and can add a timer or adjust the pump to cut down on wear. Less run time, less wear.

    Burst Copper Pipe Repair in Whitney, NV

    Copper in an exterior wall or attic can split when the water inside freezes. The northern part of the state sees regular hard freezes, so pipes in exterior walls and crawlspaces need protection.
