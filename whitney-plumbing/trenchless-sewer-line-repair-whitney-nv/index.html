    Pipe Bursting Sewer Line Replacement in Whitney

    Pipe bursting is a trenchless method used for full sewer line replacement. It involves pulling a new pipe through the old one while breaking apart the existing pipe. This creates a seamless, durable replacement, often using high-density polyethylene pipe, which is highly resistant to corrosion and root intrusion, common problems in older Whitney homes. This method is ideal when the existing pipe is severely damaged or collapsed.

    Root Intrusion Repair in Whitney, NV

    Tree and shrub roots seek out moisture in sewer joints. Once inside, they grow into dense masses that cause repeat backups. We cut roots with a mechanical cutter or hydro jet, then line the pipe to seal the joints. That breaks the cycle of cleaning the line every couple of months.

    Sewer Line Video Inspection in Whitney

    Before any trenchless repair, we perform a thorough video inspection of your sewer line. This involves inserting a high-resolution camera into the pipe to accurately diagnose the problem's exact location and nature. This crucial step makes sure we choose the most appropriate and effective trenchless method for your Whitney property, avoiding guesswork.

    Sectional Point Repair in Whitney

    When only one section of a line is damaged, a point repair can fix it without lining the whole length. We position a short resin liner at the damaged spot using the camera and inflate it until it cures. Point repairs are a cost-effective fix for a single crack, hole or root entry. They are common on otherwise healthy lines.
