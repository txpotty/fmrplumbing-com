    Sump Pump Maintenance in Whitney, NV: What We Check

    Crawlspace and Window Well Pumps in Whitney, NV

    Not every sump sits in a basement. Plenty of crawlspaces and deep window wells use a small pump to clear storm runoff. Our plumber clears the screen, tests the float and traces the discharge to daylight or an approved drain, since a line that ends under the house only relocates the water. Freezing weather is uncommon in the southern desert of Nevada, though occasional cold nights can still damage exposed outdoor pipes.

    Home Sump Pump Maintenance in Whitney

    A typical house pit is about 18 inches across and two feet deep, and most of what goes wrong happens inside it. We pull the pump, clear the intake screen and scoop out the silt. After that, the pump gets a real water test. Most homes sit on concrete slabs, so leaks in buried supply lines often show up as warm spots on the floor or higher water bills.

    Check Valve and Discharge Pipe Repair in Whitney, NV

    A bad check valve can nearly double how often the pump starts. Every startup puts heat into the motor. Our plumbers replace worn valves with quieter spring-loaded types and check the pipe for cracks and loose couplings out to where it leaves the house. The water should exit well away from the slab or foundation so it does not soak back toward the pit.

    Discharge Line and Check Valve Service in Whitney

    The check valve sits on the discharge pipe a foot or so above the pump and keeps the water in the line from falling back into the pit. When it fails, the pump pumps the same water twice. Motors wear out faster. We test the valve, look for a weep hole between the pump and the valve, and check every joint up to the wall.

    Commercial Sump Pump Maintenance in Whitney, NV

    Commercial pits are often bigger and deeper, and many have two pumps on an alternating controller. Each pump gets tested, along with the alternator and the high-water alarm. You get a written record of each reading, which comes in handy for insurance renewals and inspections. Quarterly visits are common.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Maintenance in Whitney, NV

    Backup pumps sit in the pit next to the main pump and run off a 12-volt battery when the power drops or the primary fails. We check the battery with a load tester, not just a voltmeter. Then we unplug the main pump and let the backup empty the pit on its own. Alarms get tested too.

    Float Switch Inspection in Whitney, NV

    The float switch tells the pump when to start and stop, and a float with too little travel between on and off makes the pump short cycle dozens of times an hour. Our plumber watches the float rise and drop through a complete cycle. Then we set the on and off points so the pump runs longer, less often.

    Backup Pump Battery Service in Whitney

    Outages and heavy rain tend to arrive together. The backup takes over when the primary loses power or simply stops. Our plumber load tests the battery, tests the charger and alarm, then runs the backup alone with the primary unplugged. Most batteries need replacing every three to five years.

    Sump Pump Maintenance Near Me in Whitney, NV

    Water pools in predictable places around a house: the downspout corners, the low side of the yard and wherever the grade slopes back toward the wall. We look at all of that while we are outside checking the discharge. Small grading fixes help the pump.

    Low spots on a block flood first, and our crews know which Whitney streets see standing water after a hard storm. It helps us judge how far a discharge line needs to run. Pumps in low lots cycle far more often.
