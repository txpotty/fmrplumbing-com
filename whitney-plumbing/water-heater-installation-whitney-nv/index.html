    Water Heater Installs in Whitney, NV

    Gas Tank Water Heater Installation in Whitney

    A gas tank is the most common water heater we install, in 40, 50 and 75 gallon sizes. Every job gets a fresh connector, a drip leg, a shutoff and a vent connector with the right rise toward the chimney or flue. We test draft before leaving. Big families do well with gas.

    Tankless Water Heater Installation in Whitney, NV

    Going tankless means the heater fires only when a tap opens, and it never runs out. Gas tankless models usually need a bigger gas line than the old tank had, plus a dedicated vent run through the wall or roof. They cost more to install. We check gas capacity before quoting.

    Gas Water Heater Replacement in Whitney

    Swapping a gas water heater comes down to three connections done right, the gas, the vent and the water. Each gas swap gets a new connector, a drip leg, a draft test at the hood and a leak check with a detector at every fitting. Venting is what keeps CO out. All work follows local code.

    Electric Tank Water Heater Installation in Whitney, NV

    Without gas service, an electric tank is the usual pick. We match the voltage and breaker size to your panel, and because electric tanks need no vent, they can go in a closet, an attic or a laundry room where a gas unit could not. A drain pan goes under closet installs. Quiet and simple.

    Water Heater Installation Cost in Whitney, NV

    Up front, a tank heater costs less than tankless. Gas tankless installs often run higher because of the bigger gas line and new venting, and attic jobs, relocations or code upgrades add labor on top of that. Permits are included. You also get an estimate of yearly energy cost for each option.

    Commercial Water Heater Installation in Whitney

    Restaurants, salons and offices burn through hot water at rates no household tank can keep up with. We install commercial tanks over 100,000 BTU and racked tankless systems, with the venting and gas supply those loads require. Downtime costs you money. Night installs are available.

    Attic and Closet Water Heater Installs in Whitney

    Attic and closet installs take more planning than a garage swap. An attic heater needs a pan with its own drain line, a properly routed relief line and enough access to get the old tank out and the new one in. A leak lands on drywall. If moving it makes sense, we can relocate the heater.

    Electric Water Heater Installation in Whitney, NV

    Electric tanks need a 240-volt circuit, usually a 30-amp double-pole breaker with 10-gauge wire. Before hooking up the new tank we check that the breaker and wire size match the elements, since a mismatch is a common cause of tripped breakers and scorched terminals. No venting needed. You get more spots to put it.
