    PEX Pipe Repair and Installation in Reno

    PEX is flexible and handles freezing better than rigid pipe. Fittings let go.     Galvanized Pipe Replacement in Reno

    Older homes were plumbed with galvanized steel, which rusts from the inside out. Pressure fades over years. Every time a fitting is disturbed, the next one tends to leak, which makes full replacement of the affected run the smarter spend. We use dielectric unions at transitions.

    Slab Leak Pipe Repair in Reno, NV

    Slab leaks start when pipe under the concrete wears through or gets stressed by soil movement. Caliche, expansive clay and rocky desert soil are common in Nevada, and caliche can make digging for sewer lines slow and costly. A hot spot underfoot is common. We isolate the leaking line with pressure tests and find it with acoustic and thermal equipment, then choose between a small slab cut at the leak and rerouting the line through the walls or attic. We quote each way.

    PEX and CPVC Pipe Repair in Reno, NV

    Plastic supply pipe like PEX and CPVC is common in homes built over the last few decades. Both have weak points. PEX can fail at crimp rings, older brass fittings or where it was exposed to UV light in an attic, while CPVC turns brittle with age and heat and cracks when bumped, so we replace failed fittings and damaged sections with parts matched to each material. Brittle CPVC needs a gentle hand.
