    Septic Repair Costs Compared in Reno, NV

    Baffle or tee replacement often runs $300 to $900.     Septic Lid and Riser Repair in Reno

    Old concrete lids crack, and a cracked lid over a 1,000-gallon tank is a real danger to kids and pets. We replace failing lids and add risers that bring access up to the lawn. Future pumping gets cheaper. Each lid we install gets screws or a lock so a child cannot lift it.

    Inlet and Outlet Baffle Replacement in Reno, NV

    Baffles hold back the scum layer on top and the sludge on the bottom so only clear effluent leaves the tank. Concrete ones corrode from the acid that sewer gas forms and eventually fall off into the tank. With no outlet baffle, sludge flows right out to the leach lines. We replace them with PVC sanitary tees. Usually a short job.

    Emergency Septic Overflow in Reno

    Raw sewage coming up through a tub drain carries bacteria you do not want on your skin or your floors. Keep everyone out of the area and shut off water use. Our tech clears the line or calls in a pump truck, depending on what we find. Cleanup guidance comes with it.

    Septic Line Clog Clearing in Reno

    A slow toilet and a gurgle in the tub usually point to a clog between the house and the tank. We run a camera down the line first, then clear it with a jetter sized for 4-inch sewer pipe. Grease and wipes cause most of these, and what we do next depends on what comes out of the pipe. Roots mean a joint has opened.

    Septic Code and Permit Work in Reno, NV

    New tanks and new fields need a permit from the local health authority in most of the country, along with any change to the system's layout. We draw the site plan and file the paperwork, then book the inspector before anything is covered. Small fixes like a tee usually skip the permit. The inspector's sign-off goes in your file.

    Septic Alarm Troubleshooting in Reno

    A septic alarm sounding is the system's way of saying the water is too high in the pump chamber or the aerobic unit. Hit the mute switch and go easy on the water. We check whether the pump runs and whether a float is hung up on its cord. A tripped breaker is the cheapest answer.

    Aerobic and Alternative System Repair in Reno, NV

    Aerobic treatment units use an air pump to feed oxygen to the bacteria, and when that pump dies the unit starts to smell within days. Our techs rebuild linear aerators and swap rotary ones, and we clear diffusers that have plugged with sludge. Timers and panels get tested too. We also keep the service log your permit may require.
