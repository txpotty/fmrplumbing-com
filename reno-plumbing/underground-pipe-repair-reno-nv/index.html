    Frequently Asked Questions About Underground Pipe Repair in Reno

    The cost of underground pipe repair in Reno, NV can vary widely, typically ranging from $500 for minor spot repairs to $3,000 to $10,000 or more for trenchless full line replacements, or even more for extensive excavation and re-piping. Factors include the pipe material, depth, length of the damaged section, the repair method chosen and the extent of property restoration needed. We provide transparent, upfront pricing after a thorough diagnosis.

    Common signs include a water bill that jumps without explanation, a meter that moves with all water off, low pressure and wet or sinking spots in the yard. A hissing sound near the meter box can also point to a leak.

    FMR Plumbing is equipped to repair a wide range of underground pipe materials, including PVC, ABS, cast iron, copper, PEX and even older materials like clay or polybutylene. Our technicians are trained on the specific repair techniques for each material.

    For water lines we typically use PEX or Type K copper, both approved for direct burial. For sewer lines we use Schedule 40 PVC. The right choice depends on the existing pipe and local code.

    The duration of an underground pipe repair depends heavily on the severity and location of the damage, as well as the repair method chosen. Minor repairs using trenchless techniques might be completed in a single day. More extensive issues, such as full sewer line replacements requiring excavation, could take two to five days, including restoration work.

    Many water utilities offer a one-time leak adjustment if you repair the leak promptly and provide proof. We give you an invoice and repair documentation to support your request.

    Ignoring an underground pipe leak can lead to serious problems, including costly water damage to your foundation, landscaping erosion, mold growth, higher water bills and potential contamination of your water supply. It's crucial to address these issues promptly.

    FMR Plumbing uses high-quality, industry-approved materials from reputable brands known for durability and longevity. This includes PEX piping from manufacturers like Uponor or Viega, and various PVC and ABS pipe products that meet ASTM standards.
