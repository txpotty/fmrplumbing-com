    Common Toilet Repairs in Reno, NV

    Fill Valve Replacement in Reno

    Old ballcock valves with a brass arm and a big plastic float are loud and slow to refill, and they tend to stick open when scale builds up in the ports. We swap them for an adjustable fill valve from Fluidmaster or Korky and set the height so the critical level mark sits at least an inch above the overflow. It is a twenty-minute job. You will notice the difference the first night, because the tank stops hissing.

    Emergency Toilet Repair in Reno, NV

    An overflowing bowl in a one-bathroom house is an emergency, and so is a burst supply line spraying water across the floor at full house pressure. Shut the angle stop first. Turn the valve on the wall behind the toilet clockwise until it is tight, then call us so we can walk you through the rest. We stop the leak on the first visit and fix it for good when parts allow.

    Flush Mechanism Repair in Reno, NV

    A weak flush usually means the tank is not filling to the fill line, the chain has too much slack, or the flapper is closing before the tank empties. We set the water level to the mark on the overflow tube and adjust the chain to about half an inch of slack. Handles break too. A cracked handle arm or a stripped mounting nut takes about five minutes to replace.

    Running Toilet Repair in Reno

    A tank that keeps hissing and refilling every few minutes is usually leaking past the flapper or overfilling into the overflow tube. It adds up fast. We look at the chain, float, flapper and seat before touching a part, then replace only what failed. Figure on thirty minutes for a typical running toilet, longer if the flush valve seat is pitted.

    Commercial Toilet Repair in Reno

    Office and retail restrooms take more flushes in a day than a house sees in a month, so the parts wear faster and fail harder. Commercial bowls usually run on flushometer valves rather than tanks, and those need diaphragm kits and vacuum breakers from time to time. We carry Sloan and Zurn kits. If downtime is a problem, we can come in before you open or after you lock up.

    Cracked Tank and Supply Line Repair in Reno, NV

    Braided supply lines last years, but the plastic nut on the toilet end can crack and a bulge in the braid means the inner hose is about to burst. Every visit includes a look at the supply line. Bulges get replaced on the spot. If you still have a vinyl or solid plastic line, we change it to braided stainless before it lets go.

    Sensor and Flushometer Repair in Reno

    Sensor flush valves fail in their own ways, from dead batteries and dirty sensor eyes to solenoids that stick open and keep a bowl running. Before replacing parts, we test the sensor and the solenoid. Batteries are the cheap fix. Ghost flushing on a sensor valve usually means the eye needs adjusting or cleaning.

    Running Toilet Fixes in Reno

    Phantom refills, the tank topping itself off for a few seconds in the middle of the night, almost always mean a slow leak at the flapper. The water level drops until the fill valve kicks on again. Sometimes the cause is a refill tube pushed too far down the overflow, which siphons the tank. We clip it above the water line.
