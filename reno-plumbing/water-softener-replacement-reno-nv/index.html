    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Softener Replacement in Reno

    The cost of water softener replacement in Reno, NV varies based on the type of system, its capacity and installation complexity. In most cases, homeowners can expect to pay between $1,500 and $4,000 for a quality unit and professional installation. We provide clear, upfront quotes after assessing your needs.

    We install systems with Fleck, Clack and Kinetico valves, among others. These brands have good parts availability and reliable performance.

    Spots on dishes, scale on faucets, dry skin and increased soap use can point to a failing softener. A softener that no longer uses salt or keeps flooding the brine tank may also need replacement.

    In many areas, a plumbing permit is usually not required for a direct like-for-like water softener replacement if no significant changes to the existing plumbing are made. However, if new water lines or drain lines are being installed or relocated, a permit may be required. We will advise you on any permitting requirements.

    Most softener replacements take two to four hours. New loops or drain lines can take a bit longer.

    Salt-free conditioners reduce scale buildup but do not remove hardness minerals. A true softener removes them, which is better for soap and fixtures.

    New water softeners typically come with manufacturer warranties ranging from 1 to 10 years on different components, such as the control valve and resin tank. FMR Plumbing also provides a labor warranty on our installation work for your peace of mind. Exact warranty details will be provided with your quote.

    Salt-based softeners remove hardness minerals, calcium and magnesium, through ion exchange, truly softening the water. Salt-free conditioners do not remove minerals but alter their crystal structure so they do not stick to surfaces. That prevents scale buildup without actually softening the water.
