    Air Switch Disposal Installation in Reno

    An air switch puts a push button on the countertop or sink deck instead of a wall switch. The button pushes a puff of air down a thin tube.     Standard InSinkErator Installation in Reno

    A standard install goes into a sink that already has a drain and power. We take the old disposal down, scrape the old putty off the flange and set a new flange with fresh putty. Then the mount ring goes on. We hang the unit, turn the lower ring until it locks and connect the discharge tube to the trap with a new slip-joint washer so nothing weeps. We connect power through a cord kit or the old hardwire and confirm the ground first.

    Upgrading Your InSinkErator in Reno, NV
