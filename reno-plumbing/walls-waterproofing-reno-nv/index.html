    Exterior Foundation Waterproofing in Reno

    Exterior foundation waterproofing involves excavating around the foundation to apply a waterproof membrane or coating directly to the wall. This is often the most reliable method for preventing water from entering the basement or crawl space. We use high-quality asphalt-modified polyurethane membranes or polymer-based coatings that resist hydrostatic pressure and soil movement. Clay soils that swell when watered and shrink when dry can shift slabs and buried drain lines over time. Proper drainage solutions, such as French drains, are also integrated to divert water away.

    Plumbing Leak Behind Walls in Reno

    A slow drip from a shower valve, supply stub or drain fitting can soak a wall for months before you notice. We locate the leak with moisture meters, thermal imaging and pressure tests. The damaged section of pipe or valve is replaced, and wet drywall is cut out and dried. Once the cavity is dry, we restore the wall and seal any wet-area surfaces.

    Crawl Space Encapsulation in Reno

    Crawl space encapsulation involves sealing the crawl space entirely with heavy-duty vapor barriers and often includes a dehumidifier. This not only waterproofs the walls but also controls humidity, preventing mold growth and wood rot in the crawl space. Water heaters in hot garages wear out sooner, and outdoor pipes and irrigation lines take a beating from sun and heat. An encapsulated crawl space can noticeably improve indoor air quality and energy efficiency by keeping moisture from moving up into the home. We use durable, reinforced poly sheeting for walls and floors, sealed at all seams and penetrations.

    Drainage System Integration in Reno, NV

    Effective walls waterproofing often requires a robust drainage system to divert water away from your foundation. This can include interior perimeter drains that channel water to a sump pump, or exterior French drains that collect surface water. Our team designs and installs custom drainage solutions for Reno, NV properties, so water is efficiently managed and moved away from your home's vulnerable areas.

    Interior Walls Waterproofing in Reno, NV

    Interior walls waterproofing focuses on managing water that has already passed through the foundation walls. This typically involves installing an interior drainage system, often called a French drain, along the perimeter of the basement floor. Water is collected and channeled to a sump pump, which then sends it safely away from the foundation. While not preventing water from entering the wall, it effectively stops it from pooling inside your living space. This solution is less disruptive than exterior excavation and works well where exterior access is limited.

    Crawl Space Vapor Barriers in Reno

    Many homes in Reno have crawl spaces, which are highly susceptible to moisture issues. Installing a heavy-duty vapor barrier on crawl space walls and floors is a key waterproofing strategy. This thick polyethylene sheeting stops ground moisture from evaporating into the crawl space, protecting subflooring and joists and improving indoor air quality. It's an effective way to control humidity and prevent wood rot.

    Waterproofing for Older Homes in Reno, NV

    Older homes, especially those built before modern waterproofing standards, are particularly prone to water intrusion. Much of the housing in Nevada was built in recent decades, with copper or PEX supply lines and plastic drains. Many older foundations may lack adequate exterior drainage or proper sealing. We specialize in assessing and upgrading waterproofing systems for these properties, often combining exterior excavation with interior drainage solutions. We respect the existing structure while installing modern, effective waterproofing, with attention to the challenges of older brick and stone foundations.

    Foundation Crack Repair & Sealing in Reno

    Even minor cracks in foundation walls can allow significant water intrusion. Our services include crack repair using epoxy or polyurethane injection to seal these entry points for good. This method fills the entire depth of the crack, restoring strength and preventing water penetration. We address both vertical and horizontal cracks, knowing that horizontal cracks can indicate structural issues that need a thorough assessment before sealing. Addressing these early prevents further water damage and costly future repairs.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Walls Waterproofing in Reno

    The cost of walls waterproofing in Reno, NV can vary a lot, typically ranging from $3,000 for interior crack sealing to $15,000 or more for extensive exterior foundation waterproofing with drainage systems. Factors like the size of the area, severity of water damage, chosen methods, and accessibility all affect the final price. We provide detailed, transparent quotes after a thorough inspection.

    The duration of a walls waterproofing project varies significantly. Smaller jobs like interior crack sealing might take a day or two, while extensive exterior waterproofing with excavation and drainage system installation could take several days to over a week. We'll provide a clear timeline during your consultation.

    For extensive walls waterproofing projects involving excavation, structural changes, or major changes to drainage systems, a permit from the city of Reno may be required. Simple crack repairs typically do not need a permit. FMR Plumbing will help you determine if a permit is necessary for your project and assist with the application process if needed.

    While minor DIY fixes like patching small cracks are possible, complete walls waterproofing is best left to professionals. It involves specialized knowledge of drainage, foundation mechanics, and proper material application. Improper DIY work can lead to ineffective results, wasted money, and worse water damage over time.

    Key signs you need walls waterproofing include damp or wet basement walls, visible mold or mildew growth, a musty odor in your basement or crawl space, efflorescence on concrete walls, cracks in the foundation, or standing water after heavy rains. Don't ignore these indicators, as they often point to ongoing water intrusion.

    Sprinklers and drip lines aimed at the house are the most common cause. Heavy storms and poor grading also send water toward walls. Masonry absorbs that water and leaves mineral deposits as it dries.

    Waterproofing prevents the moisture mold needs to grow. Existing mold should be removed and the area dried before sealing. We remove affected materials when needed and recommend remediation for heavier growth.

    Look for signs such as damp or wet spots on basement or crawl space walls, a musty odor, visible mold or mildew growth, efflorescence, a white powdery residue, on concrete or brick, and standing water. Cracks in foundation walls or bowing walls also point to potential water entry issues. If you notice any of these, it's wise to get a professional inspection.
