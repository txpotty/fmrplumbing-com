    Repipe Costs Versus Leak Repairs in Reno, NV

    A whole-home PEX repipe for an average house often runs $5,000 to $12,000, with copper costing more. A single slab leak repair can run $1,000 to $4,000 once access and restoration are included.     Repiping for Hard Water in Reno, NV

    Water in much of Nevada is very hard, and scale buildup is one of the most common reasons water heaters and fixtures fail early. Hard water leaves calcium and magnesium scale inside pipe, and over years it narrows the bore and speeds up corrosion in metal lines. Pressure suffers. For homes with hard water, we usually recommend PEX-A, which resists scale and does not corrode, and we can add a softener loop or a whole-house filter during the repipe. Adding the loop now costs less than later.

    Commercial Repiping in Reno, NV

    Commercial buildings in Reno, NV often have larger pipe, risers that serve several floors and tenants who cannot lose water for a full day. Materials are usually Type L copper or commercial PEX, sized to the fixture count and installed to commercial code.

    Galvanized Pipe Replacement for Older Homes in Reno, NV

    Older homes may still have galvanized steel pipe that rusts from the inside out. Weak flow and rusty water are common. Old galvanized gets removed or abandoned and replaced with PEX or copper, and most homes see better pressure and clearer water right away.

    Galvanized Pipe Replacement in Reno, NV

    Galvanized steel rusts from the inside, and over decades the inside diameter closes down to a fraction of what it was. Pressure drops slowly. Rust-colored water in the morning, poor pressure upstairs and dripping threaded joints all point to failing galvanized. New PEX or copper takes its place. Most homeowners notice the pressure difference the first time they turn on the shower.

    Whole-Home Copper Repiping in Reno

    Copper is the traditional choice, and Type L copper has a thicker wall than the Type M that went into many older homes. Type L tolerates UV, stays rigid and has decades of field history. It costs more. Our plumbers solder joints with lead-free solder or use press fittings, support the pipe at the spacing the code calls for and pressure test the system before any wall is closed.

    PEX Versus Copper Repipe Pricing in Reno, NV

    PEX repipes usually cost less than copper because the material is cheaper and installation goes faster. Copper costs more. For a typical house, copper often runs 20 to 40 percent above PEX, though some buyers and homeowners like the look and feel of metal pipe and are willing to pay for it. Our quotes for either include permit, fittings, patching and cleanup.
