    Slab Leak Repiping Solutions in Reno

    Slab leaks are a costly and damaging problem for homes built on concrete foundations. Most homes sit on concrete slabs, so leaks in buried supply lines often show up as damp areas on the floor or higher water bills. These leaks often require rerouting or repiping entire sections of your plumbing system to bypass the damaged pipes under the slab. Our repipe specialists use advanced detection tools to pinpoint slab leaks and then reroute new PEX or copper lines through your attic or walls, completely bypassing the problematic underground sections. This prevents further foundation damage and restores water service without breaking your slab.

    Repiping for Hard Water Areas in Reno, NV

    Homes around Reno that rely on private wells should test for hardness and other minerals before installing treatment equipment. Water in many areas contains minerals that cause scale buildup inside pipes over time. This hard water scale narrows the pipe's diameter, reduces water pressure, and can speed up corrosion, especially in galvanized and older copper pipes. Our repipe specialists consider local water conditions, recommending materials like PEX or properly installed copper that are more resistant to mineral buildup, ensuring a longer-lasting plumbing system.

    Slab Leak Reroutes in Reno
