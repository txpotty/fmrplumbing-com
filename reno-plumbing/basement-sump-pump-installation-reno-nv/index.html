    Frequently Asked Questions About Basement Sump Pump Installation in Reno

    Replacing a pump in an existing pit typically runs $500 to $1,200 including the pump and check valve. Installing a new pit through the slab generally falls between $1,500 and $3,500, and adding a battery backup adds about $600 to $1,500.

    Replacing a pump usually does not need a permit, but new pits, new discharge routes or new electrical circuits may. We check local requirements and pull permits when they are needed.

    We recommend having your sump pump inspected and maintained annually, ideally before the rainy season. Regular maintenance includes checking the float switch, cleaning debris out of the pit, testing the check valve and confirming the discharge line is clear. This helps prevent unexpected failures and extends the life of your pump.

    Usually, standard home insurance policies do not cover a new sump pump installation or repairs unless the damage was caused by a sudden, accidental event like a burst pipe, rather than gradual wear and tear or groundwater seepage. Some policies offer separate water backup or sump pump failure endorsements for an extra premium. It is best to check with your insurance provider.

    Sudden summer storms can cause fast-moving floodwater that overwhelms drainage and washes debris into storm drains. Because storms and power outages tend to arrive together, a battery backup sump pump is highly recommended. It provides crucial protection by switching on automatically when the main power fails, so your basement stays dry even without electricity. It is a smart investment for peace of mind.

    We install a wide range of high-quality, reliable sump pump brands, including Zoeller, Wayne, Little Giant and Liberty Pumps. We can help you choose the best brand and model for your budget and water management needs in your Reno home, considering horsepower and features.

    A submersible sump pump sits entirely within the sump pit, making it quieter and less obtrusive. A pedestal sump pump has its motor above the pit, making it easier to access for maintenance and often more affordable. Both types are effective for removing water from your basement.

    FMR Plumbing provides a warranty on our labor for basement sump pump installations, typically for one year. The sump pump itself comes with a manufacturer's warranty, which can range from 1 to 5 years depending on the brand and model. We will give you all warranty details at the time of installation.
