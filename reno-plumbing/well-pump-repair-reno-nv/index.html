    Jet Pump Repair in Reno, NV

    Jet pumps, usually installed above ground, are often used for shallower wells or as booster pumps. Common issues include loss of prime, seal leaks, or motor wear.     Control Box and Electrical Repair in Reno, NV

    Three-wire submersible pumps rely on a control box with a start capacitor and relay.     Frequently Asked Questions About Well Pump Repair in Reno

    The cost of well pump repair in Reno can vary significantly, typically ranging from $300 for minor repairs like a pressure switch replacement to over $2,000 for complex issues such as replacing a submersible pump motor.     Common signs include no water, inconsistent water pressure, air sputtering from faucets, strange noises coming from the well or pressure tank, or the pump running constantly. If you notice any of these, it's smart to call for a professional inspection.

    FMR Plumbing services all major well pump brands, including but not limited to Goulds, Grundfos, Franklin Electric, Myers, and Sta-Rite. Our technicians are trained and equipped to work on a wide variety of models and system types.

    Well pump repair involves complex electrical components, high-pressure water systems, and often heavy equipment for pulling pumps from wells. DIY repair is generally not recommended due to safety risks and the potential for further damage. It's better handled by experienced professionals to ensure a correct and safe repair.

    Most pump replacements take four to eight hours once parts are on site. Very deep wells or stuck pumps can take more time.

    We work with Franklin Electric, Grundfos, Goulds, Flint and Walling and similar quality brands. We also repair pressure tanks from Well-X-Trol, Amtrol and Flexcon.

    The length of a well pump repair depends on the issue. Simple fixes like a pressure switch replacement might take a few hours, while replacing a submersible pump can take a full day, especially if the well is deep. We strive for efficient service to restore your water as quickly as possible.
