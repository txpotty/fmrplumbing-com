    Sewer Pipe Relining in Reno, NV

    Sewer pipe relining is a trenchless repair method where a new, durable pipe is created within the existing damaged one. A flexible, epoxy-impregnated liner is inserted into the old pipe and inflated, curing in place to form a seamless, strong and corrosion-resistant new pipe. This method is well suited for addressing cracks, minor breaks and root intrusion without major digging. It extends the life of your sewer system and is an excellent option for preserving landscapes in Reno.

    Sewer Repair Cost Comparison in Reno, NV

    A single spot repair is the most affordable fix when the rest of the line is sound. Pipe lining costs more per foot but avoids excavation and works well for long runs under driveways or landscaping. Full replacement is the right call when the pipe is Orangeburg, badly bellied or broken in several places. We lay out all three options with real numbers so you can compare cost against how long the repair will last.

    Sewer Line Hydro-Jetting in Reno

    Hydro-jetting is a powerful cleaning method that uses high-pressure water streams to clear stubborn blockages, grease buildup and even tree roots from sewer lines. It's far more effective than snaking for thorough cleaning and stopping recurring clogs. This service is particularly helpful for restaurants and commercial properties in Reno dealing with heavy grease accumulation, as well as homes with recurring drain issues.

    Orangeburg and Cast Iron Sewer Replacement in Reno

    Some older homes still have Orangeburg pipe, a tar-paper material that flattens and blisters over time. Cast iron from the same era corrodes from the inside and scales up until little flow remains. When a camera shows widespread failure, patching is a waste of money. We replace the line in sections or end to end with PVC, and we can often use trenchless methods to avoid tearing up the whole yard.

    Bellied Sewer Line Correction in Reno, NV

    A belly is a low spot where the pipe has sagged and holds standing water and solids. Bellies usually form where the original bedding washed out or where the soil settled after heavy rain. Clay soils that swell when watered and shrink when dry can stress slabs and buried drain lines over time. We confirm the depth of standing water on camera, then re-bed and re-grade that section to restore proper fall. Correct slope is typically a quarter inch per foot for a typical residential line.

    Trenchless Sewer Line Repair in Reno, NV

    For many sewer line issues, especially those caused by tree roots or pipe corrosion, trenchless repair offers a less invasive option. This method, which includes pipe bursting or pipe lining, requires minimal excavation, saving your yard and landscaping from major disruption. We use specialized equipment to either pull a new pipe through the old one or insert a resin-coated liner that cures in place, creating a new, durable pipe inside the old one. It's an effective and popular choice for preserving property appearance in Reno.

    Traditional Sewer Line Replacement in Reno

    When trenchless repair isn't feasible due to severe damage or specific pipe configurations, traditional dig-and-replace methods are necessary. This involves excavating a trench to access and replace the damaged section of the sewer line. While more intrusive, it is sometimes the only way to address badly collapsed pipes, significant offsets or certain pipe materials common in older Nevada properties. Our team ensures careful excavation and efficient replacement, minimizing the impact on your property in Reno.
