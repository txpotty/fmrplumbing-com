    Workstation Sink Installation in Reno, NV

    Workstation sinks include ledges for cutting boards, colanders and drying racks. They are usually deep, single-bowl designs with offset drains. We position the drain to fit your cabinet and disposal and confirm the faucet reach. These sinks are popular in modern kitchen remodels.

    Undermount Kitchen Sink Installation in Reno

    Undermount sinks need strong clips and adhesive to stay attached to granite or quartz. We mount the bowl, seal the rim with silicone, and support it while the adhesive sets. Then we install strainers, traps and supply lines. A proper undermount install prevents gaps and leaks along the counter edge.

    Under-mount Sink Installation in Reno, NV

    Under-mount sinks provide a sleek, seamless look, making countertop cleaning easier. Their installation is more complex than top-mount sinks, requiring precise measurements and strong adhesive or clips for secure attachment to the underside of the counter. FMR Plumbing is skilled at under-mount sink installation, ensuring a robust bond and a fully sealed edge to prevent water infiltration. We ensure the cutout is smooth and the sink is level for optimal drainage.

    Drop-In Kitchen Sink Installation in Reno

    Drop-in sinks rest on the countertop with a rim, making them a good choice for laminate and tile. We check the cutout size, seal the rim, and tighten mounting clips evenly. This is usually the fastest and most affordable kitchen sink upgrade. It also works well in rentals and older homes.
