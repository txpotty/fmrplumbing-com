    What Goes Into a Water Heater Swap in Elko, NV

    Tankless Water Heater Upgrades in Elko

    Going tankless when the old tank fails is common, but it is a bigger job than a tank swap. A whole-home tankless unit can draw four times the gas of a 40-gallon tank, so the gas line from the meter usually has to be upsized, and the old metal vent gets replaced with stainless or PVC run to the maker's specs. We price both options.

    Conventional Tank Water Heater Replacement in Elko

    Standard tank heaters are still in most homes, and they usually fail from age, rust or sediment. A leak at the base, loud popping or rusty hot water means it is time, and a like-for-like or resized swap usually takes two to four hours. You shower tonight.

    Commercial Water Heater Replacement in Elko

    Businesses in Elko run their water heaters harder than homes do, so commercial replacements need more capacity and faster recovery. Restaurants, salons and offices all draw differently, so we size by peak gallons per hour and required temperature, which may mean a larger tank, a booster or a tankless rack. Downtime costs money.

    Emergency Water Heater Replacement in Elko, NV

    A leaking tank can put 40 or 50 gallons on the floor faster than most people expect. If water is coming from the heater, shut off the cold supply valve on top, turn the gas valve to off or flip the breaker, and open a hot tap to relieve pressure, then call and we will try to have a plumber and a new tank at your home that day. Leaks only get worse.

    Bradford White, Rheem and AO Smith in Elko, NV

    We install Bradford White, Rheem and AO Smith on most tank jobs. Bradford White is sold only through plumbers and known for durability, Rheem has a wide lineup and good parts support, and AO Smith offers many sizes and efficiency options, so the right pick usually depends on fit, warranty and stock. Any of the three works.

    Upsizing Your Water Heater in Elko

    Running out of hot water usually means the house outgrew the tank. A larger tank or a model with a higher first-hour rating can keep up with more showers back to back, and in some homes a 50-gallon high-recovery gas unit does the job of a 75-gallon standard tank without taking up any more floor space. Bigger is not always needed.

    Gas Water Heater Replacement in Elko, NV

    Gas water heaters reheat quicker than electric tanks, and in Elko, NV homes they fail most often from a corroded tank, a bad gas valve or a burner clogged with rust flakes. We shut off the gas, disconnect and cap the line, drain the old tank and set the new one, then install a new flex connector, a sediment trap and a vent connector sloped up toward the flue. Every joint gets checked.

    Bradford White Water Heater Services in Elko
