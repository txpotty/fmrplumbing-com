    Frequently Asked Questions About Trenchless Sewer Line Repair in Elko

    Most trenchless sewer jobs in Elko, NV fall between $4,000 and $15,000. The biggest factors are the length of the run and whether we line or burst. Depth, pipe diameter and how hard the pits are to reach also move the number, so a line under a driveway with only one cleanout will cost more than an open-lawn lateral of the same length.

    Plan on a day for most lining jobs and one to two days for a burst. An open-trench replacement often takes a week, plus restoration. The water is off only during the cure, which is usually a few hours, so most families never leave the house and can use the toilets again by dinner.

    One to three days covers most jobs, while an open-trench replacement can run a week or longer once restoration is counted. Lining a short lateral can finish in a single morning. Longer runs and bursts take more time, mostly for pit work and fusing the new HDPE pipe into one length before the pull. Cure time adds a few hours.

    Your lawn and driveway stay in one piece. For most homeowners that is the whole reason to ask. The job also goes faster, often in a day, and you skip the restoration work that follows a trench, like new concrete and sod, which can cost almost as much as the plumbing.

    Less digging is the big one. There is no trench across the lawn and no slab cut in the driveway, and the new liner or HDPE pipe has no joints for roots to get into, which is a real upgrade over clay or cast iron. The restoration bill shrinks to almost nothing.

    Pipe bursting uses high-density polyethylene, HDPE, fused into one continuous length with no joints. Liners are felt tubes soaked in epoxy. Neither material corrodes, and roots cannot get through, and both are rated by their makers for a service life of around fifty years when installed on properly prepared pipe.
