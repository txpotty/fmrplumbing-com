    Gas Water Heater Efficiency Ratings in Elko

    Efficiency ratings on tank heaters are listed as UEF, uniform energy factor, and a higher number means less gas per gallon heated. A standard tank sits around 0.60 to 0.65. High-efficiency condensing tanks reach 0.80 or more and can qualify for rebates.

    Atmospheric Vent Gas Water Heater Replacement in Elko

    Atmospheric vent heaters last 10 to 12 years on average, and the end usually shows up as rusty hot water or a slow drip from the bottom. The new tank ties into your existing gas line and flue. Combustion air matters. A tank in a tight closet needs louvered doors or a ducted air supply, and we check that before lighting it.

    Power Vent Gas Water Heater Installation in Elko, NV

    Power vent heaters use a blower to push exhaust through 2 or 3-inch PVC, so they can sit far from a chimney. They need a 120-volt outlet nearby. We run the vent within the maker's length and elbow limits.

    Power Vent Water Heater Replacement in Elko

    Blower motors and pressure switches are the weak points on power vent tanks. A second blower failure on an older tank is usually the point where a new heater makes more sense. A dedicated outlet gets confirmed first.

    Large Capacity 75 Gallon Gas Heaters in Elko, NV

    Big families with soaking tubs often outgrow a 50 gallon tank and need a 75 gallon heater or a high-recovery 50 at around 65,000 to 75,000 BTU. A bigger burner needs more gas. We size the gas line before installing, since an undersized line cuts recovery.

    40 and 50 Gallon Tank Replacement in Elko

    Forty and fifty gallon gas tanks are the workhorse in most Nevada homes, and a leak at the bottom seam means the tank is finished. Current tanks are often taller and wider. We measure the closet before ordering. Most of these swaps take two to three hours.

    Rheem Gas Water Heater Replacement in Elko, NV

    Rheem Performance and Performance Platinum tanks cover most of the range we install, from basic 6-year models to 12-year tanks with better anodes. The longer warranty usually means a bigger anode. We help you pick.

    Bradford White tanks are sold only through plumbing wholesalers, not big-box stores, and plenty of Nevada plumbers install them for that reason. Replacement parts are easy to find. We follow their venting and clearance specs to the letter, which keeps the warranty valid.
