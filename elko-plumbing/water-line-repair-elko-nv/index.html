    Emergency Water Line Break Repair in Elko, NV

    Trenchless Water Line Repair in Elko

    For some water line repairs, traditional trenching can be avoided. Trenchless repair methods, like pipe bursting or pipe lining, allow us to fix or replace underground pipes with minimal disruption to your landscaping, driveway, or foundation. This method is often faster and less intrusive than traditional digging, making it a popular choice for homeowners in Elko looking to preserve their property. We'll assess whether your water line issue is a good candidate for trenchless technology.

    Trenchless Water Line Replacement in Elko

    If your service line runs under a driveway, pool deck or mature landscaping, trenchless replacement can save a lot of restoration cost. You keep your pavers and trees, and the new line carries a long manufacturer warranty.

    Main Water Line Repair in Elko

    The main water line brings water from the public supply to your home. When this line leaks or breaks, it can cause serious water loss, foundation damage, and low water pressure. Our team focuses on diagnosing and repairing main water line issues, from pinpoint leaks to full pipe ruptures, so your home's primary water source is secure. We use advanced detection methods to find the exact location of the problem, minimizing excavation and disruption to your property in Elko.     Water Line Repair at the Meter in Elko, NV

    Leaks often start at the compression fittings and couplings right next to the meter box. Shifting soil and heavy traffic over the box can work loose those joints over time. When the leak is yours, we replace the fittings and the customer shutoff valve in the same visit.

    PEX (cross-linked polyethylene) piping is a flexible, durable, and corrosion-resistant option more and more used in homes across Elko, NV.
