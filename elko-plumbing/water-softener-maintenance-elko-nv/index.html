    Bypass Valve and Drain Line Repair in Elko, NV

    A leaking bypass valve can let hard water slip past the softener without you noticing. We test and rebuild bypass valves and replace cracked fittings. We also inspect the drain line for kinks and proper air gap and the brine overflow line for correct routing. These small fixes head off leaks and backflow problems.

    Resin Bed Cleaning and Regeneration Optimization in Elko, NV

    The resin beads are responsible for ion exchange, removing hard minerals from your water. Over time, these beads can become fouled with iron or other contaminants, reducing their effectiveness. Our maintenance includes cleaning the resin bed and adjusting the regeneration cycle settings for full softening capacity. We confirm your system is regenerating efficiently for your water quality, providing truly soft water.

    Commercial Softener Maintenance in Elko

    Restaurants, laundromats, salons and car washes rely on soft water to protect equipment like dishwashers, ice machines and boilers. Commercial twin-tank systems regenerate often and need closer attention. We set up scheduled visits to test hardness, clean brine tanks and service valves. Consistent maintenance keeps equipment warranties valid and reduces repair costs.

    Control Valve and Injector Service in Elko, NV

    The control valve directs water through backwash, brine draw, rinse and refill. Tiny injectors and screens inside the valve clog with sediment and salt residue, which stops brine draw. We disassemble the injector, clean the screens and replace worn seals and spacers on valves like the Fleck 5600 and Clack WS1. A clean valve gives you a full regeneration every time.

    Brine Tank Cleaning and Salt Bridge Removal in Elko, NV

    The brine tank is crucial for salt dissolution and regeneration. Our maintenance service includes thorough cleaning of the brine tank and removal of any salt bridges, so your system can draw brine effectively for full softening.
