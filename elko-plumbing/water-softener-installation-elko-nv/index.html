    Whole-Home Water Softener Installation in Elko

    Whole-home softeners treat every gallon that enters the house, right after the main shutoff.     Water Softener Replacement in Elko, NV

    Older softeners lose ground as resin wears out, valve seals leak or injectors clog. While the old system is out, we inspect the loop and the drain, then install a newer metered unit. Salt use drops. You will notice soft water the same day.

    Water Softener Installation for New Construction in Elko, NV

    Building a home in Elko, NV? New construction is the cheapest time to add a softener loop, because the walls are open and the main can be routed past the garage or utility room without cutting finished drywall. We work with builders. We keep outdoor taps off the softener.

    Salt-Based Water Softener Systems in Elko, NV

    Salt-based softeners use ion exchange, swapping calcium and magnesium for sodium on a bed of resin beads. FMR Plumbing installs these with a properly sized resin tank and brine tank. We program regeneration by metered water use so the system only recharges when the resin is actually spent, which saves a lot of salt. Most effective option.

    Water Hardness Testing and Programming in Elko

    Testing is what makes the settings right. We test for hardness, iron and chlorine before installing, then program the regeneration frequency and salt dose so you get soft water without wasting salt. Iron adds to the setting. We review the settings with you before we leave.
