    Badger Series Installation in Elko, NV

    The Badger line runs from the 1/3 HP Badger 1 up to the Badger 5XP. Every Badger shares one quick-lock mount. That shared mount is why a Badger-to-Badger swap is often a 45-minute job, since the upper ring and flange can stay put if they are not corroded. Badgers ship without a cord, so we bring the cord kit.

    Garbage Disposal Replacement in Elko

    If your current disposal hums, leaks from the bottom or trips its reset button every week, replacement usually beats repair. A leak from the housing means the internal seal is gone. No one repairs that seal. We take down the old one, keep the dishwasher hose capped and set the new unit to the existing drain, trimming the tailpiece if the height changed.

    Standard InSinkErator Installation in Elko

    A standard install goes into a sink that already has a drain and power. The old disposal comes down, the flange gets scraped clean and a new one goes in on fresh putty. Then the mount ring goes on. The unit hangs on the ring, locks with a quarter turn, and the discharge tube meets the trap on a new slip-joint washer. Power comes from a cord kit or the existing hardwire, checked for ground before the breaker goes back on.

    Dishwasher Connection and Air Gap Setup in Elko

    Nearly every InSinkErator has a dishwasher inlet with a factory knockout plug inside. The plug gets knocked loose and pulled out of the grind chamber. Then the dishwasher hose goes on with a proper clamp, routed through an air gap or up into a high loop under the counter so sink water cannot siphon back into the dishwasher tub. Both appliances get run together.

    Air Switch Disposal Installation in Elko

    An air switch puts a push button on the countertop or sink deck instead of a wall switch. The button pushes a puff of air down a thin tube. That pulse trips a switch in a control box plugged in under the sink, so no live wiring sits anywhere near the water, which is the main reason people like them. If there is a spare faucet hole, we use it, otherwise we drill.

    Disposal Replacement and Haul-Away in Elko, NV

    Old disposals are heavy and awkward to get rid of. We take them with us. That includes the old cord, the bent mounting ring if we replaced it and the corroded slip nuts, so nothing is left in a bag under your sink for you to deal with on trash day. Most replacements finish in one visit.

    Disposal Installs for New Sinks in Elko, NV

    A new sink changes the drain math. Undermount and farmhouse bowls are often deeper than the old drop-in. A deeper bowl pushes the disposal lower, and if the trap arm in the wall was set for a shallow sink, the discharge can end up below it, so we sometimes lower the wall stub. We plan that during the remodel, not after.

    New Construction Disposal Installs in Elko

    New builds and gut remodels get the disposal in two trips. At rough-in we set the drain height and confirm a switched outlet is in the sink base. We hold the unit back for now. Once the countertop and sink are in and the faucet is set, we come back to hang the unit, connect the dishwasher and test everything with the water on.
