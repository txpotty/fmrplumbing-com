    Commercial Flush Valve Upgrades in Elko

    Manual flush valves on older commercial toilets leak at the handle and stick open, and swapping to a sensor valve during a replacement improves hygiene without changing the rough-in.     One-Piece Toilet Replacement in Elko, NV

    One-piece toilets have no tank-to-bowl joint, so there are fewer leak points and fewer crevices to clean. They cost more and weigh more.     Rental and Multi-Unit Toilet Replacement in Elko, NV

    Landlords with several units do better standardizing on one toilet model with common parts, because the next flapper or fill valve is then the same part in every unit.     Commercial Toilet Replacement in Elko, NV

    Replacing Pressure-Assisted Toilets in Elko, NV

    Pressure-assisted toilets use a sealed vessel in the tank that stores air under line pressure and pushes water through the bowl hard, which helps in houses with long, low-slope drain runs.
