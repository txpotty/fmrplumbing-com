    Fixture, Drain and Shutoff Valve Check in Elko, NV

    We run every faucet, shower and toilet and look for slow drains, leaks and weak flow. Shutoffs get tested too. Stops and the main valve tend to seize with age, and gurgling drains can mean a vent problem, both of which are simple fixes once found.

    Preventive Maintenance Checks in Elko, NV

    A maintenance check is a lighter version of a full inspection for owners who want to stay ahead of problems. We flush the water heater, exercise the main shutoff and angle stops, check pressure and look for slow drains and weeping fittings. Plan on an hour or so. Yearly or every-other-year checks stretch heater life and keep valves from seizing.

    Gas Connection Safety Check in Elko, NV

    During a full inspection, we look at the visible gas piping and the connectors to each appliance. Old brass connectors, missing shutoffs or drip legs and rust all get noted, and we sweep joints with a gas detector. Any leak is reported on the spot. Most of our gas findings are in older homes, often at original appliance connectors.

    Water Heater Safety Inspection in Elko

    A water heater stores a lot of hot water under pressure, so safety items matter. We test the T and P valve, confirm its discharge line runs to a safe place, look for an expansion tank where one is needed and check the drain pan. Gas units get a CO check. Backdrafting heaters can push combustion gases indoors, which is why we test the vent and report problems before we leave.

    Seasonal Plumbing Checks in Elko, NV

    The high desert in the north brings cold winters and big daily temperature swings that stress pipes and fittings. A seasonal check looks at the parts of the system that weather affects most. We look at hose bibbs, exposed pipe in garages and crawlspaces, irrigation backflow devices and the water heater's relief valve and drain. We look at pipe insulation. Swapping a cracked outdoor faucet or insulating a pipe costs little next to a burst line in the wall.

    Residential Plumbing Inspections in Elko

    A full home inspection covers the system from the water meter to the sewer connection. Our plumber looks at all exposed pipe, runs every faucet and toilet and tests the heater, the main valve and the fixture stops. Pressure gets measured too. We note anything above 80 psi.
