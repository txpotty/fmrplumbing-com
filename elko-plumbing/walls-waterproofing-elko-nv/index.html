    How We Keep Water Out of Walls in Elko, NV

    Exterior Foundation Waterproofing in Elko

    Exterior waterproofing means digging down to the footing and coating the outside of the wall. It is the most reliable fix for a leaking basement, because the water never touches the block or concrete. We apply a rubberized asphalt or polymer membrane, add a dimple board to protect it, and set perforated drain pipe in gravel along the footing so water has somewhere to go. Clay soils that swell when watered and shrink when dry can stress slabs and buried drain lines over time. We backfill after the drain is tested.

    Commercial Walls Waterproofing in Elko

    Commercial basements, elevator pits and mechanical rooms in Elko take on water the same way houses do, just with more at stake. A wet electrical room can shut a business down. We schedule around operating hours, work with property managers on access, and meet the waterproofing specs in the building plans for below-grade walls. Downtime stays short.

    Shower Pan Leak Repair and Waterproofing in Elko, NV

    Wet baseboards outside the shower or a stain on the ceiling below usually point to the pan. We plug the drain, fill the pan with an inch or two of water, and mark the level and wait 24 hours. A drop means a leak. Rebuilding means a mortar pre-slope of a quarter inch per foot, a new PVC liner or bonded membrane, and a clamping drain tightened onto the liner. The new pan gets its own flood test before tile.

    Sump Pump Installation and Repair in Elko, NV

    A sump pump is the part of an interior system that actually moves the water. We set a lidded basin below slab level, install a cast iron pump sized for the inflow, and add a battery backup or water-powered backup for the night the power goes out during a storm. Check valves matter. A missing check valve lets the discharge line drain back into the pit and the pump short-cycles. The discharge line gets routed at least 10 feet from the house.

    Crawl Space Vapor Barriers in Elko

    Bare dirt in a crawl space releases gallons of water vapor a day into the air under your floor. A 10 mil or heavier polyethylene barrier, lapped 12 inches at the seams and taped, cuts that off and keeps the joists and subfloor dry. We run it up the foundation walls and hold it in place with termination strip. Cheap fix, big difference.

    Plumbing Leak Behind Walls in Elko

    A drip from a shower valve cartridge, a sweated copper stub or a loose drain slip nut can soak a wall cavity for months before paint bubbles. We find it with a moisture meter, a thermal camera that shows the cool wet spot, and a pressure test on the supply lines. Then we cut a clean access hole. We replace the leaking fitting or pipe section. We pull wet insulation and run fans until the cavity reads dry.

    Exterior Block Wall Waterproofing in Elko, NV

    White crust on block or stem walls is efflorescence, salt left behind as water moves through the masonry and dries. The usual cause is a sprinkler head spraying the wall instead of the plants, or soil graded toward the foundation. We fix the water first. After that the wall gets cleaned, dried and coated with a breathable masonry sealer or elastomeric coating.

    Cost-Effective Waterproofing Solutions in Elko, NV

    Not every wet wall needs a $15,000 excavation. Sometimes the fix is a downspout extension and regrading the soil so it slopes away from the house, a drop of about six inches in the first ten feet, and that costs a few hundred dollars. We start with the cheapest fix that will actually work. You get the options in writing, side by side.
