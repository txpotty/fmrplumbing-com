    Shower Pan Installation in Elko

    Shower pans fail quietly. Pinholes and loose drain clamps let water into the subfloor slowly, which is why tiled pans sit flooded for a full day before tile. Prefab pans get a level check. Both kinds get slope toward the drain.

    New Construction Shower Installation in Elko, NV

    New homes get their shower plumbing during framing. We rough in valves, supply lines and drains once the walls are framed, set each valve to the depth of the planned finish and locate the drain for the pan you picked. Inspection comes next. We return for trim after tile.

    Steam Shower Plumbing in Elko

    Steam showers need four things: a generator, a water supply, a drain line and an electrical circuit. The generator goes within the maker's distance limit, and we plumb its supply, drain and steam line to the head. An electrician runs the circuit. The enclosure needs a sealed door and a sloped ceiling.

    Digital Shower Systems in Elko, NV

    Digital shower systems like U by Moen and Kohler DTV need a little more planning. These valves usually sit in a nearby closet or vanity and need low-voltage wiring to the controller and an outlet close by. An electrician handles the outlet. We plumb the valve and outlets.
