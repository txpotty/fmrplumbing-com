    Pit Cleaning and Debris Removal in Elko

    Long dry stretches let dust, sand, gravel and leaves settle in the pit. That debris can get sucked into the intake screen and jam the impeller. We pump down and scoop out the basin, rinse the intake and inspect the pit liner for cracks. A clean basin helps the pump run cooler and last years longer.

    Float Switch Inspection and Adjustment in Elko, NV

    The float switch is a critical component that tells your sump pump when to turn on and off. A stuck or improperly adjusted float switch can lead to the pump running continuously or failing to activate at all. During maintenance, we inspect the float switch for freedom of movement and proper calibration, making adjustments as needed to support reliable operation for your home in Elko, NV.

    Annual Sump Pump Check-ups in Elko

    Regular annual maintenance is key for the longevity and reliability of your sump pump. Our technicians perform a thorough inspection, checking the motor, impeller, float switch and discharge lines for any signs of wear or blockage.     Submersible Sump Pump Service in Elko

    Most local codes do not allow sump discharge into the sanitary sewer. We route discharge to the yard, a dry well or another approved location.
