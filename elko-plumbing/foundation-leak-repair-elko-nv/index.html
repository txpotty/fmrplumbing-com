    Copper Pipe Leak Repair in Elko, NV

    Many older homes in Elko, NV have copper water lines beneath their foundations, which can corrode over time due to soil conditions or water chemistry. Mineral deposits crust over aerators, showerheads and valves, which is why softeners and regular water heater flushing are popular. When these copper pipes develop pinhole leaks or cracks, they require specialized repair. Our technicians repair or replace sections of damaged copper piping, providing a durable seal that holds up to pressure and ground movement and helps prevent future leaks.

    Water Line Reroute in Elko

    Rerouting abandons the leaking line under the foundation and runs a new line through the attic or walls. This avoids breaking concrete and removes the risk of future leaks under the slab. We typically use PEX for reroutes because it is flexible and resists corrosion. Drywall openings are kept small and neat for easy patching.
