    Copper Pipe Replacement in Mesquite, NV

    Copper still has fans. It handles heat well and lasts decades. We install Type L copper for most interior supply lines, sweat or press the joints, while strapping each line properly, so the new pipe does not rattle or sag. Costs more than PEX.

    PEX Pipe Replacement in Mesquite

    PEX bends around corners and comes in long rolls, so a typical repipe needs fewer fittings than copper would on the same layout. The northern part of the state sees regular hard freezes, so pipes in exterior walls and unheated spaces need protection. PEX can stretch a little in a freeze instead of splitting. We use PEX-a with expansion fittings where possible, keeping flow strong at each joint. Faster install.

    Galvanized Pipe Replacement in Mesquite, NV

    Galvanized steel was common before the 1960s. The zinc wears off and the steel rusts inside. Over decades the opening shrinks, pressure drops and the water turns brown after sitting, and the threads where pipes join are often the first to leak. Replacing it is the only fix.

    PEX Pipe Installation in Mesquite

    PEX costs less than copper and goes in faster. Scale and rust do not affect it. Water in much of Nevada is very hard, and scale buildup is one of the most common reasons water heaters and fixtures fail early. We use PEX-a with expansion fittings or PEX-b with crimp rings depending on the job, and we run it in home runs from a manifold or in trunk-and-branch layouts. Fewer joints, fewer leaks.

    Pipe Replacement for Multi-Unit Buildings in Mesquite

    Apartments and condos share risers. One leak can affect several units. We plan riser replacement in phases, one stack at a time, notify tenants ahead of shutoffs, and test each section before moving on. Organized work.

    Corroded Pipe Replacement in Mesquite, NV

    Corrosion shows up as green or white crust on copper, rust on steel, or pinholes that drip. Some water is harder on pipe. We replace corroded sections or the whole system depending on how widespread the damage is, and we check the water heater connections and shutoffs since they often corrode too. Look at the joints.

    Copper Pinhole Leak Replacement in Mesquite, NV

    Pinhole leaks start small. A drip, then a stain. They usually mean the copper wall has thinned from corrosion, and when one shows up, more are often on the way along the same lines. Repipe or patch, we explain.
