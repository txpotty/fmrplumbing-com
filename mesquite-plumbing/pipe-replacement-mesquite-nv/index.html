    PEX Pipe Replacement in Mesquite

    PEX (cross-linked polyethylene) piping has become a common choice for residential pipe replacement due to its flexibility, affordability, and resistance to freezing. The northern part of the state sees regular hard freezes, so pipes in exterior walls and crawlspaces need protection. Unlike rigid pipes, PEX can expand, reducing the risk of burst pipes during sudden cold snaps. FMR Plumbing specializes in PEX installation, which often allows for less invasive replacement methods compared to traditional materials. This modern solution offers excellent performance and water quality for your home.

    Copper Pipe Replacement in Mesquite, NV

    Polybutylene Pipe Replacement in Mesquite

    Gray polybutylene pipe was installed in many homes from the late 1970s into the mid-1990s. It breaks down from the inside when exposed to chlorinated water, and fittings can crack with no warning. Some insurers raise premiums or decline coverage on homes that still have it. We remove polybutylene supply lines and replace them with PEX-A or copper. A completed repipe can also help when listing the home.
