    Regeneration Settings and Hardness Testing in Mesquite, NV

    Many softeners are set incorrectly from day one or never updated when household size changes. We test your raw water hardness and program the valve to match your actual use. Metered systems regenerate based on gallons used, which saves salt compared to time clock models. Correct settings keep water soft without squandering salt or water.

    Salt-Free Conditioner Maintenance in Mesquite

    Template assisted crystallization and other salt-free conditioners do not remove hardness but change how it behaves. They still need media replacement every few years and sediment prefilter changes much more often. We inspect the media tank, replace prefilters and check bypass valves. We also explain honestly how these systems differ from a true softener.

    Preventative Maintenance for All Brands in Mesquite, NV

    Whether you have a Fleck, Culligan, Kenmore, GE or EcoPure system, our technicians are trained to service all major water softener brands.     Bypass Valve and Drain Line Repair in Mesquite, NV

    A leaking bypass valve can let hard water slip past the softener without you noticing. We test and rebuild bypass valves and replace cracked fittings. We also inspect the drain line for kinks and proper air gap and the brine overflow line for correct routing. These small fixes prevent leaks and backflow problems.

    Commercial Softener Maintenance in Mesquite

    Restaurants, laundromats, salons and car washes rely on soft water to protect equipment like dishwashers, ice machines and boilers. Commercial twin-tank systems regenerate often and need closer attention. We set up scheduled visits to test hardness, clean brine tanks and service valves.     Resin Bed Analysis and Regeneration in Mesquite

    The resin bed is the heart of your water softener, where ion exchange occurs. During maintenance, we check the condition of the resin beads and confirm the regeneration cycles are programmed correctly for your household's water usage. Proper resin bed function is key to consistent soft water throughout your home in Mesquite.
