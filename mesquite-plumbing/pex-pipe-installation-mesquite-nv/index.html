    PEX-A vs. PEX-B Installation in Mesquite

    We offer installation for both PEX-A and PEX-B piping and understand the different advantages of each. PEX-A, often preferred for its flexibility and ability to recover from minor kinks, uses expansion fittings. PEX-B is more rigid but also very durable, usually using crimp or compression fittings. Our plumbers in Mesquite can help you decide which type of PEX suits your project, considering factors like budget and application.

    Uponor PEX System Installation in Mesquite

    FMR Plumbing often installs Uponor PEX systems, a leading brand known for high-quality, durable PEX-A piping.     PEX-A Expansion Fitting Systems in Mesquite, NV

    PEX-A, such as Uponor, uses an expansion tool to stretch the pipe and a ring over the fitting, which then shrinks back into a tight seal. The fittings have a larger inner diameter, so flow restriction is lower. PEX-A is also more flexible and can be repaired from kinks with a heat gun. It is our favored choice for many full repipes.
