    Frequently Asked Questions About Sewer Line Cleaning in Mesquite

    Sewer line cleaning in Mesquite typically costs $200 to $500 with a cable machine, or $400 to $900 with hydro jetting. Adding a recorded camera inspection usually runs $150 to $350.

    While minor drain clogs can sometimes be handled with DIY methods, sewer line cleaning requires professional equipment like powerful augers or hydro jetters and expertise in diagnosing the problem. Trying to clear a main sewer line without proper tools can lead to further damage or poor results, so professional help is highly recommended.

    Rental machines can work on simple clogs, but they can kink or get stuck on roots. A pro also offers a camera to verify the line is clear.

    In most cases, sewer line cleaning methods like snaking and hydro jetting are non-invasive and do not require digging. We access the line through existing cleanouts. Digging is usually only necessary if there is a severe structural issue like a collapsed pipe requiring replacement, which we would confirm with a camera inspection first.

    Common causes include tree root intrusion, buildup of grease and food particles, flushing non-biodegradable items like wet wipes or feminine hygiene products, and structural issues like cracked or collapsed pipes. Older homes often have clay pipes that are prone to root invasion.

    Lines with roots or past clogs often need cleaning every 12 to 24 months. Newer PVC lines without issues may go years without service.

    Hydro jetting is generally safe for most pipe materials, but it is crucial to have a professional assess your sewer line's condition first.
