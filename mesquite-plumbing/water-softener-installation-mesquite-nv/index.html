    Whole-Home Water Softener Installation in Mesquite

    Whole-home softeners treat every gallon that enters the house, right after the main shutoff. That protects every faucet, shower, water heater and appliance downstream, which is where hard water does most of its damage over the years. Sizing comes first. Hardness and household use set the grain capacity.

    Commercial Water Softener Installation in Mesquite, NV

    Restaurants, hotels and laundromats feel hard water in their equipment first. Commercial softeners we install use larger resin tanks or twin-tank alternating systems so soft water is available around the clock, even during regeneration. Equipment lasts longer. We schedule around your hours.

    Water Softener Replacement Services in Mesquite, NV

    Worn-out softeners usually show it in one of two ways, hard water at the tap or a brine tank that never drops. We remove the old unit, check the loop and drain for problems and install a newer model that fits your current hardness and household size. Disposal is included. The work is usually done in a few hours.

    Ecodyne Water Softener Installation in Mesquite

    Ecodyne softeners are sold at many home stores, and we install them for homeowners who already bought one. Install follows the manufacturer manual for bypass, drain and overflow, then we set hardness and regeneration. We install store-bought units. Programming is part of the job.

    Reverse Osmosis Drinking Water Systems in Mesquite

    Reverse osmosis pushes water through a fine membrane that removes most dissolved solids, including the sodium a softener adds. Our RO installs go under the sink with their own faucet, and we can tie in the fridge or ice maker too. Pairs well with a softener. We can handle filter changes.

    Whole-House Water Softener Systems in Mesquite

    On a whole-house system, the softener goes on the main line after the shutoff and before the water heater. We add a bypass valve so you can isolate the softener for service, and we install a pressure regulator if your street pressure runs above 80 psi. Soft water reaches every fixture. We can skip the hose bibs.

    Pelican Water Softener Installation in Mesquite, NV

    Pelican sells both salt-based softeners and salt-free conditioners, often direct to homeowners. When a homeowner has already bought one, we handle the plumbing, the bypass and the startup flush the manual requires. Startup matters. Every step gets done.

    High-Efficiency Water Softener Installation in Mesquite

    High-efficiency softeners use upflow brining and metered valves to cut salt and water use. Compared with an older timer-based unit, a modern metered softener can use roughly half the salt and noticeably less regeneration water each month. The savings add up. Several models are available.
