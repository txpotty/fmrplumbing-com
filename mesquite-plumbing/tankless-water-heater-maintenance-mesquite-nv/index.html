    Every tankless unit has an inlet water screen that catches grit and scale flakes. Those screens can clog with sand and debris, especially after municipal line work.     Navien Tankless Heater Tune-Ups in Mesquite

    Navien tankless water heaters often feature condensing technology, making them highly efficient but also requiring specific maintenance.     Heat Exchanger Descaling in Mesquite

    The heat exchanger in a tankless heater is a coil of small tubes that water races through while the burner fires. Hard water drops calcium and magnesium on those hot surfaces, narrowing the passages over time. We connect a submersible pump and hoses to the service valves and circulate descaling solution for 45 to 60 minutes. Then we flush with fresh water to rinse the system completely. Restored flow means more stable temperatures and fewer shutdowns.

    Condensing Unit and Neutralizer Service in Mesquite

    High-efficiency condensing tankless heaters produce acidic condensate that must be neutralized before it goes down a drain. We inspect the condensate trap, line and neutralizer cartridge and replace the media when it is spent. A clogged trap can cause shutdowns or water leaking inside the cabinet. We also check that the line is protected where it runs outdoors.

    Commercial Tankless Rack Maintenance in Mesquite

    Restaurants, salons and multi-family buildings often run several tankless units linked together. Commercial racks see heavy use and scale faster than home units. We descale each heater, check cascade controls and balance the load across the system. Quarterly or semiannual service keeps the hot water flowing during busy hours.
