    Evolution Series Installation in Mesquite

    Evolution units grind in two or three stages and carry extra sound insulation around the motor housing, which is why they sit taller and weigh more than a Badger. Cabinet clearance matters. We measure from the sink bottom to the trap arm before the install so the unit does not crowd the trap. The Compact fits tight cabinets, and the Excel needs the most room.

    Upgrading Your InSinkErator in Mesquite, NV

    Moving up from a 1/3 HP builder unit to a 3/4 HP Evolution changes more than the grind. The new unit is taller. That extra height can put the discharge outlet below the trap arm, so we measure the drop before install day and lower the trap or the arm if the numbers do not work. The circuit gets checked for the bigger motor's draw too.

    Hard Water and Disposals in Mesquite

    Mineral deposits crust over aerators, showerheads and valves, which is why softeners and regular water heater flushing are popular. Scale builds up on grind rings and drain tailpieces over the years. Scale makes a disposal noisy. Drainage slows too, since crust narrows the tailpiece and trap, and we check those parts before tying a new unit into them. Heavily scaled parts get replaced the same visit.

    Batch Feed vs Continuous Feed in Mesquite

    Continuous feed is the type most kitchens have. It runs while the switch is on. Batch feed only runs with its stopper turned in place, so nobody can reach into a running unit, which some families with small kids prefer and which needs no wall switch at all. Each type gets wired its own way.

    Disposal Installs for New Sinks in Mesquite, NV

    A new sink changes the drain math. Deeper undermount or farmhouse bowls are common in remodels. A deeper bowl pushes the disposal lower, and if the trap arm in the wall was set for a shallow sink, the discharge can end up below it, so we sometimes lower the wall stub. Better to sort that out mid-remodel than after the tile is up.
