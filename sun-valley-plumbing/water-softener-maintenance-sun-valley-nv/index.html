    Control Valve and Injector Cleaning in Sun Valley, NV

    Brine draw depends on a venturi injector, a tiny nozzle and throat that pulls brine out of the salt tank as water rushes past. A few specks of grit can stop it cold. We pull the injector and screen, clean them, and replace the seals and spacers on valves like the Fleck 5600 and Clack WS1 when they show wear.

    Commercial Softener Service in Sun Valley

    Commercial units work harder. Restaurants and laundromats can burn through a softener's capacity in a single day, so twin-tank systems swap tanks, keeping one in service while the other regenerates. We set visits around your business hours so the water never goes hard during a rush.

    Hardness Testing at the Tap in Sun Valley

    We test with a titration kit, counting drops until the sample changes color, because test strips are too rough for setting a valve. We take the raw sample from an unsoftened hose bib or with the softener on bypass. The softened sample comes from a kitchen faucet.

    Resin Cleaning and Regeneration Tuning in Sun Valley, NV

    Iron is the most common resin fouler we see. The beads turn rusty brown and stop grabbing hardness. We feed a resin cleaner through the brine well, run a regeneration and retest, and if the capacity comes back we shorten the time between regenerations so the iron does not build back up.
