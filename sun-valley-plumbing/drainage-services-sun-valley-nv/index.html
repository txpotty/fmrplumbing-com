    Dry Well Installation in Sun Valley, NV

    Where there is no place to discharge on the surface, a dry well lets water soak into deeper soil. Drainage depends on the soil. A perc check comes first, then the well gets sized for the area it serves and set away from the foundation and septic.

    Hydro-Jetting Services in Sun Valley

    A hydro jetter sends water at several thousand psi through a nozzle that scours the full pipe wall. Cables punch through clogs, but jetting removes the grease and scale coating the pipe, which is what keeps causing them. Grease lines benefit most. A camera check comes first to confirm the pipe can take it.

    Downspout and Roof Drain Solutions in Sun Valley

    Gutters concentrate a lot of roof water into a few points, often right next to the foundation. Clay soils that swell when watered and shrink when dry can shift slabs and buried drain lines over time. We pipe downspouts underground in solid PVC to a pop-up emitter or dry well well away from the house, with a cleanout at the base so the line can be flushed. An inch of rain on a 1,000 square foot roof sheds roughly 600 gallons. That is a lot of water.

    Drainage Service Cost Comparison in Sun Valley, NV

    Clearing a single fixture drain typically costs $125 to $300, and a main line clearing runs $200 to $500. Hydro jetting tends to run $350 to $800. Camera work adds a few hundred dollars, and French drains are priced by the linear foot.

    Outdoor and Storm Drain Cleaning in Sun Valley

    Area drains, catch basins and driveway trench drains fill with leaves and silt. A buried grate sends water across the yard, and the pipe under it fills with mud. We vacuum the basins and jet the lines. Fall cleaning helps most.

    Kitchen, Bath and Laundry Drain Clearing in Sun Valley

    Slow sinks, gurgling tubs and overflowing laundry standpipes usually come from grease, hair and lint. Water in much of Nevada is very hard, and scale buildup is one of the most common reasons water heaters and fixtures fail early. Scale roughens the inside of old pipe so debris catches, and the same drain keeps clogging. Lint traps help laundry lines.

    French Drain Installation in Sun Valley

    A French drain is a gravel-filled trench with perforated pipe that collects groundwater and carries it away. We wrap the gravel in filter fabric so silt does not clog it. It needs steady fall to an outlet, and the outlet gets a grate to keep critters out.

    Sump Pump Services in Sun Valley, NV

    A sump pump keeps groundwater out of basements and crawlspaces. We test the float, check the discharge line and check valve, and replace pumps that hum, run nonstop, or cannot keep up during a hard rain. Never pipe it to the sewer. Many sump floods come from a blocked discharge line, not a dead pump.
