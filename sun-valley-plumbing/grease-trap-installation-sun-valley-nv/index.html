    What Goes Into a Grease Trap Install in Sun Valley, NV

    Hydro-Mechanical Grease Trap Installation in Sun Valley, NV

    Hydromechanical traps use baffles and a vented flow control to pull grease out of water moving at a set rate, usually 20 to 100 gallons per minute. You can put one under a sink, beside a dish station or in a pit in the slab. The flow control is not optional. Without it, a dumped sink pushes water through faster than the unit is rated for and the grease goes out the outlet. We look for units certified to PDI G-101 or ASME A112.14.3.

    Commercial Grease Trap Installation in Sun Valley

    A commercial kitchen with a three-compartment sink, a pre-rinse station and two floor sinks can push 50 gallons a minute of greasy water at the dinner rush. We size the trap to that peak flow, not the average. Undersized traps are the usual failure. We set the unit, run the inlet and outlet in schedule 40 PVC or cast iron, and add the flow control fitting the manufacturer calls for.

    Commercial Kitchen Grease Trap Installs in Sun Valley

    Every commercial install starts with a fixture list. The tech walks the kitchen and writes down every fixture that drains to the trap and how fast each one empties. That number decides between an indoor unit and an outdoor tank, and where it can go with clean pipe runs to the sewer. Fewer bends, fewer clogs.

    Under-Sink Grease Trap Setup in Sun Valley, NV

    Small cafes and coffee bars often get by with a 10 to 25 GPM hydromechanical trap tucked under the prep sink or beside it. It sits on the floor or in a recess, with the flow control fitting on the inlet side. Clearance matters most. We leave room to pull the lid and baffle without unhooking the sink, because a trap nobody can open is a trap nobody cleans.

    Automatic Grease Removal Units in Sun Valley

    An automatic grease removal unit heats the trapped grease and skims it into a container on a timer, so staff empty a bucket instead of scraping a tank. The purchase price is higher than a passive trap. Plan for a dedicated circuit near the unit, which your electrician will need to run. Fry-heavy kitchens like them most.

    New Restaurant Build-Out Grease Traps in Sun Valley

    On a new build-out, the grease trap gets decided before the slab is poured, not after. The drawings and the equipment list from your kitchen designer tell us what will drain where. Changes get expensive. Saw-cutting a finished floor to relocate a trap can add several days to a build-out, so we flag sizing or location problems at plan review.

    Replacement and Upgrade Services in Sun Valley, NV

    Steel traps from decades back rust through at the baffle seams, and by then the outlet is usually passing grease straight to the sewer. We run a camera down the outlet first to see how much grease got past the old unit. Sometimes the line needs jetting too. If your seats or menu grew since opening, the swap is the time to step up to a bigger GPM rating.

    Residential Grease Interceptor Setup in Sun Valley, NV

    Homes rarely need a grease trap, though some do. Licensed home bakeries and houses on old clay laterals that keep clogging are the usual cases. A compact under-sink unit handles the load, and a homeowner can clean it in a few minutes. Check with your local health office first.
