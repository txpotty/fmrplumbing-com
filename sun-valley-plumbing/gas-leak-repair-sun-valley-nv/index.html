    Commercial Gas Leak Repair in Sun Valley

    Commercial gas piping often runs at 2 psi or higher with regulators at each appliance bank, so a leak test there is not the same as a house test.     Gas Leak Detection in Sun Valley

    Some leaks announce themselves with a hiss you can hear across the room, and others lose a few cubic feet a day and only show up on the bill. The slow ones are where the gauge earns its keep. We isolate, then sniff. Testing happens with the appliance valves shut and again with them open, since some leaks hide past a valve. Finding the exact joint keeps the job small.

    Corrugated Stainless Steel Tubing Repair in Sun Valley, NV

    CSST is the yellow-jacketed flexible tubing used in many newer homes, and it can be damaged by a nearby lightning strike if it was never bonded. Bonding means a 6 AWG copper wire from the gas piping to the electrical grounding system. Plenty of older installs lack it. We repair CSST only with the matching brand's fittings, because TracPipe and Gastite parts do not interchange. A splice that skips that rule fails inspection.

    Outdoor Gas Line Repair in Sun Valley

    Grills, fire pits, pool heaters and patio heaters often sit at the end of a long buried line that nobody has looked at since it went in. More outdoor lines get hit by shovels and fence augers than fail from rust. Dead grass is a tell. The outdoor branch gets isolated and tested by itself, then the bad run is replaced with polyethylene and a tracer. Above-ground stub-ups get a new riser and a shutoff valve.

    Permit and Inspection Coordination in Sun Valley

    Replacing more than a connector usually means a permit, and in Sun Valley the inspector will want to see a gauge holding pressure before the pipe is covered. The permit and the inspection booking are on us. Most inspections take under twenty minutes. The test is set to whatever the local fuel gas code requires, often a few psi held for fifteen minutes or longer. The signed permit card stays with your house.

    Interior Gas Pipe Repair in Sun Valley, NV

    Inside the house, gas leaks hide at unions in the basement and at the threaded nipple behind the furnace, places nobody looks. Testing runs from the meter inward, one section at a time. Drywall stays intact when possible. If the leak is inside a finished wall, running a new CSST line around it often costs less than opening and patching the wall. Nothing is turned back on until the gauge holds steady.

    Appliance Connector Replacement in Sun Valley

    Old uncoated brass connectors, the kind made before the mid-1970s, are the subject of consumer safety warnings because the flare nut can separate from the tube. Any of those still in service should be replaced. We see them weekly. The replacement is a coated stainless connector sized for the appliance, with a new ball valve and a sediment trap where one is required. Bubble testing follows before the burner is lit.

    Gas Line Replacement and Upgrades in Sun Valley, NV

    Adding a gas range or a tankless heater can overload pipe that was sized years ago for just a furnace and a water heater. We add up the BTU load and measure the run length before choosing a pipe size. Undersized pipe starves burners. Old pipe with heavy rust or bad threads gets replaced instead of patched, usually with black iron or CSST depending on access. A big tankless unit may also need a bigger meter from the utility.
