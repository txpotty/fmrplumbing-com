    Pressure Switch Replacement in Sun Valley

    The pressure switch tells the pump when to start and stop. Pitted contacts, clogged sensing tubes and insect damage are common causes of failure. We replace the switch, clean or replace the tube and set the cut-in and cut-out pressures to match your tank. This is one of the most affordable well repairs.

    Diagnosing Low Water Pressure in Sun Valley

    Low water pressure from a well system can be a frustrating problem, often indicating issues beyond just the pump itself. This could be due to a failing pressure switch, a partially clogged well screen, a leak in the supply line, or even a pump that is no longer meeting demand. Our team conducts careful diagnostics to pinpoint the exact cause of low pressure in your Sun Valley home, delivering a targeted and effective repair solution.

    Well Pump Maintenance & Repair Cost Comparison in Sun Valley, NV

    Constant Pressure System Repair in Sun Valley, NV

    Variable speed systems like the Grundfos SQE and Franklin SubDrive keep pressure steady by adjusting motor speed. When they fault out, the drive usually shows a code that guides diagnosis. We troubleshoot drives, sensors and wiring and repair or replace components. These systems need a smaller pressure tank but careful setup.

    Submersible Well Pump Repair in Sun Valley

    Submersible pumps are common in residential wells, designed to operate submerged within the well casing. When these pumps fail, symptoms usually include no water, low water pressure, or strange noises from the well. Our technicians are skilled at diagnosing issues like motor burnout, damaged impellers, or faulty check valves that can affect submersible pump performance. We use dedicated equipment for safe and efficient pump extraction and reinstallation, ensuring the integrity of your well system.

    Control Box and Electrical Repair in Sun Valley, NV

    Three-wire submersible pumps rely on a control box with a start capacitor and relay. Sudden summer storms can cause flash flooding that overwhelms drainage and washes debris into storm drains. Lightning and power surges frequently damage these components. We test capacitors, relays and overload protection and replace failed parts. A surge protector at the control box can help reduce repeat failures.

    Well Pump Repair vs Replacement Cost in Sun Valley, NV

    Control and pressure switch repairs usually cost $150 to $500, while a pressure tank replacement typically runs $600 to $1,500. Pulling and replacing a submersible pump can range from $1,800 to $5,000 or more, depending on depth, horsepower and wire. If your pump is older and already needs pulling, replacing it at the same time can save a second pull later. We lay out both options with clear numbers.

    Well Pump Control Box & Electrical Repair in Sun Valley, NV

    Many well pump failures trace back to electrical issues within the control box or wiring. This can include tripped breakers, faulty capacitors, or corroded connections. Our licensed technicians are trained to safely diagnose and repair these complex electrical components. We ensure all wiring meets Nevada electrical codes, preventing hazards and ensuring reliable pump operation in Sun Valley homes.
