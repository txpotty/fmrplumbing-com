    Sump Pump vs. Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Sun Valley

    Sump pumps and ejector pumps look alike in a catalog but do very different jobs. Sump pumps push groundwater out to the yard, ejectors push sewage to the sewer, and only the ejector pit must be sealed and vented against gas. Never mix the two.

    Cost-Effective Sewage Ejector Pump Upgrades in Sun Valley, NV

    Ejector Pump Alarms and Backup Options in Sun Valley

    A high water alarm belongs on every ejector system, since a pump failure can lead to a sewage backup quickly.
