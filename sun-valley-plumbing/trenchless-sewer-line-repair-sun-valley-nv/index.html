    Perma-Liner and NuFlow Trenchless Systems in Sun Valley

    Perma-Liner and NuFlow both make lining systems we use, each with its own resin chemistry and installation equipment. The brand matters less than the prep, because a liner bonded to a dirty, scaly pipe wall will not hold the way the manufacturer rates it. So the line gets jetted or descaled to bare wall before any resin goes in.

    Cured-In-Place Pipe Lining in Sun Valley

    Prep is most of the job. We jet the line at high pressure to strip roots, grease and loose scale, then camera it again to measure the exact footage before cutting the liner tube to length. The roots cannot get back in.
