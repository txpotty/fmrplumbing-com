    Pressure Vacuum Breaker (PVB) Installation in Sun Valley, NV

    For low-hazard applications, such as lawn irrigation systems in Sun Valley, NV, a pressure vacuum breaker, or PVB, is a common and cost-effective backflow prevention device. PVBs protect against back-siphonage by letting air into the system when pressure drops. Our certified plumbers expertly install PVBs above the highest point of downstream piping, as code requires, to maintain effective protection. We also check for proper clearance and drainage.

    Reduced Pressure Zone (RPZ) Assembly Installation in Sun Valley

    RPZ assemblies offer the highest level of backflow protection and are often required for high-hazard applications in Sun Valley. These devices feature two independent check valves and a relief valve that opens to discharge water if pressure differentials indicate a backflow condition. FMR Plumbing specializes in precise RPZ installation, with proper sizing, placement and testing to meet all regulatory requirements for residential and commercial properties. We work with trusted brands like Watts and Febco.

    Double Check Valve Assembly Installation in Sun Valley

    Double check assemblies are used for low hazard connections such as many fire sprinkler systems and some irrigation lines. They have two spring-loaded check valves and test cocks for annual certification. Unlike an RPZ, they do not discharge water, so they can be installed in vaults or below grade where local rules allow. We size them to avoid excessive pressure loss on your system.

    Reduced Pressure Zone (RPZ) Device Installation in Sun Valley, NV

    For high-hazard applications in Sun Valley, NV where toxic substances could contaminate the water supply, a reduced pressure zone, or RPZ, device is often required. RPZ backflow preventers offer the highest level of protection, using a relief valve to discharge water if the pressure drops. FMR Plumbing specializes in RPZ installation, making sure all components are correctly sized, positioned and set up according to state and local plumbing codes. Proper installation is critical for effective operation.

    Backflow Enclosures and Freeze Protection in Sun Valley, NV

    Above-ground assemblies sit in direct sun in summer and can freeze on cold nights. Wrapping backflow devices, irrigation lines and outdoor spigots before cold nights prevents most freeze damage. We install insulated enclosures, freeze blankets or heated cabinets based on your local climate. Enclosures also discourage theft, since brass backflow assemblies are a common target for scrap thieves. We choose enclosures with enough room for testing and repairs.
