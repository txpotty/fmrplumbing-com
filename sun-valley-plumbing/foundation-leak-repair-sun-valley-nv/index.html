    Tunneling for Foundation Leaks in Sun Valley, NV

    PEX and PVC Foundation Leak Repair in Sun Valley

    Newer houses use PEX or PVC under the slab. Plastic pipe fails too. Most failures we find are at a fitting that was crimped short or a joint that pulled apart when the soil moved, not in the pipe wall itself. FMR Plumbing replaces the fitting with a proper crimp, expansion or solvent-weld connection so the line under your Sun Valley foundation is sound again.

    Direct Foundation Leak Repair in Sun Valley, NV

    Direct repair means cutting into the floor right over the leak. It is often the cheapest fix. We saw a small opening, dig down to the pipe, replace the damaged section and pressure test it, then backfill in layers and patch the concrete flush with the floor around it. Your flooring contractor can lay new tile or wood afterward.

    Foundation Leak Repair Cost Comparison in Sun Valley, NV

    Direct repairs usually cost least up front. Flooring adds to it. A reroute costs somewhat more but avoids concrete work and future leaks in the abandoned pipe, and a repipe fits homes that have already leaked more than once. We line up the full cost of each, flooring and drywall included.

    Drain Line Leak Under Foundation in Sun Valley, NV

    Drain leaks under a slab are harder to find. They aren't pressurized. We plug the line and fill it with water to see if the level drops, then run a sewer camera through it to spot the cracked hub, offset joint or root intrusion that is letting water out. Fixes include tunneling, slab cuts or a pipe liner, and we lay out the trade-offs.
