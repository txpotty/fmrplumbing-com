    What We Pump and Clean in Sun Valley, NV

    Under-Sink Grease Trap Maintenance in Sun Valley, NV

    Under-sink hydromechanical traps are rated by flow, usually 10 to 50 gallons per minute, and they hold far less than people expect. In a busy kitchen, that means cleaning every week or two. We take the lid off, remove grease and solids, scrape the baffles, check the flow control fitting on the inlet, and replace the lid gasket if it is flattened or torn. A bad gasket is why most of these smell.

    Grease Line Jetting in Sun Valley

    Grease that slips past a full trap coats the 4-inch line out to the main. It builds up in rings, thickest at the bottom of the pipe. A snake cuts a narrow path through and the line plugs again within weeks, while a jetter with a rotating nozzle peels the grease off the whole pipe wall. A camera confirms it afterward.

    Residential Grease Trap Cleaning in Sun Valley, NV

    Homes rarely have a grease trap, though a few large custom kitchens and some home-based catering setups in Sun Valley, NV put a small unit under the sink. Most are 10 to 20 gallon boxes. Service means scooping out grease and solids by hand, cleaning the baffle, and seating the gasket so the lid seals tight. About thirty minutes.

    Emergency Grease Trap Cleaning in Sun Valley, NV

    Sometimes the trap is fine and the problem is downstream. We find that out fast with a camera. A trap that is half empty while floor sinks keep backing up points to a clog in the lateral toward the main, which needs jetting, not another pump-out. We can jet on the same visit.

    Indoor Grease Trap Cleaning in Sun Valley

    Indoor traps usually sit under the three-compartment sink or in a recessed pit near the dish station. Room to work is tight.     Odor Control and Trap Inspections in Sun Valley

    A trap that stinks between pump-outs usually has a flattened lid gasket or a vent problem. Both get checked every visit. Odors are often fixed the same day.
