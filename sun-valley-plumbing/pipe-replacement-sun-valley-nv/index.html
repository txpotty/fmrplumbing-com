    Copper Pipe Replacement in Sun Valley, NV

    PEX Pipe Replacement in Sun Valley

    PEX (cross-linked polyethylene) piping has become a common choice for residential pipe replacement due to its flexibility, affordability, and resistance to freezing. Wrapping backflow devices, irrigation lines and outdoor spigots before cold nights prevents most freeze damage. Unlike rigid pipes, PEX can expand, reducing the risk of burst pipes during sudden cold snaps. FMR Plumbing specializes in PEX installation, which often allows for less invasive replacement methods compared to traditional materials. This modern solution offers strong performance and water quality for your home.

    Copper Pinhole Leak Replacement in Sun Valley, NV

    Pinhole leaks in copper usually start as a small green stain or a damp spot on drywall. They are caused by a mix of water chemistry, high velocity and thin Type M pipe, and once they begin they tend to keep appearing. Patching each leak costs $300 to $800 per visit plus drywall repair. We can replace the affected branch or repipe the home with PEX, which does not develop pinholes. We help you weigh the cost of both paths.
