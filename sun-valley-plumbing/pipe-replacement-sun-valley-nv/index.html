    Copper Pipe Replacement in Sun Valley, NV

    Copper still has fans. It is durable and heat tolerant. We install Type L copper for most interior supply lines, sweat or press the joints, while strapping each line properly, so the new pipe does not rattle or sag. Costs more than PEX.

    Copper Pinhole Leak Replacement in Sun Valley, NV

    Pinhole leaks start small. First a damp spot, then drywall damage. They usually mean the copper wall has thinned from corrosion, so finding one often means others are close. Repipe or patch, we explain.

    Emergency Pipe Replacement in Sun Valley, NV

    A burst pipe needs fast action. Close the main shutoff right away. Then call, because water spreading through walls and floors does damage every minute, and our crews aim to stop it fast. Drying comes next.

    Drain Pipe Replacement in Sun Valley, NV

    Drain lines fail too. Cast iron cracks and scales. We replace cracked cast iron, failing ABS or old galvanized drains with PVC, checking slope and venting so the new drains flow freely. Camera first.

    Galvanized Pipe Removal in Sun Valley, NV

    Removing every piece is rarely needed. It is disconnected and capped, while new lines run beside it, so we avoid tearing open walls just to pull out dead pipe. Only accessible pipe comes out.

    PEX Pipe Replacement in Sun Valley

    PEX bends around corners and comes in long rolls, so a typical repipe needs fewer fittings than copper would on the same layout. The northern part of the state sees regular hard freezes, so pipes in exterior walls and crawlspaces need protection. PEX can stretch a little in a freeze instead of splitting. We use PEX-a with expansion fittings where possible, keeping flow strong at each joint. Faster install.
