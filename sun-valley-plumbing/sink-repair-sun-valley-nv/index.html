    Hair Clog Clearing in Sun Valley

    Hair is the bathroom sink's main problem. We pull the stopper, strip the hair mat off it, then run a small hand auger through the trap and into the wall arm instead of pouring in drain cleaner. The overflow channel gets flushed too. That slot smells when it fills with slime.

    P-Trap Repair and Replacement in Sun Valley, NV

    The trap holds a plug of water that keeps sewer gas out of the room. Code calls for a trap seal between 2 and 4 inches, and a trap that leaks, sags or was cut down by a past owner can lose that seal and let the smell in. We replace bad traps with a matched kit. Chrome traps that have rusted thin get swapped for PVC or brass.

    Kitchen Sink Repair in Sun Valley

    Kitchen sinks take the most abuse in the house. Strainers lose their putty seal, sprayer hoses split inside the cabinet, and disposal flanges vibrate loose over the years. We fix the part that is wet. A cracked composite or porcelain bowl is a replacement, and we will say so.

    Burst Supply Line Repair in Sun Valley

    A burst line under a sink can push out several gallons a minute. If the angle stop will not stop it, the main shutoff will, and the sooner it is closed the less water ends up inside the vanity floor and the subfloor below. Then lay towels down. We replace the line and the stop and moisture-check the cabinet.

    Office and Restaurant Sink Repair in Sun Valley
