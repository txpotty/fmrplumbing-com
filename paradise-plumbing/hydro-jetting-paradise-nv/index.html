    Frequently Asked Questions About Hydro Jetting in Paradise

    The cost of hydro jetting in Paradise, NV typically ranges from $300 to $800, depending on the severity of the clog, the length and diameter of the pipe, and ease of access. More complex jobs, like main sewer line descaling or extensive root removal, may be on the higher end. We provide a transparent quote after an initial inspection.

    For most homes in Paradise, hydro jetting every 1 to 3 years can be a good preventative measure, especially if you have recurring drain issues or older pipes. Businesses, particularly restaurants, may benefit from quarterly or twice-yearly hydro jetting to manage grease buildup. We can recommend a schedule based on your needs.

    Repeat clogs in the same line, slow drains after snaking, sewer odors and known grease or root problems are good reasons to consider jetting.

    While hydro jetting is very powerful, it is usually safe for most sound plumbing systems, including PVC, cast iron, and concrete pipes. However, it is not recommended for severely damaged, corroded, or fragile pipes, which is why a pre-jetting camera inspection by FMR Plumbing is crucial to assess pipe integrity and prevent further damage.

    A thorough jetting often keeps a line clear for a year or more, depending on use and roots. Lines with heavy root intrusion may need maintenance or a repair.

    No permit is needed for jetting. If the inspection reveals damage that needs repair, permits may be required and we handle them.

    Drain snaking uses a cable with an auger that punches a hole through a blockage to restore some flow. Hydro jetting, however, uses high-pressure water to completely scour the inside of the pipe, removing all buildup and restoring the pipe to a near-new condition. Hydro jetting offers a more thorough and longer-lasting clean.

    For most modern and well-maintained pipes, hydro jetting is very safe. However, for very old, corroded, or severely damaged pipes, the high pressure could cause further damage.
