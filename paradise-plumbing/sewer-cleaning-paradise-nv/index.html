    Root Removal From Sewer Lines in Paradise

    Trees and shrubs send roots toward any moisture they can find, and a sewer joint is a steady source during dry months. Thirsty yard trees and shrubs are common causes in local yards. We use a sectional cable with a root saw sized to the pipe to cut the mass free and pull it out. Heavy root growth may need a follow-up with a jetter to clear fine root hairs. We clean these lines carefully and use the camera to map joints and soft spots. If the pipe is failing, we will tell you plainly and show the footage. Cleaning keeps you running while you plan a replacement.

    Grease and Scale Buildup Cleaning in Paradise, NV

    Grease from kitchens cools and coats the inside of sewer pipes, and minerals in the water bond with it to form a stubborn crust.
