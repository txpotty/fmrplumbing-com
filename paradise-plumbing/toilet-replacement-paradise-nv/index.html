    Commercial Flush Valve Upgrades in Paradise

    Manual flush valves on older commercial toilets leak at the handle and stick open, and swapping to a sensor valve during a replacement improves hygiene without changing the rough-in. Battery and hardwired models are both available. We do the work after hours if needed, and we check that the new fixtures meet accessibility requirements.

    ADA and Commercial Toilet Replacement in Paradise, NV

    Public restrooms need bowls rated for heavy use, and many need to meet ADA height and clearance rules when a fixture is replaced. Clearances get checked before we order anything. Adding a sensor flush valve during the swap takes little extra time, and they cut down on unflushed bowls.

    Toilet Rough-In Adjustments in Paradise, NV

    Rough-in is the distance from the finished wall to the center of the drain, usually 12 inches, sometimes 10 or 14 in older homes. Take the measurement from the wall to the closet bolts, not from the baseboard trim. If the new toilet's rough-in does not match, it either sits too far out or will not fit. An offset flange can correct an inch or two without moving the drain.

    High-Efficiency Toilet (HET) Upgrades in Paradise, NV

    High-efficiency toilets carry a WaterSense label and a MaP score, which tells you how many grams of waste the toilet clears in one flush. Look for 800 or higher. Higher numbers mean fewer double flushes and fewer plunger trips, and that is where the real water savings come from.

    Water-Saving Toilet Upgrades in Paradise, NV

    Older toilets are one of the largest indoor water users in a house, and replacing a 3.5 gallon model with a 1.28 gallon one cuts toilet water use by more than half. Dual-flush models go lower for liquids. We recommend models with high MaP scores so the savings do not come with double flushing.

    Toilet Flange Repair During Replacement in Paradise

    Pulling the old toilet is the only chance to see the flange, and a cracked plastic ring or a rusted cast iron flange will ruin a new install within a year. We check it every time. For a cracked flange, a stainless repair ring is often enough, while a flange that sits below the finished floor gets a spacer or extender so the seal compresses properly.

    Rental and Multi-Unit Toilet Replacement in Paradise, NV

    Landlords with several units do better standardizing on one toilet model with common parts, because the next flapper or fill valve is then the same part in every unit. We can replace several toilets in one visit. Tenants keep a working bathroom. We coordinate entry directly with them, so you do not have to be there.

    Commercial Toilet Replacement in Paradise, NV

    Office and retail restrooms wear out toilets faster than any house, and a replacement there usually means a floor-mount or wall-hung bowl with a flushometer instead of a tank. We schedule around your hours. Bowls are matched to the flush valve's gallons per flush, since a mismatch leaves waste behind or wastes water.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Toilet Replacement in Paradise

    A standard toilet replacement usually runs $400 to $800 with the new toilet and labor included, depending mostly on the model you choose. Flange or subfloor repairs add to it. Those get their own price before any work, so the base number does not move.

    Homes around Paradise that rely on private wells should test for hardness and other minerals before choosing treatment equipment. Hard water leaves calcium scale in the rim jets and on the flush valve seat, which slowly weakens the flush and makes flappers leak. Glazed trapways help. We recommend models with fully glazed trapways and simple fill valves.

    Watch for cracks, weekly clogs, a base that rocks after it has been reset, and a tank that uses 3.5 gallons or more. Age matters too. A toilet more than 25 years old is usually a good candidate even if nothing is broken yet.

    Most swaps take 1.5 to 2.5 hours. Flange or subfloor repairs can add an hour.

    Replacement wins when repair costs start to approach the price of a new toilet, or when the toilet is old enough to waste water every time it flushes. Cracks settle it. Frequent clogs and a rocking base point the same way.

    TOTO, American Standard, Kohler and Gerber all make good toilets, and we steer people toward the models in each line with a 3 inch flush valve and a strong MaP score. The flush design matters more than the name on the tank. We also weigh how easy the parts are to find.

    Handy homeowners can do it, but the trouble starts when the old toilet comes off and the flange turns out to be cracked or sitting below the new floor. That is where most DIY installs go wrong. Overtightened closet bolts are the other common mistake, and that one cracks a new bowl.

    Our short list starts with TOTO Drake, American Standard Champion, Kohler Highline and Gerber Viper, all of which flush well and use parts you can find at most supply houses. For a tighter budget, Mansfield holds up well. The model gets matched to your rough-in and space.
