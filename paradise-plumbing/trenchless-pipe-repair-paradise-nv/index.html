    Cured-in-Place Pipe (CIPP) Lining in Paradise

    A CIPP liner is a felt tube soaked in resin. It hardens inside the old pipe. We pull or invert the tube into the cleaned line, inflate it against the pipe wall and cure it with hot water, steam or UV light, which leaves a jointless pipe inside the old one. It fixes cracks, breaks and roots without digging up your Paradise yard.

    Cured-in-Place Pipe Lining in Paradise

    The old pipe gets cleaned first. Then the liner goes in. We inflate an epoxy-soaked liner against the wall and cure it with heat or UV, leaving a smooth pipe with no joints. Lining works best for cracks, root intrusion and separated joints.
