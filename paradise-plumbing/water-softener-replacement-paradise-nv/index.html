    Salt-Based Water Softener Replacement in Paradise, NV

    Salt-based water softeners are a common and proven solution for hard water in Nevada. When replacing these units, we carefully disconnect the old brine tank and resin tank, then install the new components. We make sure all connections are leak-free and the drain line is correctly routed. We will also provide guidance on initial setup and salt refills for lasting soft water.

    Softener Loop Installation in Paradise, NV

    Many homes have a softener loop near the main water line that makes for easy installation. When a home lacks one, we can add a loop with shutoff valves and a bypass. We also route a drain line to a laundry box, floor drain or approved location. A good loop keeps outdoor hose bibs on hard water to save salt. This makes future service simpler.

    Salt-Free Water Conditioner Replacement in Paradise, NV

    For homeowners in Paradise, NV seeking an alternative to traditional salt-based systems, salt-free water conditioners offer a different approach. These units, sometimes called descalers, condition water to prevent mineral scale without removing the minerals or adding salt. While they do not produce soft feeling water, they reliably protect pipes and appliances from scale. We can replace your existing salt-free system or help you transition from a salt-based unit.

    Commercial Softener Replacement in Paradise
