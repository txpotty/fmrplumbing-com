    Cured-In-Place Pipe Lining in Paradise

    Cured-in-place lining creates a new pipe inside the old one. We clean the line, then pull or invert a resin-saturated liner into place and cure it with hot water, steam or UV light. The result is a seamless, jointless pipe that roots cannot penetrate. Lining works well for cracked or root-intruded lines that still hold their form.

    Perma-Liner and NuFlow Trenchless Technologies in Paradise

    FMR Plumbing uses proven trenchless technologies from trusted brands like Perma-Liner and NuFlow. These systems are known for their durability, effectiveness and adherence to plumbing standards. Using these materials and equipment means your trenchless sewer line repair in Paradise is not only low impact but also a high-quality, long-lasting solution, backed by manufacturer warranties.

    Cost Comparison of Trenchless vs Traditional in Paradise, NV

    While the upfront cost of trenchless repair might sometimes seem higher than traditional excavation, it often becomes more cost-effective when you count the savings on landscaping, driveway or foundation repair. For many Paradise, NV homeowners, avoiding extensive property restoration makes trenchless the more sensible long-term choice.

    Cast Iron Sewer Line Repair in Paradise, NV

    Cast iron drains and sewer lines corrode from the inside, leaving rough scale, cracks and channel rot along the bottom of the pipe. We descale the pipe with chain knockers or cutters, then line it to restore a smooth, sealed interior. This is particularly useful for cast iron running under slabs where excavation is costly. Lining can add many years of service.

    Causes of Sewer Line Damage in Paradise, NV

    Sewer lines in Paradise, NV can suffer damage from several factors, including shifting soils that cause pipes to crack or separate. Clay soils that swell when watered and shrink when dry can stress slabs and buried drain lines over time. Tree root intrusion is another major cause, as roots seek out moisture in pipes and grow, causing blockages and structural damage. Age and corrosion, especially in older cast iron or clay pipes, also add to deterioration. Our trenchless solutions address these common issues effectively.

    Sectional Point Repair in Paradise

    When only one section of a line is damaged, a point repair can fix it without lining the whole length. We position a short resin liner at the damaged spot using the camera and inflate it until it cures. Point repairs are a cost-effective fix for a single crack, hole or root entry. They are a good fit on otherwise healthy lines.
