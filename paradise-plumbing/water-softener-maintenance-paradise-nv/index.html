    Control Valve Diagnostics and Repair in Paradise

    The control valve is the brain of your water softener, controlling regeneration cycles and water flow. Our maintenance service includes complete diagnostics of the control valve's programming and mechanical components. We check for proper timing, motor function and potential blockages.     Resin Bed Analysis and Regeneration in Paradise

    The resin bed is the heart of your water softener, where ion exchange occurs.     Control Valve and Injector Service in Paradise, NV

    The control valve directs water through backwash, brine draw, rinse and refill. Tiny injectors and screens inside the valve clog with sediment and salt residue, which stops brine draw. We disassemble the injector, clean the screens and replace worn seals and spacers on valves like the Fleck 5600 and Clack WS1. A clean valve gives you a full regeneration every time.
