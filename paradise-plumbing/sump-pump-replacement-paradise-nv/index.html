    Frequently Asked Questions About Sump Pump Replacement in Paradise

    The cost of sump pump replacement in Paradise, NV usually ranges from $500 to $1,500, depending on the type of pump, submersible or pedestal, its horsepower and any needed pit changes. Battery backup systems will add to this cost, usually between $300 and $800. We provide a detailed, upfront quote after assessing your needs.

    Most standard sump pump replacements can be finished within 2 to 4 hours. More complex installations, such as those requiring new pit excavation or extensive discharge line work, might take longer, up to a full day.

    A battery backup sump pump system is a secondary pump powered by a battery, designed to activate automatically if your primary sump pump loses power during an outage or fails. This is critical for maintaining flood protection during power outages caused by severe weather.

    It is recommended to test your sump pump at least every three months, and especially before and after rainy seasons. To test, simply pour a bucket of water into the sump pit. The pump should activate, drain the water and then shut off automatically. If it does not, it is time for a professional inspection.

    Yes, in addition to replacement, we offer sump pump maintenance services to keep your system ready. Regular maintenance, including cleaning the pit, checking the float switch and inspecting the discharge line, can extend the life of your pump and prevent surprise failures. This is especially important for homes in Paradise that rely heavily on their sump pumps.

    To maintain your new sump pump, regularly check the pit for debris, test the pump monthly by pouring water into the pit, and make sure the discharge pipe is clear. An annual professional inspection by FMR Plumbing can also help lengthen its lifespan.

    Most sump pumps last 7 to 10 years. Pumps that sit idle for long periods can fail earlier due to stuck switches.

    A primary sump pump is your main defense against basement flooding, powered by your home's electricity. A backup sump pump, usually battery-powered or water-powered, is a secondary system that kicks on if the primary pump fails, loses power or cannot keep up with heavy water inflow. Many Paradise homes benefit from a backup system due to storm risks.
