    Which Sump Pump Suits Your Paradise, NV Home

    Combination Sump Pump System Replacement in Paradise, NV

    Combination units put a primary AC pump and a DC backup on one discharge manifold, so you get two pumps and two floats in a single assembly. They are handy when the basin is too small for two pumps side by side. We replace the whole assembly or just the failed side.

    Submersible Sump Pump Replacement in Paradise

    A submersible pump sits at the bottom of the pit, sealed in an oil-filled housing, and it runs quieter than anything else you can put down there. Out comes the old pump, out comes the muck at the bottom of the basin, and the new one goes in on a brick to stay clear of silt. The check valve gets replaced at the same time. A worn valve lets water fall back and makes the pump run twice as often.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Systems in Paradise

    Sudden summer storms can cause flash flooding that overwhelms drainage and washes debris into storm drains. A standard pump just sits there once the power drops. The fix is a second, 12-volt pump on its own float, set a little higher than the main pump and wired to a charger and deep-cycle battery. It also covers a stuck primary switch. The alarm will tell you which one is running.

    Choosing a Backup Battery in Paradise

    Battery choice decides how long the backup actually runs. Most backups use a sealed AGM or deep-cycle battery around 75 to 100 amp-hours, which covers several hours of on-and-off pumping. We size it to how often your pump runs on a wet day. Plan on a new battery every three to five years.

    Emergency Sump Pump Replacement in Paradise, NV

    A pit that is filling with the pump silent means you have maybe an hour or two before water crosses the basement floor, depending on how fast it is coming in. Check the breaker and the GFCI outlet first, then lift the lid and see whether the float is stuck. A wet vac buys time. We stock the common pumps on the truck, and most emergency swaps are done on the first trip.

    Pedestal Pumps for Shallow Pits in Paradise, NV

    Older basements sometimes have a narrow, shallow pit poured right into the floor, and a pedestal pump is often the only thing that fits without breaking concrete. We replace the pump and float rod and rerun the PVC up to the check valve. Cheaper than a submersible, too.

    High-Capacity Sump Pump Upgrades in Paradise

    If your pump runs every 30 seconds during a storm and still loses ground, it is undersized for the water coming in. First we time the pump cycles and measure the pit refill rate. From there it is math. Often a 1/2 or 3/4 HP pump with more GPM at your lift, plus a 2-inch discharge line, solves it.

    Sewage Ejector Pump Replacement in Paradise

    Basement bathrooms below the sewer line drain into a sealed, vented basin, and the ejector pump in that basin has to pass 2-inch solids. That is not a sump pump job. The ejector and check valve get replaced, then we reseal the lid and check the vent connection. A bad seal shows up as sewer odor.
