    Water Line Repair at the Meter in Paradise, NV

    Leaks often start at the compression fittings and couplings right next to the meter box. Shifting soil and heavy traffic over the box can loosen those joints over time. When the leak is yours, we replace the fittings and the customer shutoff valve in the same visit.

    Copper Water Line Repair in Paradise

    Copper pipes are known for their durability and longevity, but they can still develop leaks due to corrosion, excessive water pressure, or physical damage. In Paradise, we regularly see pinhole leaks in older copper lines. Our skilled plumbers are adept at repairing copper water lines, whether it involves soldering a small patch or replacing a damaged section. We make sure all repairs meet current plumbing standards for a reliable, leak-free connection.

    Main Water Line Repair in Paradise

    The main water line brings water from the public supply to your home. When this line leaks or breaks, it can cause significant water loss, foundation damage, and low water pressure. Our team specializes in diagnosing and repairing main water line issues, from pinpoint leaks to full pipe ruptures, so your home's primary water source is secure. We use advanced detection methods to pinpoint the exact location of the problem, minimizing excavation and disruption to your property in Paradise.     Galvanized Water Line Replacement in Paradise, NV

    Many homes built before the 1970s still have galvanized steel service lines that rust from the inside. Homes around Paradise that rely on private wells should test for hardness and other minerals before choosing treatment equipment. Hard water speeds up scale buildup, which chokes flow and causes rust-colored water. Patching galvanized pipe rarely lasts because the surrounding pipe is just as corroded. We usually recommend replacing the full run from meter to house with copper or PEX, which restores pressure and stops repeat leaks.

    Repair vs. Replace Your Water Line in Paradise, NV

    A single leak on a newer copper or PEX line is usually a simple spot repair. Repeat leaks, galvanized pipe or a line older than about 40 years usually justify full replacement. Spot repairs often run a few hundred dollars, while a full replacement costs more but resets the clock for decades. We show you the condition of the pipe we expose so you can make the call with real information.

    Trenchless Water Line Replacement in Paradise

    If your service line runs under a driveway, pool deck or mature landscaping, trenchless replacement can save a lot of restoration cost. We dig small pits at each end and pull new HDPE or PEX pipe through using a pneumatic boring tool or pipe pulling equipment. The process usually takes one day. You keep your pavers and trees, and the new line carries a long manufacturer warranty.
