    Frequently Asked Questions About Toilet Installation in Paradise

    The cost of toilet installation in Paradise, NV typically ranges from $200 to $500, not including the toilet fixture itself. This price can vary based on factors like the type of toilet, any necessary plumbing modifications, or the complexity of removing an old unit.

    A standard toilet installation, assuming no major plumbing modifications are needed, usually takes about 1 to 3 hours. More complex installations, such as those requiring new rough-ins or dealing with damaged subflooring, may take longer.

    Installing a new toilet can offer several benefits, including improved water efficiency, lower water bills, better flushing power, a better look, and increased comfort. Modern toilets are also designed to be more durable and simpler to clean.

    TOTO, Kohler and American Standard consistently perform well in flush tests and have good parts availability. Gerber and Mansfield are solid budget options. We help you match the brand and model to your needs.

    A comfort height toilet has a bowl that is taller than standard models, typically around 17 to 19 inches from the floor to the top of the seat. This height is closer to a standard chair, making it more comfortable for many adults, especially those with mobility issues.

    When choosing a new toilet, consider factors like bowl shape (round vs. elongated), height (standard vs. ADA-compliant), flushing system (gravity-fed vs. pressure-assisted), water efficiency (gallons per flush), and overall style to match your bathroom.
