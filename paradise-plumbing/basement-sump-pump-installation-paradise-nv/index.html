    Watch for a pump that runs constantly, cycles on and off rapidly, makes grinding noises or never seems to turn on. Rust stains, a stuck float and water sitting above the switch level are also red flags.

    FMR Plumbing provides a warranty on our labor for basement sump pump installations, typically for one year.     Manufacturer warranties typically range from 3 to 5 years on quality cast iron pumps. We also back our installation labor, and we will walk you through the specific coverage for the model you choose.

    It is recommended to test your sump pump every 2 to 3 months, especially before and after the rainy season. Simply pour a bucket of water into the sump pit to make sure the pump activates, drains the water and shuts off properly. Regular testing helps catch issues early.

    While it might seem like a DIY project, basement sump pump installation involves complex plumbing, electrical work and proper discharge routing. It is always best to hire a licensed plumber for a correct, code-compliant installation in Paradise.

    Typically, standard home insurance policies do not cover a new sump pump installation or repairs unless the damage was caused by a sudden, accidental event like a burst pipe, rather than gradual wear and tear or groundwater seepage.
