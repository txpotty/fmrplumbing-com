    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Heater Installation in Paradise

    The cost of water heater installation in Paradise, NV typically ranges from $1,200 to $3,500, depending on the type of water heater (tank vs. tankless), its capacity, fuel type (gas or electric) and any necessary plumbing or electrical modifications. We provide a written, upfront quote after assessing your particular needs.

    To improve efficiency, ensure your new water heater is properly sized for your home, consider a tankless model for on-demand heating, insulate hot water pipes and set the thermostat to 120°F (49°C) to prevent scalding and cut energy consumption.

    The decision to repair or replace often depends on the age of your unit, the cost of the repair and its energy efficiency. If your water heater is over 10 years old, often breaks down, or the repair cost is more than 50% of a new unit, replacement is often the more economical choice. Newer models also offer significantly better energy efficiency.

    If your home has a pressure reducing valve or check valve, an expansion tank is usually required. It protects your water heater and pipes from pressure spikes.

    A traditional tank water heater typically lasts between 8 and 12 years with proper maintenance. Tankless water heaters often have a longer lifespan, ranging from 15 to 20 years, thanks to their durable components and on-demand operation.

    An expansion tank absorbs excess water pressure created as water heats up inside the tank. Many homes in Paradise, NV, especially those with closed-loop plumbing systems, are required by code to have an expansion tank to protect the water heater and plumbing system from damage.

    New water heaters typically come with a manufacturer's warranty on the tank and components, usually ranging from 6 to 12 years. FMR Plumbing also provides a labor warranty on our installation work, ensuring peace of mind. We will discuss specific warranty details for your chosen unit during the consultation.

    We install Rheem, A.O. Smith, Bradford White, Navien, Rinnai and other trusted brands. We recommend models based on reliability, warranty and budget.
