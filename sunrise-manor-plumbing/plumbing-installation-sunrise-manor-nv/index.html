    Whole-House Water Softener Installation in Sunrise Manor

    Water in much of Nevada is very hard, and scale buildup is one of the most common reasons water heaters and fixtures fail early. Softener sizing comes down to hardness times daily gallons, which tells us how many grains the resin has to remove between regenerations. A unit sized too small regenerates constantly and wastes salt. We set the drain line with an air gap. Programming follows, set to your hardness, and we run one test cycle.

    PEX Pipe Installation in Sunrise Manor, NV

    PEX bends around corners, shrugs off corrosion and needs far fewer fittings than copper. In a repipe, fewer fittings means fewer hidden joints and fewer chances for a leak to soak a wall. It can also stretch when water freezes. Freezing weather is uncommon in the southern desert of Nevada, though occasional cold nights can still damage exposed outdoor pipes. That stretch is not a license to skip insulation, so exterior-wall and crawl-space runs still get wrapped.

    Gas Line Installation in Sunrise Manor, NV

    A new range or an outdoor kitchen all start with the same math: total BTU load and the length of the run. We size the pipe from that, pick black iron or CSST, and trench where the line goes underground. Every joint gets a soap test. Then the whole line holds pressure on a gauge before the inspector sees it.

    Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Sunrise Manor, NV

    A basement bathroom sits below the sewer line, so gravity cannot carry the waste out. The fix is a sealed basin with an ejector pump that lifts waste to the main. We size the pump and basin to the fixtures it serves, vent the basin to code, and put a check valve and a full-port shutoff on the discharge so the pump can be pulled for service. We usually install Liberty or Zoeller pumps.
