    A snake bores a hole through the clog. A jet scrubs the full wall. The snake leaves grease, scale and root fibers on the pipe walls where they catch the next clog, while jetting removes that buildup so the line stays clear far longer.

    Jetting fits when the same line clogs again and again. A camera confirms it. If the footage shows heavy grease, scale or roots coating the pipe wall, jetting is the right fix, but if it shows a broken or collapsed section, the line needs repair first.

    No approval is needed for routine jetting. It is treated as maintenance. Permits only apply if we move on to a repair or a new cleanout.
