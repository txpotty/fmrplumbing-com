    Sewer Pipe Relining in Sunrise Manor, NV

    Relining builds a new pipe inside the old one. The old pipe gets jetted clean before anything else. A flexible liner saturated with epoxy is pulled or inverted into place, inflated against the old wall and left to cure, so every crack and leaking joint is covered by one jointless pipe. The finished pipe loses a little diameter. For most houses the flow difference is negligible.
