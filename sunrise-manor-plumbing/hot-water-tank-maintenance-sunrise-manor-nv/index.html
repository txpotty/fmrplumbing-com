    Electric Element and Thermostat Service in Sunrise Manor

    Electric tanks rely on upper and lower heating elements that get crusted with scale in hard water. We test each element with a meter, check the thermostats and look for scorched wires at the terminal block. If an element has failed we replace it with the matching wattage and voltage. Plenty of families who think they need a new heater just need a lower element and a flush.

    Gas Burner and Venting Tune-Up in Sunrise Manor, NV

    On gas tanks we clean the burner, inspect the thermocouple or flame sensor and look for soot, rust flakes or scorching around the combustion chamber. We check the draft hood and vent connector for proper slope and secure joints. Dust and lint from garages can block the flame arrestor screen on newer FVIR models, which causes the pilot to drop out. A clean burner gives a steady blue flame and lower gas bills.

    Emergency Hot Water Tank Inspections in Sunrise Manor

    Commercial and Rental Tank Maintenance in Sunrise Manor

    Property managers, restaurants and small offices often run several tanks that work much harder than a home unit.     Relief Valve and Expansion Tank Testing in Sunrise Manor

    The temperature and pressure relief valve is the part that keeps a tank from becoming dangerous. We lift the lever, confirm it opens and reseats and check the discharge line for proper material and termination. We also check your thermal expansion tank pressure with a gauge, since a waterlogged expansion tank lets pressure jump every heating cycle. High street pressure is common in many service areas, so we test static pressure at a hose bib too.

    Attic and Garage Tank Checkups in Sunrise Manor, NV

    Many homes put the water heater in a garage or attic where temperatures can swing widely. Heat speeds up the wear on plastic drain valves, gas valve seals and wiring insulation. We check drain pans, pan drain lines, platform height for gas units in garages and clearances around the tank. A leak in an attic tank without a working pan can destroy a ceiling, so we confirm that protection is in place.

    Anode Rod Inspection & Replacement in Sunrise Manor, NV

    The anode rod is your hot water tank's sacrificial protector against corrosion. Mineral deposits clog aerators, showerheads and valves, which is why softeners and regular water heater flushing are popular. During our maintenance, we pull and inspect the anode rod. If it's significantly depleted, we recommend and perform a replacement to extend the life of your tank in Sunrise Manor, NV, preventing rust from eating away at the tank lining.
