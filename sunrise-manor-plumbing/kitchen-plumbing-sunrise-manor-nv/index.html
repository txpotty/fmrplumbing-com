    Watch for slow drains, a sour smell from the sink, gurgling when the dishwasher drains, or a disposal that hums but will not grind. Look in the cabinet as well. A warped or stained cabinet base means a slow leak has been going for a while.

    Keep grease, coffee grounds, rice and pasta out of the drain. Use cold water while the disposal runs. Cold water keeps grease solid so the disposal can chop it up and push it through instead of letting it coat the pipe walls. Put a strainer in the second bowl as well.
