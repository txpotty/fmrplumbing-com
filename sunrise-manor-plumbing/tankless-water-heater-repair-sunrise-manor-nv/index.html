    Gas Tankless Water Heater Repair in Sunrise Manor

    Igniter & Flame Rod Repair in Sunrise Manor, NV

    Gas tankless heaters light with a hot surface or spark igniter, then prove flame with a flame rod. A rod coated in oxide reads low on a microamp meter and the board shuts the gas valve after a few seconds. We clean or replace the rod, check the igniter for cracks or weak spark, and confirm the gas valve opens on call. Five-minute fix sometimes.

    Noritz Tankless Water Heater Repair in Sunrise Manor, NV

    Noritz heaters hold up well, but their flow sensors and vent connections still wear out. A code 90 points to combustion trouble, often a dirty fan or a restricted intake.     Rinnai Error Code Repair in Sunrise Manor

    Rinnai owners usually call about a code 10, which points to an air supply or exhaust blockage.     Recirculation Pump Repair in Sunrise Manor

    Recirculation cuts the wait for hot water at a far bathroom from a minute or more to a few seconds.
