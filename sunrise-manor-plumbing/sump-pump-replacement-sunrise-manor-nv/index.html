    Pedestal Sump Pump Replacement in Sunrise Manor, NV

    Pedestal sump pumps have their motor mounted above the sump pit, making them easier to access for maintenance and often more long-lasting. We provide expert pedestal sump pump replacement in Sunrise Manor, NV, with proper alignment and secure mounting. This type is often preferred in shallower pits or where noise is not a major concern, offering a robust solution for flood prevention.

    Sump Pump Basin and Pit Replacement in Sunrise Manor, NV

    Sometimes it is not just the pump that needs replacing, since the sump basin or pit itself might be cracked, damaged or too small. FMR Plumbing offers sump pump basin and pit replacement in Sunrise Manor, NV so your entire system works properly. A properly sized and intact pit is essential for the efficient operation and longevity of your new sump pump.

    Float Switch and Check Valve Replacement in Sunrise Manor

    Many sump pump failures start with a stuck float switch or a leaking check valve. A failed check valve lets water flow back into the pit and causes the pump to short cycle. We install new check valves with every pump replacement and verify the switch moves without binding. Vertical float switches work well in tight pits. Tethered floats work best in larger pits.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Installation in Sunrise Manor

    Power outages during severe storms can leave a primary sump pump useless, leading to basement flooding. A battery backup sump pump offers crucial protection during these times. We install and replace complete battery backup systems, including dedicated marine-grade batteries and smart charging units. Our team helps you select a system with enough run-time to protect your Sunrise Manor home through extended power interruptions. That gives you peace of mind when the lights go out.

    Commercial Pit Pump Replacement in Sunrise Manor

    Commercial buildings often have elevator pits, parking garage drains and mechanical room sumps. These require pumps rated for higher flow and sometimes oil-sensing controls. We replace commercial pumps and alarms to meet code and insurance requirements. Duplex systems with alternating pumps provide a backup. We schedule work to avoid disrupting operations.

    Combination Sump Pump System Replacement in Sunrise Manor, NV

    For maximum protection, many homeowners in Sunrise Manor choose a combination sump pump system, which pairs a primary electric pump with a battery backup pump in one unit. This keeps the system running even if the power fails or the primary pump malfunctions. FMR Plumbing can replace your existing combination system or install a new one, evaluating your needs for groundwater management. We make sure the components are well matched for strong performance and longevity.

    Sewage Ejector Pump Replacement in Sunrise Manor

    Basement bathrooms and laundry rooms below the sewer line need sewage ejector pumps instead of sump pumps. These pumps handle solids and must be installed in sealed, vented basins. We replace ejector pumps with models rated for two inch solids. We also check the basin seal, vent and discharge check valve. Proper installation prevents sewer gas odors.

    High-Capacity Sump Pump Upgrades in Sunrise Manor

    If your current sump pump struggles to keep up with heavy water flow, especially during intense thunderstorms, it may be undersized. We offer high-capacity sump pump replacements and upgrades designed to handle large water volumes.
