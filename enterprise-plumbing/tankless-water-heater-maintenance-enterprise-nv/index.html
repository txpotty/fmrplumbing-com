    Step-by-Step Tankless Flushing in Enterprise

    First we shut off the gas and power, then close both isolation valves so the heater is cut off from the house plumbing. The pump pulls solution from a bucket, through the cold port, out the hot port and back again. After the cycle we rinse with clean water and pull the inlet screen. Then we check for leaks.

    Rinnai Tankless Service in Enterprise

    Rinnai units tell you a lot through the controller. A code 11 is a failed ignition, while code 12 is a flame that lit and then dropped out. We clean the Rinnai air intake filter and look over the flame rod for soot or cracks. The LC codes are scale warnings, and they usually clear once the exchanger has been flushed and the unit is reset.

    Tankless Error Code Diagnosis in Enterprise, NV

    An error code is the heater telling you what it saw. Rinnai LC codes point to scale, Navien E003 is a failed ignition and Noritz 90 points at combustion air or exhaust trouble. We pull the code history before touching anything. If a part has failed, you hear the repair and the price before we start.

    Noritz Tankless Tune-Ups in Enterprise, NV

    Noritz builds a solid heater, but solid does not mean maintenance-free. We start a Noritz tune-up at the exhaust, checking for soot and any blocked termination. Next comes the combustion air intake and the fan. Last, we check the temperature setting and run a few fixtures at once, which shows whether the unit holds temperature between back-to-back showers.

    Same-Day Tankless Help in Enterprise, NV

    A blinking error code and a cold shower usually mean the heater protected itself from something. Usually it is a blocked exhaust or heavy scale. We bring common parts and a descaling kit on emergency calls, so most heaters are firing again before we pack up the truck.

    Rinnai Air Filter and Flame Rod Care in Enterprise

    Rinnai cabinets are tight. We wash the air intake filter and check the flame rod for carbon. On recirculating models we also check the pump schedule, because a wrong setting burns gas for nothing.

    Heat Exchanger Descaling in Enterprise

    Picture a bundle of thin tubes with a gas flame under them and cold water racing through at three or four gallons a minute. That is your heat exchanger. Minerals settle on the hottest tube walls first, and the passages get narrower. A 45 to 60 minute circulation with descaling solution dissolves it.

    Navien Condensing Tune-Ups in Enterprise

    Navien NPE units are condensing heaters with a second heat exchanger that pulls extra heat out of the exhaust before it leaves the house. The secondary exchanger produces acidic water. We clear the condensate trap and check the neutralizer media. On A-series models with the built-in recirculation pump, we test the pump and the buffer tank too.
