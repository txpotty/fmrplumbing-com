    Well Pump and Pressure Tank Replacement in Enterprise, NV

    Rural properties near Enterprise, NV need the well pump for all their water, so a failure is an emergency. Watch for rising and falling pressure, sputtering taps, nonstop running or a breaker that trips, since each points to the pump, switch or tank. Pumps rarely recover once this starts.

    Well Pressure Tank Replacement in Enterprise

    A well pump that clicks on and off every few seconds usually points to a failed pressure tank. When the bladder inside the tank ruptures, the tank fills with water, loses its air cushion and forces the pump to start every time someone opens a tap, which burns out pump motors years early. Replacing the tank stops it. The pressure switch gets checked too.

    Water Softener Replacement in Enterprise, NV

    Softeners wear out as the resin breaks down and the control valve stops cycling properly. Water in much of Nevada is very hard, and scale buildup is one of the most common reasons water heaters and fixtures fail early. When scale starts coming back on fixtures, spots show up on dishes or the brine tank never drops, we test the water hardness, size a new unit to your household and plumb it in with a bypass valve so the house keeps running if the softener ever needs service. Salt use often drops too.
