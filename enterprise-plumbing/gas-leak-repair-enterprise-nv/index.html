    Commercial Gas Leak Services in Enterprise

    Restaurant kitchens and small shops run more appliances on bigger pipe, often 1-1/4 inch or larger, fed from a meter with its own regulator.     Appliance Connector Replacement in Enterprise

    Old uncoated brass connectors, the kind made before the mid-1970s, are the subject of consumer safety warnings because the flare nut can separate from the tube. Any of those still in service should be replaced. We see them weekly. The replacement is a coated stainless connector sized for the appliance, with a new ball valve and a sediment trap where one is required. Bubble testing follows before the burner is lit.

    Residential Gas Leak Detection in Enterprise

    A gas leak in a house rarely sits where the smell is strongest, because gas drifts along joists and collects in closets and basements. The first step is a pressure gauge on the piping, which tells us whether the system is actually losing gas. Then the sniffer comes out. Our combustible gas detector reads in parts per million, so we can follow the reading fitting by fitting until it peaks. Bubble solution on the suspect joint gives the final answer.

    Gas Meter to House Line Repair in Enterprise, NV

    The run from the utility meter to the house shutoff is the homeowner's pipe in most service areas, so the repair falls to a plumber and not the gas company. That stretch sees more weather than any other pipe in the system. Risers rust at grade. We replace the riser or the buried run, pressure test it and leave the meter connection ready for the utility to open the meter. Expect a half day for a typical riser swap.

    Corrugated Stainless Steel Tubing Repair in Enterprise, NV

    CSST is the yellow-jacketed flexible tubing used in many newer homes, and it can be damaged by a nearby lightning strike if it was never bonded. Bonding means a 6 AWG copper wire from the gas piping to the electrical grounding system. Plenty of older installs lack it. We repair CSST only with the matching brand's fittings, because TracPipe and Gastite parts do not interchange. A splice that skips that rule fails inspection.

    Appliance Gas Line Repair in Enterprise, NV

    Most appliance leaks show up at the flex connector or the shutoff valve behind the appliance, usually right at a flare nut where the two meet. Connectors get bent every time a range is pulled out to clean behind it. Valves weep at the stem. We swap in a new coated stainless connector, add a quarter-turn ball valve and set a sediment trap where the code calls for it. Each joint gets a detector pass and a bubble test before the pilot is relit.
