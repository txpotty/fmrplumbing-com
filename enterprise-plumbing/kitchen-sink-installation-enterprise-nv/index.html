    Most new kitchen sinks are paired with a disposal and dishwasher. We mount the disposal, connect the dishwasher drain through an air gap or high loop, and install the dishwasher supply. Doing it all at once saves you time and money. We test every connection under real use.

    Workstation Sink Installation in Enterprise, NV

    Workstation sinks include ledges for cutting boards, colanders and drying racks. They are usually deep, single-bowl designs with offset drains. We position the drain to fit your cabinet and disposal and confirm the faucet reach. These sinks are popular in modern kitchen remodels.

    Double Bowl Sink and Continuous Waste in Enterprise

    Double bowl sinks need a continuous waste or end outlet layout to drain both sides through one trap. We plan the layout around your disposal and dishwasher connections. Proper slope keeps both sides draining freely. We use heavy-wall fittings that resist leaks.

    Undermount Kitchen Sink Installation in Enterprise

    Undermount sinks need strong clips and adhesive to stay attached to granite or quartz. We mount the bowl, seal the rim with silicone, and support it while the adhesive sets. Then we install strainers, traps and supply lines. A proper undermount install prevents gaps and leaks along the counter edge.
