    What We Fix on Faucets in Enterprise, NV

    Ball Faucet Repair in Enterprise, NV

    Ball faucets have a single lever over a domed cap, and the drip usually comes from the two small rubber seats and springs that press against the ball. Most of them just need new seats and springs. Loose handles happen too. If the handle leaks, snugging the adjusting ring under the cap often fixes it.

    Specialty Faucet Repair in Enterprise, NV

    Pot fillers, laundry tub faucets and wall-mount utility faucets each have their own valve styles, and many use compression stems or quarter-turn cartridges that hardware stores do not stock. The common stems ride on the truck. Filtered water faucets at the kitchen sink get new cartridges or check valves as needed.

    Cracked Faucet Body Repair in Enterprise

    Cracked faucet bodies, usually from freezing or an old brass casting that finally gave out, cannot be repaired and need a replacement faucet. Turn off the stops under the sink. We can usually install a new faucet the same day, and we will check the supply lines and stops while the water is off.

    Hard Water Faucet Repair in Enterprise

    Scale is the biggest reason faucet seals wear early, because the mineral crust scratches rubber and keeps ceramic discs from closing flat. It happens faster with hard water. We clean the valve body when the old part comes out, so the new cartridge or washer seals against a smooth surface.

    Delta and Moen Faucet Repair in Enterprise, NV

    Delta and Moen are the faucets we repair most, and both back their cartridges with lifetime warranties for the original owner on many models. Ask us to check. Kohler, Pfister and American Standard each use proprietary cartridges, and we stock the common ones for each brand.

    Disc Faucet Repair in Enterprise

    Ceramic disc faucets last a long time, but grit from a water main repair or scale from hard water can scratch the discs or wear out the rubber inlet seals underneath. Inlet seals cost very little. A scratched disc means a new cartridge, since the two discs are lapped to fit each other and cannot be resurfaced.

    Low Water Flow Faucet Repair in Enterprise

    Sputtering flow, where the water spits and surges, often means air or debris in the line after a water main repair, or a partly clogged cartridge screen. Flushing usually clears it. Pull-down faucets also have check valves in the hose that can stick, and we clean or replace those.

    Leaking Faucet Handle and Base Repair in Enterprise, NV

    Water seeping around the handle or pooling at the base usually means failed O-rings on the spout or cartridge, or a mounting nut that has worked loose under the sink. Check the cabinet floor. New O-rings go on and the mounting nut gets tightened, then reseal the base so water on the counter cannot get under it.
