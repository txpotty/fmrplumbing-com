    Hard Water and Mineral Considerations in Enterprise

    Water in much of Nevada is very hard, and scale buildup is one of the most common reasons water heaters and fixtures fail early. Hard water leaves scale on disposal grind rings, sink flanges and drain tailpieces. Over time that buildup can make a disposal noisy and cause slow drainage. We inspect the drain line for scale and debris before connecting a new unit. If heavy buildup is present, we recommend cleaning or replacing the trap and tailpiece at the same time. Stainless steel grind components on Evolution models resist corrosion better in hard water.
