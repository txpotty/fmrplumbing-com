    How We Install Showers in Enterprise, NV

    Accessible and ADA Shower Installation in Enterprise, NV

    Accessible showers start with blocking in the wall. We add solid wood blocking for grab bars while the studs are bare, set the valve within reach of a seat and install a handheld on a slide bar at a height that works seated or standing. Thresholds stay low or flat. We follow ADA guidelines when you ask.

    Gym and Locker Room Showers in Enterprise

    Gym showers run back to back from open to close. We install heavy-duty commercial valves, push-button or metered controls when you want to limit run time, and drains sized for several showers at once. Accessible stalls get handhelds on slide bars. Work is phased so part of the room stays open.

    Rain and Multi-Head Shower Installation in Enterprise

    Rain heads pull up to 2.5 gallons per minute each. Add a handheld and a couple of body sprays and you can be running more water than a standard 40-gallon water heater can keep hot for a long shower. The water heater gets sized up. Thermostatic valves with volume controls run the outlets.

    Walk-In Shower Installation in Enterprise

    Walk-in showers depend on floor slope. With no curb, the floor needs a steady quarter-inch-per-foot pitch to the drain and waterproofing that extends past the opening. The slope is our job. We add blocking for grab bars while the studs are bare.

    Custom Shower System Installation in Enterprise, NV

    Multi-head showers depend on valve capacity. One head works fine off a standard valve, while multiple outlets need a thermostatic valve, volume controls and larger 3/4-inch supply lines. We plan the layout first. The water heater gets checked for capacity too.

    Shower Valve and Fixture Installation in Enterprise, NV

    Pressure-balancing valves are the baseline for any new shower. Thermostatic valves hold a set temperature and add volume control, which works well with rain heads and handhelds. Both prevent scalding. The outlet count usually decides it.

    Barrier-Free Walk-In Showers in Enterprise, NV

    Barrier-free entries need careful drain placement. The membrane and slope get planned with the tile setter, so water reaches the drain instead of spilling out the open side. The high desert in the north brings cold winters and big daily temperature swings that stress pipes and fittings. We pick the pan system to match.

    Curbless Shower Installation in Enterprise

    Curbless showers need the floor lowered in the shower area. On a wood floor, we drop the joists or use a recessed pan system, and on a slab, we cut and lower the concrete so the finished floor runs flat into the shower. Linear drains help a lot. We plan the slope with your contractor before demo.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Shower Installation in Enterprise

    Shower plumbing for a remodel, including valve, drain and trim, usually costs $1,200 to $3,500. Full walk-in showers with tile, glass and plumbing often total $8,000 to $20,000 or more, with most of that in tile and glass rather than plumbing. Prefab units cost less.

    Pressure-balance valves keep the hot and cold ratio steady when pressure changes. Thermostatic valves control the actual temperature and allow separate volume control, which works well for multi-head showers.

    A pressure-balancing valve watches pressure. When someone flushes a toilet and the cold side drops, the valve cuts hot flow to match, so the mix stays close to where you set it. A thermostatic valve watches temperature itself. It suits showers with several outlets.

    Small bathrooms are where shower choice matters most. Corner units and neo-angle stalls fit tight spaces, and a custom shower with a slider can work where a tub will not. We measure first.

    One to two days for a standard stall. Custom walk-ins with tile work or plumbing reroutes can take three to five days of plumbing and tile time, sometimes longer if the slab has to be cut. You get the schedule before we start.

    Plan on one to two days for a tub-shower replacement. Custom tiled walk-ins or showers that need significant plumbing reroutes can take three to five days, longer if demolition or structural work is involved. The timeline comes with the quote.

    Valves and fixtures carry manufacturer warranties, many lifetime for the original owner. Our workmanship is guaranteed too.

    Center drains and linear drains cover most showers. Round and square center drains work with a pan sloped from all four sides, while a linear drain lets the floor slope one way, which suits curbless showers and large-format tile. Tile size often decides.
