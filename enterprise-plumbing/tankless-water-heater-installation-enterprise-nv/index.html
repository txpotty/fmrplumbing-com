    Hard Water Protection for Tankless Units in Enterprise, NV

    Calcium scale is the most common cause of tankless heater failure. Water in much of Nevada is very hard, and scale buildup is one of the most common reasons water heaters and fixtures fail early. Scale coats the heat exchanger, reducing efficiency and triggering error codes. We install isolation valves so the unit can be flushed with a vinegar or descaling solution once a year. A scale inhibitor cartridge or whole-home softener provides added protection.
