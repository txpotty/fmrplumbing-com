    Tree Root Invasion Solutions in Enterprise

    Roots in a sewer line are not the tree's fault. The pipe was leaking first. Warm, wet, nutrient-rich air escaping from a loose joint draws roots toward the line, and once they get in they keep growing until the joint splits or the pipe cracks open. We cut them out, then talk about the long-term fix. Lining or a joint replacement keeps them from coming back.

    Emergency Sewer Repair in Enterprise

    Raw sewage on a basement floor is not a next-week problem. We answer at night and on weekends.     Traditional Sewer Line Replacement in Enterprise

    Some lines are too far gone for a liner. A fully collapsed section is one example. When the camera shows a collapse, a large offset or a belly deep enough to hold several inches of water, we open a trench, remove the old pipe and lay new Schedule 40 PVC on proper bedding at a quarter inch of fall per foot. Older homes often need this on at least one section. The dig is staged so the house is without a sewer for as little time as possible.

    Trenchless Sewer Line Repair in Enterprise, NV

    Trenchless repair means we fix the pipe without opening a trench along its full length. Usually two small pits. Pipe bursting pulls a new HDPE line through the old one and splits the old pipe outward, while cured-in-place lining pushes a resin-soaked felt tube into the old pipe and hardens it into a new wall. Both save driveways and mature trees. Not every line qualifies, and we will say so.

    Sewer Repair Cost Comparison in Enterprise, NV

    A spot repair is the cheapest fix when the rest of the line is sound. Lining costs more per foot. It avoids excavation, though, and on long runs under driveways or patios the savings on concrete and landscaping often outweigh the higher price of the liner itself. Full replacement is right for Orangeburg or a line broken in several places. We price all three so you can compare.
