    Root Intrusion Removal in Enterprise, NV

    Tree roots are a leading cause of sewer line blockages and damage, especially with the mature trees found in many Enterprise neighborhoods. Roots can infiltrate tiny cracks, expand and eventually crush or clog pipes. We use specialized augers and hydro-jetting to cut through and remove roots, followed by inspection to assess pipe integrity. For ongoing issues, we may recommend trenchless repair or pipe replacement to prevent future root problems.

    Cracked and Broken Pipe Repair in Enterprise

    Clay and cast iron sewer pipe crack from soil movement, heavy vehicles and simple age. A cracked section lets sewage leak into the soil and lets dirt fall into the pipe, which leads to recurring clogs. We excavate at the marked spot, cut out the damaged length and install new Schedule 40 PVC with shielded couplings rated for the transition. The repair is tested before we backfill so you know it will hold.

    Trenchless Sewer Line Repair in Enterprise, NV

    For many sewer line issues, especially those caused by tree roots or pipe corrosion, trenchless repair offers a less invasive option. This method, which includes pipe bursting or pipe lining, requires minimal excavation, saving your yard and landscaping from extensive disruption. We use specialized equipment to either pull a new pipe through the old one or insert a resin-coated liner that cures in place, creating a new, durable pipe inside the old one. It's an effective and popular choice for preserving property appearance in Enterprise.

    Sewer Line Hydro-Jetting in Enterprise

    Hydro-jetting is a powerful cleaning method that uses high-pressure water streams to clear stubborn blockages, grease buildup and even tree roots from sewer lines. It's far more effective than snaking for thorough cleaning and preventing recurring clogs. This service is particularly helpful for restaurants and commercial properties in Enterprise dealing with heavy grease accumulation, as well as homes with recurring drain issues.

    Sewer Pipe Relining in Enterprise, NV

    Sewer pipe relining is a trenchless repair method where a new, durable pipe is created within the existing damaged one. A flexible, epoxy-impregnated liner is inserted into the old pipe and inflated, curing in place to form a seamless, strong and corrosion-resistant new pipe. This method is effective for addressing cracks, minor breaks and root intrusion without major digging. It extends the life of your sewer system and is an great option for preserving landscapes in Enterprise.

    Tree Root Invasion Solutions in Enterprise

    Frequently Asked Questions About Sewer Repair in Enterprise

    A single spot repair typically runs $1,500 to $4,500 depending on depth and access. Trenchless lining usually costs $80 to $250 per foot, and full replacement of a typical residential line ranges from $5,000 to $15,000. We give you a written quote after the camera inspection.

    Trenchless sewer repair is a method of repairing or replacing sewer lines with minimal digging, often requiring only one or two access points. It involves techniques like pipe bursting, where a new pipe is pulled through the old one, or pipe lining, where a resin-impregnated liner is inserted and cured inside the existing pipe. This protects your landscaping and is less disruptive than traditional methods.

    To prevent future sewer line problems, avoid flushing anything other than toilet paper, dispose of grease properly and be mindful of what goes down your drains. Consider having yearly professional drain cleaning or sewer inspections, especially if you have older pipes or mature trees near your sewer line. Regular maintenance can identify small issues before they become major problems.

    The duration of a sewer repair depends heavily on the scope of work. Simple spot repairs or hydro-jetting might take a few hours. Trenchless repairs like pipe lining can often be completed in one to two days. Full sewer line replacements involving excavation can take anywhere from two to five days, depending on the length and complexity of the line.

    Sewer repair is complex and best left to licensed professionals due to the specialized equipment required, potential health hazards and strict local plumbing codes. Attempting DIY repairs can lead to further damage, costly mistakes and even safety risks. Always talk to a professional plumber.

    Many cities require a permit for sewer line replacement and for any work in the public right-of-way. Small repairs on private property may not, depending on local rules. We check with your city and pull the permit when it is needed.
