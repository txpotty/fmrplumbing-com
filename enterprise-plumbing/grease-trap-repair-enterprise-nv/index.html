    Grease Line Clog Repair in Enterprise, NV

    Lines downstream of the trap should stay clean if the trap is working. Grease past the outlet is a red flag.     Grease Trap Drain Line Repair in Enterprise

    Sometimes the trap is not the problem. Lines between the sinks and the trap clog first, because that is where hot water cools. We put a camera in from the nearest cleanout and record the run, looking for sags, offset joints, roots in clay sections downstream, or a belly where grease collects every week. Jetting a belly buys time, but only regrading the pipe fixes it.

    Baffle Repair and Replacement in Enterprise

    Inside a concrete gravity interceptor the baffle is usually a poured or precast wall with a crossover opening. A crumbling wall lets grease slip into the second compartment and out the outlet. We can rebuild the wall with form work and a sulfate-resistant mix or set a polyethylene baffle panel, depending on the tank. Either way, it needs a full pump-out first.

    Leaking Grease Trap Repair in Enterprise, NV

    A wet floor around an under-sink trap usually points to a failed lid gasket or a loose inlet coupling. Older steel bodies rust out along the bottom seams, too. We dry the area, fill the trap and watch where the water shows up. Sometimes it is a $15 gasket. If the seam itself has opened, patching rarely holds and we will tell you so before you pay for it.

    Grease Trap Leak Repair in Enterprise

    Plastic and fiberglass trap bodies do not rust, but they can crack from a dropped lid or a dishwasher dumping water hotter than the trap is rated for. Hairline cracks sometimes take a plastic weld. Anything through the floor of the tank or along a molded seam we treat as a replacement, because patches there fail under the weight of a full trap. You will see the crack yourself before we suggest a fix.

    Cost-Effective Grease Trap Solutions in Enterprise, NV

    Pricing depends on what failed, so we quote after the trap is pumped and we can see inside it. A gasket and a tee are a small bill.     Damaged Baffle and Lid Repair in Enterprise, NV

    Lids crack when someone sets a stockpot on them or a dolly rolls across a floor-mounted unit. A cracked lid lets odor out and lets mop water in. Baffle damage often shows up at the same visit.

We put both options in writing with a price on each.
