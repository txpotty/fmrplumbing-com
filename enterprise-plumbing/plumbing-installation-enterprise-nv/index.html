    Tankless Water Heater Installation in Enterprise, NV

    Navien and Rinnai tankless units hang on a wall and free up the floor space a tank used to take. Most gas units need upsized gas piping and a new vent run in stainless or PVC. Electric models need amperage. Sizing is based on flow in gallons per minute and how far the incoming water has to be heated. Water heaters in hot garages age faster, and outdoor pipes and irrigation lines take a beating from sun and heat. Every unit gets isolation valves so it can be flushed.

    Gas Line Installation in Enterprise, NV

    A new range or an outdoor kitchen all start with the same math: total BTU load and the length of the run. From there we size the pipe, choose between black iron and CSST, and dig the trench for any buried section. Every joint gets a soap test. Then the whole line holds pressure on a gauge before the inspector sees it.

    Whole-House Water Softener Installation in Enterprise

    Water in much of Nevada is very hard, and scale buildup is one of the most common reasons water heaters and fixtures fail early. Softener sizing comes down to hardness times daily gallons, which tells us how many grains the resin has to remove between regenerations. A unit sized too small regenerates constantly and wastes salt. We set the drain line with an air gap. Then we program the regeneration schedule to your water and run a test cycle.

    Water Heater Replacement in Enterprise

    Most tank heaters give out between 8 and 12 years, sooner with heavy sediment. Our stock covers 40 and 50 gallon tanks and 75 gallon units for larger families, and each install includes new connectors, a code relief line and a vent check. An expansion tank check is part of every job. Most installs finish in one visit.

    PEX Repiping in Enterprise

    Copper repipes cost more and take longer, which is why most whole-house repipes today use PEX-A or PEX-B tubing with brass or poly fittings. It runs quieter than copper, with less water hammer banging in the walls. Freeze damage is less likely too. Freezing weather is uncommon in the southern desert of Nevada, though occasional cold nights can still damage exposed outdoor pipes. We use fittings matched to the tubing brand.

    Sump Pump and Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Enterprise

    Basements and crawl spaces that take on water need a sump pump, and fixtures below the sewer line need an ejector. They are different pumps for different water, and one cannot stand in for the other. Sizing comes first. We match pump capacity to how fast the pit fills, put the pump on its own circuit and run the discharge with a check valve.

    PEX Pipe Installation and Repiping in Enterprise

    Pinhole leaks in copper keep coming back once the pipe walls start to thin. Repiping in PEX stops the cycle. The layout is a manifold with home runs for balanced pressure, or trunk and branch when the framing makes home runs a fight. Access holes are kept small and patched, or left square and clean for your drywall contractor.

    Water Softener Installation in Enterprise, NV

    Water in much of Nevada is very hard, and scale buildup is one of the most common reasons water heaters and fixtures fail early. Scale shows up first as white crust on faucet aerators and a rumbling water heater. We test hardness in grains per gallon at the kitchen tap, then size a salt-based softener or a salt-free conditioner to your household use. The bypass valve matters. It lets you shut the unit off for service without losing water to the house.
