    Brine Tank Cleaning and Inspection in Fernley, NV

    Over time, sludge and insoluble materials can build up in your brine tank, hindering the softening process.     Hardness Test and Water Quality Check in Fernley

    As part of our maintenance, we run a water hardness test to verify your water softener is effectively reducing mineral content to proper levels. This ensures you are receiving the soft water benefits you expect and allows us to fine-tune your system if necessary. We aim for consistent water quality in every Fernley home we serve.

    Control Valve Diagnostics and Repair in Fernley

    The control valve is the brain of your water softener, dictating regeneration cycles and water flow. Our maintenance service includes complete diagnostics of the control valve's programming and mechanical components. We check for proper timing, motor function and potential blockages. If issues are found, we can often repair or replace worn parts to restore full function, avoiding the need for a costly full system replacement in Fernley.

    Routine Water Softener Inspections in Fernley

    Regular inspections are the cornerstone of effective water softener maintenance. Our technicians will examine your entire system, including the brine tank, resin tank, control valve and all connecting pipes. We check for leaks, corrosion, proper salt levels and any signs of wear that could lead to future problems. This proactive approach helps extend the life of your unit and keeps performance consistent, especially since water hardness varies from place to place.

    Regeneration Settings and Hardness Testing in Fernley, NV

    Many softeners are set incorrectly from day one or never updated when household size changes. We test your raw water hardness and program the valve to match your actual use. Metered systems regenerate based on gallons used, which saves salt compared to time clock models. Correct settings keep water soft without squandering salt or water.

    Maintenance Versus New Softener Costs in Fernley, NV

    A maintenance visit typically costs far less than a new system, which often runs $1,200 to $3,500 installed.
