    Curbless Shower Installation in Fernley

    Curbless showers need the floor lowered in the shower area. On a wood floor, we drop the joists or use a recessed pan system, and on a slab, we cut and lower the concrete so the finished floor runs flat into the shower. A linear drain often works best. We plan the slope with your contractor before demo.

    Shower Stall Installation in Fernley

    A one-piece stall needs a level, fully supported base. We set fiberglass and acrylic bases in a bed of mortar or setting compound so the floor of the unit does not flex underfoot, since flex is what cracks a base within a couple of years. Then the drain goes in. Flow and temperature get tested after trim.

    Tiled Walk-In Shower Installation in Fernley

    Frameless glass walk-ins are popular, and the plumbing behind them has to be dead on. Valve, head, handheld and drain go in first, then the pan is sloped a quarter inch per foot to the drain. Testing follows. The valve gets a pressure test and the pan a 24-hour flood test before tile.

    Rain and Multi-Head Shower Installation in Fernley

    Rain heads pull up to 2.5 gallons per minute each. Add a handheld and a couple of body sprays and you can be running more water than a standard 40-gallon water heater can keep hot for a long shower. We check the heater. Thermostatic valves with volume controls run the outlets.
