    Repiping Services We Handle in Fernley, NV

    Galvanized Pipe Replacement for Older Homes in Fernley, NV

    Older homes may still have galvanized steel pipe that rusts from the inside out. Low pressure, brown water and leaks at threaded joints are the typical signs. Old galvanized gets removed or abandoned and replaced with PEX or copper, and most homes see better pressure and clearer water right away.

    PEX Pipe Replacement in Fernley, NV

    PEX tubing resists freezing damage, corrosion and scale better than most metal pipe. It is quieter too. Because PEX flexes, it absorbs some of the shock that causes water hammer, and long runs with few fittings mean fewer places for a leak to start inside a wall. Attic PEX gets kept away from flues and out of UV.

    Drain Line Repiping in Fernley

    Drain pipe wears out too, especially old cast iron that cracks and scales up inside. Repeat clogs, sewer smells and several slow drains at once often mean the drain pipe itself is failing. We camera the lines first. Failing sections are replaced with PVC or ABS sloped and vented to code, which drains faster and resists buildup much better than the pipe it replaces.

    Type L Copper Repiping in Fernley

    Copper has been plumbing homes for generations, and done right it lasts a long time. We use Type L, not the thinner Type M, and we support it with the right hangers so it does not rub or rattle in the walls. Lead-free solder only. Copper can pinhole in aggressive water, so we ask about your water and any past leaks before recommending it over PEX.

    Kitec and Polybutylene Replacement in Fernley

    Kitec, an orange or blue PEX-AL-PEX pipe with brass fittings, and gray polybutylene are both known for failures. Both still turn up in older homes. The pipe at the water heater usually tells us which one you have, and we replace the full system so the home is easier to insure and to sell.

    Urgent Repipes After Repeated Failures in Fernley, NV

    A widespread pipe failure sometimes forces a repipe sooner than you planned. We make the house safe and usable first, with temporary repairs that get you water, then schedule the full repipe as soon as the crew and permit allow. We move fast.     Commercial Repipe Services in Fernley

    Older commercial buildings in Fernley often have galvanized risers, copper that has pinholed or plastic pipe that no longer meets code.     Slab Leak Repiping in Fernley

    A slab leak shows up as a warm spot on the floor, a running meter or a wet patch of carpet with no visible cause. Most homes sit on concrete slabs, so leaks in buried supply lines often show up as damp areas on the floor or higher water bills. Rather than jackhammer to reach one leak, we run new lines through the attic and walls and leave the old under-slab pipe out of service. No jackhammer needed. It ends this leak and takes the rest of the slab plumbing out of the picture.
