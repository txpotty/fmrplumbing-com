    Gas Water Heater Replacement in Fernley, NV

    Gas water heaters reheat quicker than electric tanks, and in Fernley, NV homes they fail most often from a corroded tank, a bad gas valve or a burner clogged with rust flakes. Gas gets shut off and capped, the old tank drained and removed, and the new one set with a fresh connector, sediment trap and properly sloped vent. Then we leak test.

    Bradford White, Rheem and AO Smith in Fernley, NV

    We install Bradford White, Rheem and AO Smith on most tank jobs. Bradford White is trade-only and durable, Rheem offers range and parts, and AO Smith brings plenty of sizes, so the pick comes down to fit, warranty and stock. All three hold up.

    Attic Water Heater Replacement in Fernley

    Some homes from the 1980s and 1990s may still have polybutylene supply pipe, which is known for sudden failures and is usually replaced.     Upsizing Your Water Heater in Fernley

    Running out of hot water often means the family has outgrown it. Bigger is not always needed.

    Rheem and AO Smith Water Heater Replacement in Fernley, NV

    Rheem and AO Smith cover nearly every size and fuel, from 30-gallon short tanks to 80-gallon tall models. Both are solid, and the pick usually depends on what fits the space, the warranty length you want and what the supply house has in stock. We help you compare.

    Commercial Water Heater Replacement in Fernley

    Businesses in Fernley run their water heaters harder than homes do, so commercial replacements need more capacity and faster recovery.     Hybrid Water Heater Installation in Fernley, NV

    Hybrid heat pump water heaters pull heat from the air around them, using a fraction of the electricity a standard electric tank uses. They need space and airflow, often a room of about 700 cubic feet or a louvered door, plus a condensate drain, and they work best in a garage or basement that stays above freezing. They also cool the room.
