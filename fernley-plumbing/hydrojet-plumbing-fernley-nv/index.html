    Main Line Jetting in Fernley, NV

    The lateral from your house to the street handles every drain in the house, so buildup there causes backups everywhere at once. We jet it from the cleanout to the tap.     Grease Trap Line Cleaning in Fernley, NV

    Restaurant kitchen lines carry hot grease that hardens as it cools. Much of it coats the pipe before it ever reaches the interceptor. Short jetting passes keep loosened grease from plugging the line downstream. Quarterly works for most.

    Root Removal Jetting in Fernley

    Shade trees and shrubs send roots toward small cracks in clay and cast iron pipe. Root-cutting nozzles clear them. Roots come back through the same cracks, so the camera shows where a repair or liner would stop them.

    Storm and Yard Drain Jetting in Fernley, NV

    Yard drains, channel drains and downspout lines fill with sand, silt and leaves between storms. Snowmelt in the northern mountains can raise groundwater in spring, which can affect basements and low-lying properties. We jet yard lines from the outlet back and clean the basins before the wet season. Pop-up emitters get checked too.

    Hydrojetting vs Snaking Cost Comparison in Fernley, NV

    Snaking a main line typically costs $200 to $450, while residential hydrojetting usually runs $400 to $900 with a camera inspection. Jetting costs more up front. Fewer repeat visits often make jetting cheaper over a few years.

    Hydrojetting for Shops and Offices in Fernley, NV

    Retail and office buildings have long horizontal drain runs that collect buildup. Low-slope lines clog more. We jet those runs on a schedule that matches the building's use, and we can work after hours so tenants are not dealing with equipment in hallways or restrooms during the day.

    Kitchen Grease Line Jetting in Fernley

    Kitchen drains collect grease that cools and hardens along the pipe wall, especially in long horizontal runs under a slab. Cabling only cuts a narrow channel. Jetting with a spinning nozzle strips the grease from the full circumference so it flushes downstream instead of re-forming a plug at the next fitting.
