    Pipe Bursting Services in Fernley, NV

    Bursting replaces a pipe that is too far gone to line. A cone-shaped head breaks the old pipe. As a winch drags the head through, the old pipe fractures into the soil and new HDPE is pulled into the same path behind it. HDPE does not rust or leak at joints in Fernley, NV soil.

    Cured-in-Place Pipe (CIPP) Lining in Fernley

    A CIPP liner is a felt tube soaked in resin. The resin cures in place. We pull or invert the tube into the cleaned line, inflate it against the pipe wall and cure it with hot water, steam or UV light, which leaves a jointless pipe inside the old one. It fixes cracks, breaks and roots without digging up your Fernley yard.
