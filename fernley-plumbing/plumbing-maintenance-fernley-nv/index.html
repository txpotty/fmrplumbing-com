    Water Heater Maintenance in Fernley

    Regular water heater maintenance is key to preventing sediment buildup. Our service includes flushing the tank, inspecting the anode rod for corrosion, checking the temperature and pressure relief valve, and examining all connections and heating elements. This extends the life of your unit, boosts efficiency, and helps avoid sudden cold showers or leaks.
