    What We Fix Under the Sink in Fernley, NV

    Bathroom Sink Repair in Fernley, NV

    Bath sinks have small parts that wear. Pop-up linkage parts sit in a damp cabinet all day, and the pivot nut is the leak we find most often on bathroom sinks. We check pedestal and wall-hung sinks the same way. Vanity sinks just hide the leak better.

    Loose or Wobbly Sink Repair in Fernley

    A sink that moves when you lean on it is pulling on the drain. Undermount sinks hang on clips glued under the counter, and pedestal sinks hang on a bracket that can pull loose from drywall. The clips get reset and the rim resealed. Pedestals get blocking behind the wall when needed.

    Faucet Base Leak Repair in Fernley

    Water pooling around the faucet base is a different leak than a drip from the spout. It usually means worn O-rings on a swivel spout or a failed base gasket, and on some faucets it means the body has cracked and the whole faucet needs replacing. Each faucet style rebuilds its own way. We identify yours before ordering parts.

    Low Water Pressure at the Sink in Fernley, NV

    Unscrew the aerator before anything else. A clogged aerator screen causes most low flow at a single faucet, and if cleaning it does not help we check the cartridge and the angle stop for debris. Low pressure everywhere points to a different problem. We test the pressure regulator in that case.

    Overflow and Drain Flange Leak Repair in Fernley, NV

    Some leaks only show up when the bowl is full. That points to the overflow gasket or the putty under the drain flange, since neither one sees water during a quick hand wash but both get soaked when you fill the sink. The drain comes out and gets resealed. Marble and some granite get silicone, because putty can stain them.

    Kitchen Sink Repair in Fernley

    Kitchen sinks take the most abuse in the house. Basket strainers loosen and leak around the putty, pull-down sprayer hoses split inside the cabinet, and the disposal flange works loose from years of vibration. We fix the part that is wet. A cracked composite or porcelain bowl is a replacement, and we will say so.

    Garbage Disposal Repair in Fernley

    A disposal that hums but will not spin is usually jammed. We free the flywheel from underneath with a 1/4 inch hex key, press the red reset button, and check for a bent impeller or a broken grinding plate. Leaks are a different story. A cracked housing means a new disposal, usually an InSinkErator or a Waste King.

    Commercial Sink Repair in Fernley

    Restaurants and salons cannot wait a week for a sink. Most commercial clogs are grease. We also check the grease interceptor connection when the drain backs up.
