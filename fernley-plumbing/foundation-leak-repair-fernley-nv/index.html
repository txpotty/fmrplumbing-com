    Foundation Leak Detection in Fernley

    Accurate detection is the key step in foundation leak repair. We isolate hot and cold lines and pressure test them to verify a leak exists. Then we use acoustic listening equipment and electronic line tracing to find the exact spot beneath the foundation. Thermal imaging helps verify hot water leaks by showing warm spots on the floor.

    Post-Tension Slab Leak Repair in Fernley

    Many newer homes are built on post-tension slabs with steel cables under high tension. Cutting into these slabs takes care and scanning to avoid hitting a cable. We use ground-penetrating radar or other scanning methods before opening the floor. When the risk is too great, rerouting the plumbing is usually the safer option.

    Advanced Leak Detection Technology in Fernley, NV

    The ability to accurately locate a foundation leak is crucial for an effective repair. FMR Plumbing invests in advanced leak detection technology, including high-frequency listening devices and specialized camera inspections, to pinpoint leaks with precision. This technology lets us identify the exact location of the break, whether it's a small pinhole or a significant burst, minimizing invasive searching and repair costs for our Fernley, NV customers.

    Tunneling for Foundation Leaks in Fernley, NV

    In some cases, the best way to repair a foundation leak is by tunneling beneath the slab. This method allows our plumbers to reach the damaged pipe directly without breaking through your home's interior flooring. FMR Plumbing is experienced in safe and efficient tunneling, limiting disruption to your Fernley, NV home and delivering a complete repair of the damaged pipe section.

    Slab Leak Detection and Repair in Fernley

    Slab leaks are common in homes built on concrete foundations, often caused by shifting soil or corroded pipes underneath. Our technicians use low-impact methods like electronic leak detection and hydrostatic pressure testing to find these hidden leaks. Once located, we can perform targeted repairs, either by slab tunneling or direct access, minimizing disruption to your home. We work with many pipe materials, including copper, PEX, and PVC, providing a durable repair for your Fernley property.

    Direct Foundation Leak Repair in Fernley, NV

    When the leak is in an accessible spot and the rest of the pipe is in good condition, direct repair is often the most economical choice. We cut a small opening in the floor, excavate to the pipe and replace the damaged section. The soil is compacted and the concrete is patched to match. Flooring can then be restored by your flooring contractor.

    PEX and PVC Foundation Leak Repair in Fernley

    Newer homes often feature PEX or PVC plumbing, which, while durable, can still develop foundation leaks due to ground movement or poor installation. FMR Plumbing has the tools and expertise to repair leaks in these plastic piping systems. We use proper fusion and solvent welding techniques to ensure strong, leak-proof connections, restoring the integrity of your plumbing system beneath your Fernley foundation.

    Copper Pipe Leak Repair in Fernley, NV

    Many older homes in Fernley, NV have copper water lines beneath their foundations, which can corrode over time due to soil conditions or water chemistry. Water in much of Nevada is very hard, and scale buildup is one of the most common reasons water heaters and fixtures fail early. When these copper pipes develop pinhole leaks or cracks, they need specialized repair. Our technicians repair or replace sections of damaged copper piping, ensuring a durable seal that withstands pressure and ground movement and helps prevent future leaks.
