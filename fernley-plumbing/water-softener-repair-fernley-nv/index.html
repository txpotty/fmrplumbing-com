    Water Softener Repair vs. Replacement in Fernley, NV

    Age and parts availability decide whether a softener is worth repairing. FMR Plumbing gives honest advice on both counts. A sound tank with a rebuildable valve is worth saving, but an obsolete head without parts usually is not. We lay out both options.

    Resin Bed Replacement in Fernley

    Chlorine eats resin slowly, and after enough years the beads swell, break and stop exchanging the calcium and magnesium they are supposed to hold. Older systems on chlorinated city water see it most. FMR Plumbing pulls the old resin and fills the tank with new media. The softener works like new.

    Salt Bridge & Brine Tank Repair in Fernley

    Salt bridges form when a hard crust builds across the brine tank and the salt below it stops dissolving into the water. Salt sits there unused, and regeneration fails. We break the bridge, clean out the mushy salt and check the float and brine well. A cracked brine tank gets swapped for a new one.

    Salt Usage and Settings Repair in Fernley

    Too much salt or water use usually means settings that do not match your hardness or household. An old timer head regenerates by the calendar, used or not. After retesting hardness, we reprogram the valve or swap in a metered head that regenerates only when needed. Salt bills drop.

    Control Valve Troubleshooting and Repair in Fernley, NV

    Stuck pistons, worn seals and failed drive motors cause most control valve trouble, and the symptoms are hard water, constant regeneration or a leak at the head. The valve gets tested in place. Our plumbers carry seal kits and pistons for Fleck, Clack and Autotrol heads, which covers a big share of the softeners installed in homes around here.

    Emergency Water Softener Repair in Fernley

    A softener stuck in regeneration can run water to the drain for hours or flood a utility room. Urgent calls like that get priority. The crew in Fernley shuts the unit into bypass first, then finds out whether the valve, the timer or the drain line caused it. Usually a stuck piston or a dead motor is to blame, and both can be replaced the same day.

    Control Valve Rebuild in Fernley

    Service, backwash, brine and rinse are the four cycles the control valve directs every time the softener regenerates, usually in the middle of the night. Hard water and chlorine wear out the seals, spacers and pistons. Common signs are leaks at the head or constant drain flow. A matching rebuild kit fixes it.
