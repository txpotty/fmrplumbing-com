    Emergency Shower Repair in Fernley, NV

    A shower that will not shut off is an emergency. Shut the main, or use the shower's integral stops if your valve has them behind the trim or an access panel. Then call us. FMR Plumbing sends a plumber with cartridges on the truck, and we can often get your water back on within the hour.

    Shower Handle Leak Repair in Fernley

    Leaks at the handle or behind the trim plate are easy to miss. Water running down the inside of the trim gets behind the tile at the valve opening, and over a few months it soaks the drywall around the valve without ever showing on the shower side. The trim comes off first. Worn O-rings or a loose bonnet nut are the usual cause.

    Shower Temperature Swing Repair in Fernley

    Cold blasts when a toilet flushes mean the pressure-balance spool is stuck. The spool should shift to keep the mix steady when one side loses pressure, but scale can lock it in one spot. A new cartridge fixes it. Water that never gets hot enough may only need the limit stop turned.

    Tub and Shower Combo Repair in Fernley, NV

    Tub and shower combos share one valve, one drain and a diverter between them. Because everything is linked, a weak diverter starves the head and a leaky valve drips from the spout and the head alike. We check all of it. We also check the overflow gasket, a frequent hidden leak.

    Low Shower Pressure Repair in Fernley, NV

    Low pressure at the shower with good pressure at the sink narrows it down fast. That points to the head, the flow restrictor or the cartridge, not the main or the water heater, and it is usually something we can fix within an hour. The head comes off first. If every fixture is weak, we check the regulator and the main.

    Dripping Showerhead Repair in Fernley

    Drips from the head itself almost always come from the valve, not the head. If the head drips with the valve closed, water is slipping past the cartridge, and a new head will not change that. We fix the valve. Leaks at the arm threads are different, and those get new thread tape and a snug turn.

    Hot Water Pressure Problems in Fernley, NV

    Weak hot water with strong cold usually rules out the showerhead. The problem is on the hot side, either sediment in the water heater outlet, a partly closed valve at the heater or a cartridge whose hot port is packed with scale. We test both sides. Good hot flow at the sinks points us straight at the shower cartridge.
