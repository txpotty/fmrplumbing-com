    Point-of-Use Electric Tankless in Fernley

    Point-of-use electric tankless heaters under a sink are a different animal from whole-house units. They run on a single 20 or 30 amp circuit and work well for a far bathroom or an office sink. Swaps take an hour or two.

    Commercial Tankless Replacement in Fernley

    Cascade controls on a commercial rack decide which unit fires first, and after one unit is replaced they need rebalancing. Otherwise one unit does all the work. We also set a descaling schedule.

    Navien Venting and Install in Fernley

    Navien allows 2-inch or 3-inch PVC venting on its condensing units, with maximum lengths that drop as you add elbows. Our vent runs stay within spec. Go past them and the warranty suffers.

    Gas Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Fernley

    A gas tankless unit at full fire can pull 199,000 BTU, roughly five times what a 40 gallon tank burner uses. The gas line usually has to be 3/4-inch or larger. Pipe length and meter capacity get checked against the sizing tables, and we confirm pressure at full fire. An undersized line causes ignition faults.

    Exterior Mount Tankless Units in Fernley, NV

    Exterior-mount tankless units hang on an outside wall and vent straight out the front, so there is no vent pipe to run. Sun and weather wear on the covers and wiring. The northern part of the state sees regular hard freezes, so pipes in exterior walls and unheated spaces need protection. We check the freeze protection settings and the pipe cover kit on every outdoor swap. They are usually the easiest swap.

    Tankless vs. Tank Replacement Cost in Fernley, NV

    Tankless costs more up front than a tank, often twice as much installed. Across 20 years the gap narrows, because one tankless may outlast two tanks, but your usage decides the payback. You see both quotes.

    Navien Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Fernley, NV

    Navien NPE units are condensing, and the A models have a built-in recirculation pump and buffer tank that cut down on cold-water sandwiches. Their combi boilers handle both space heat and domestic water. We set up both.

    Rinnai units we pull are usually 12 to 18 years old, and the failure tends to be a leaking heat exchanger or a dead control board. A current Rinnai often mounts on the same bracket. Less drywall work that way. We match the vent type or convert to PVC on a condensing model.
