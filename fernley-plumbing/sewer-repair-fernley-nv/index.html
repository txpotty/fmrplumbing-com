    Sewer Vent Pipe Repair in Fernley, NV

    While often overlooked, the sewer vent pipe is crucial for proper drainage and preventing sewer gas buildup. Issues with vent pipes, such as blockages from animal nests or damage from strong winds, can lead to slow drains, gurgling noises and foul odors. FMR Plumbing diagnoses and repairs vent pipe problems, ensuring your entire drainage system works correctly and safely and protecting good indoor air quality.

    Root Intrusion Repair in Fernley, NV

    Tree and shrub roots go straight for the moisture and nutrients leaking from old sewer joints. Once inside, they grow into dense mats that catch paper and waste. We cut the roots with a mechanical cutter or hydro-jetter, then repair or seal the joint so they cannot come right back. For lines with many rooted joints, we will show you when lining or replacement makes more sense than ongoing cleaning.

    Cracked and Broken Pipe Repair in Fernley

    Clay and cast iron sewer pipe crack from soil movement, heavy vehicles and simple age. A cracked section lets sewage leak into the soil and lets dirt fall into the pipe, which leads to recurring clogs. We excavate at the marked spot, cut out the damaged length and install new Schedule 40 PVC with shielded couplings rated for the transition. The repair is tested before we backfill so you know it holds.

    Root Intrusion Removal in Fernley, NV

    Tree roots are a common cause of sewer line blockages and damage, especially with the mature trees found in many Fernley neighborhoods.     Trenchless Sewer Line Repair in Fernley, NV

    For many sewer line issues, especially those caused by tree roots or pipe corrosion, trenchless repair offers a less invasive option. This method, which includes pipe bursting or pipe lining, requires minimal excavation, saving your yard and landscaping from major disruption. We use specialized equipment to either pull a new pipe through the old one or insert a resin-coated liner that cures in place, creating a new, durable pipe inside the old one. It's an effective and popular choice for protecting property appearance in Fernley.
