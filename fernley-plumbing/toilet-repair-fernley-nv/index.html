    Pressure-Assist and Dual-Flush Repair in Fernley

    Pressure-assist toilets use a sealed vessel inside the china that compresses air with incoming water pressure, and the cartridge inside that vessel eventually wears out. You will not find most of these parts at a hardware store. We carry Flushmate cartridges and dual-flush seals for the common TOTO and American Standard models, and we check house pressure, since these units need steady line pressure to flush properly. Low line pressure gives you a weak, half-hearted flush.

    Clogged Toilet Solutions in Fernley, NV

    Paper towels, wipes, dental floss and toys cause most of the repeat clogs we clear, and none of them break down the way toilet paper does. The closet auger usually hooks them and pulls them out. If the toilet clogs again within a week, the problem is further down the line. Then we look at the branch drain and the main sewer, often with a camera.

    Leaking Toilet Repair in Fernley

    Water on the floor can come from four places: the wax ring, the tank-to-bowl gasket, the supply line or sweat running off a cold tank. Each one needs a different fix. We wipe it all dry and watch where the first drop forms, which takes about ten minutes and saves pulling a toilet that did not need it. A cracked bowl gets a replacement quote, not a patch.

    Sensor and Flushometer Repair in Fernley

    Sensor flush valves fail in their own ways, from dead batteries and dirty sensor eyes to solenoids that stick open and keep a bowl running. We test the sensor before we replace anything. Batteries are the cheap fix. Ghost flushing on a sensor valve usually means the eye needs adjusting or cleaning.

    Clogged Toilet Clearing in Fernley, NV

    Most clogs sit in the trapway, the curved channel cast into the porcelain, and a closet auger with a rubber-sleeved head clears them without scratching the bowl. Plungers miss some. If the auger comes back clean and the toilet still backs up, the blockage is further down the drain or in the main line. We run a cable from the cleanout or put a camera in.

    Leaky Toilet Repair in Fernley

    Food coloring in the tank is the cheapest test we run. If color shows up in the bowl within fifteen minutes without a flush, water is leaking past the flapper or the flush valve seat, and the toilet is quietly refilling itself all day and night. Most of the time a new flapper does it, unless the seat under it is pitted or cracked.

    Cracked Tank and Supply Line Repair in Fernley, NV

    Braided supply lines last years, but the plastic nut on the toilet end can crack and a bulge in the braid means the inner hose is about to burst. We check every supply line on every visit. Bulges get replaced on the spot. If you still have a vinyl or solid plastic line, we change it to braided stainless before it lets go.

    Toilet Tank and Bowl Crack Repair in Fernley

    Hairline cracks in a tank can sit for years and then split open when someone sets a heavy lid down or a bolt is tightened too far. Sealants and epoxy are a stopgap at best on a tank that holds water every day. We replace it. A matching replacement tank is often an option, and it can cost less than a whole new toilet.
