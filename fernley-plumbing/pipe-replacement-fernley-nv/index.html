    Repiping and Pipe Replacement Choices in Fernley, NV

    Copper Pipe Replacement for Older Homes in Fernley, NV

    Older homes sometimes have copper that is thin from decades of use. Pinholes start showing up. We replace it with new Type L copper or PEX, matching the layout or improving it where the old routing was awkward. Better flow.

    Corroded Pipe Replacement in Fernley, NV

    Corrosion shows up as green or white crust on copper, rust on steel, or pinholes that drip. Water chemistry plays a role. We replace corroded sections or the whole system depending on how widespread the damage is, including fittings near the heater. Look at the joints.

    Slab Leak Pipe Replacement in Fernley

    Rerouting is often better than digging. Jackhammering floors is a last resort. We run new pipe through the attic or walls to bypass the leaking line under the concrete, which leaves the floors alone and prevents the same line from failing again. Fast too.

    Drain Pipe Replacement in Fernley, NV

    Drain lines fail too. Old cast iron rusts through. We replace cracked cast iron, failing ABS or old galvanized drains with PVC, and making sure vents and fall are correct. Camera first.

    Galvanized Pipe Replacement in Fernley, NV

    Galvanized steel was common before the 1960s. Inside, rust builds once the coating fails. Over decades the opening shrinks, pressure drops and the water turns brown after sitting, while threaded joints corrode through first. Replacing it is the only fix.

    Whole-Home Re-Piping in Fernley

    A whole-home repipe replaces every supply line from the main shutoff to each fixture, including the water heater connections. Plan on a few days for a typical house. We run new trunk lines through the attic or crawlspace, branch down to fixtures through small wall openings, with service restored each evening when possible. Then patching.

    Copper Pipe Replacement in Fernley, NV

    Copper still has fans. It is durable and heat tolerant. We install Type L copper for most interior supply lines, sweat or press the joints, while strapping each line properly, so the new pipe does not rattle or sag. Costs more than PEX.

    PEX Pipe Replacement in Fernley

    PEX bends around corners and comes in long rolls, so a typical repipe needs fewer fittings than copper would on the same layout. The northern part of the state sees regular hard freezes, so pipes in exterior walls and crawlspaces need protection. PEX can stretch a little in a freeze instead of splitting. We use PEX-a with expansion fittings where possible, which give a full-bore connection and hold up well over time. Faster install.
